## Source

The consultant's reports from production: PLATFORM_ISSUE-2 and PLATFORM_ISSUE-10.

Overlaps part 2 of BUG-331 (parked pending DOC-82): that ticket proposed a new operation plus a host refusal of `proposed` headline decisions. This one takes the smaller route: `set_decision headline chosen`, gated on the client's words, and no new refusal.

## Problems

1. **A headline stated in conversation stays "offered" (PLATFORM_ISSUE-2).** When a client writes their own headline in the chat rather than choosing on the story board, the consultant can only record it on the `headline` decision as `proposed`: `set_decision` refuses `chosen`, and `offer_headlines` needs 2–3 options. The board keeps showing the earlier offers as an open choice, and the per-turn plan keeps saying "the headline is offered, not chosen", so the client is asked to choose again after deciding. In a test run the client objected to exactly that.
   - **Expected:** the consultant can record a headline the client gave word for word as their choice: `set_decision headline` with `state: chosen`, the text, and `client_said` quoting the client, as `signature_effect` already allows. Doing so retires the offered options on the board, and the plan no longer reports the headline as open. Without a `client_said` that contains the headline, `chosen` is still refused, so the consultant can't choose for the client.
2. **`write_positioning` refuses with a message that can't be acted on (PLATFORM_ISSUE-10).** Three calls with two `{customer, why}` picks were refused with the same PLAN_INVALID: *'a "why they pick you" line names its kind of customer'*. It names no entry and doesn't say whether it's a requirement or a prohibition. It succeeded only after blind retries.
   - **Diagnosis (corrected during implementation):** the `why` text was never the cause. The only code path that emits that message is the card check `customer` filled and at most `MAX_CUSTOMER_CHARS` (60). `write_positioning` already refused an empty customer with its own message, so the refused calls had a `customer` longer than 60 characters. The retries happened to shorten it.
   - **Expected:** the refusal names the offending entry (`picks[1].customer`), quotes it with its length against the 60-character limit, and says what to do: name just the kind of customer and put the rest in `picks[1].why`. The rule itself is unchanged.

## What changed

- `set_decision` (`tools/generate/src/cli/ai/plan-core.ts`): a `headline` decision is created on demand (title "The headline", messaging, concept), like `signature_effect`. `state: chosen` on it needs `value` (the headline) and a `client_said` that holds `value` word for word, ignoring case, punctuation and spacing (`saysWordForWord` in story-core). Otherwise it's refused with PLAN_INVALID, saying to give their message in `client_said` or to use `offer_headlines` to suggest one, and quoting the mismatch when there is one. When accepted:
  - the story board headline is settled as theirs (`clientHeadline` → `settleHeadline`, `yours: true`), and the open offers move to `passed`;
  - the decision is `chosen` with `answer.quote`, and a client entry is logged in the decision log;
  - the per-turn plan line reads `headline chosen (their words): "…"`.

  The board change isn't stamped as a client board action (`client_at`/`client_did`), because the consultant recorded it and already knows.
- `write_positioning` (`story-core.ts`): `picks[i]` missing a customer or why is refused naming `picks[i]`. A customer over 60 characters is refused as ``picks[i].customer is N characters ("…"); the most is 60. Name just the kind of customer, e.g. … and put the rest … in picks[i].why``. The card check's backstop message now states the requirement: "needs its kind of customer in at most 60 characters".
- `plan-surface.json` `set_decision`: the description, `state` and `client_said` now cover the `headline` case.
