**2. That's incorrect: `webui.ts`'s `mainCheckout()` follows the worktree's `.git` *file* → `commondir` → main checkout, anchoring `createRequire` at `/Users/martin/lagrangefoundry/1stcontact/package.json`. All three components resolve to `/Users/martin/lagrangefoundry/node_modules/@gendevlabs/*`. A naive upward walk from the worktree misses this — that's what the design measured.

The guard now reads the working tree for materialised files and `HEAD` for the rest. Here the absent 1921 are all `.md` under `.xgd/` (already excluded), so the scan set is unchanged — but it no longer depends on that coincidence.

The design's "a UAT that is green here is mis-written" doesn't apply to a coupling invariant.
