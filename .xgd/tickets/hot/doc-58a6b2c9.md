# Privacy Notice

This notice explains what personal information 1st Contact collects about you, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights. It applies to people who use 1st Contact: account holders and anyone they invite. If you are a visitor to a website built with 1st Contact, see section 9.

## 1. Who is responsible for your information

**Lagrange Foundry LLC** ("1st Contact", "we", "us") is the controller of the personal information described here.

- Address: c/o California Registered Agent Inc., 1401 21st Street, Suite 370, Sacramento, CA 95811, USA
- Email: [legal@1stcontact.io](mailto:legal@1stcontact.io), for every privacy question and request

We are a US company and do not have an establishment in the UK or the European Union. We have not appointed a representative there, because our processing is occasional and unlikely to put your rights at risk. We will review this as the Service grows.

## 2. What we collect and why

**Your account**
- What: your email address, your name if you give it, and the businesses or projects you belong to and your role in them.
- Why: to sign you in and to show you the right sites.
- Legal basis: performing our contract with you.

**Sign-in and security**
- What: the time of each sign-in, your IP address and the result of an automated anti-bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile).
- Why: to keep accounts secure and limit abuse, for example by rate-limiting sign-in attempts from one address.
- Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the Service secure.

**Your conversations with the AI assistants**
- What: everything you say to the assistants and everything they say back, kept so they can remember your project between sessions.
- Why: to provide the Service.
- Legal basis: performing our contract with you.

**Material you give us**
- What: photos, logos, documents and links, and anything about you or other people they contain. Material we fetch on your behalf, such as pages of your existing website, is included.
- Why: to design and build your site, including creating and editing images and video from your photos when you ask.
- Legal basis: performing our contract with you.

**Your site**
- What: its pages, words, images and settings, and the history of changes to it.
- Why: to build, preview and publish it, and so you can go back to an earlier version.
- Legal basis: performing our contract with you.

**How you use the builder**
- What: which features you use, when, and for how long.
- Why: to run the Service and, during the beta, to understand how it is used and improve it.
- Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing and improving the Service.

**Feedback and beta review**
- What: the feedback you send us and screenshots of your site preview taken when you send it. During the beta, everything in your account, which our team may review (see the [Beta Terms](/beta-terms)).
- Why: to fix problems and improve the Service.
- Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the Service.

**Correspondence**
- What: emails you send us.
- Why: to answer you.
- Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to you.

**We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.**

## 3. Cookies

We use one cookie: a session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary, so we do not ask for consent. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

## 4. Who we share it with

We use the following providers to run the Service. Each one receives only what it needs for its task:

| Provider | What it does for us | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) | Hosting, databases, file storage, search indexing, web page capture and screenshots, image processing, DNS, anti-bot checks | Everything stored in the Service |
| Anthropic, PBC (USA) | The AI assistants | Your conversations, and the material and site content the assistants work with |
| OpenAI, L.L.C. (USA) | Creating and editing images | Image instructions, and photos you give us when they are being edited |
| fal.ai (Features & Labels, Inc., USA), and the video model provider it uses for us: Kling (Kuaishou Technology, China) | Creating short videos from images | Video instructions and the images being animated |
| Resend, Inc. (USA) | Sending email, including sign-in links | Your email address and the message |
| GitHub, Inc. (USA) | Our development work-tracking | Beta feedback, screenshots and extracts from reviewed sessions |

We have chosen AI and media providers whose business terms say they do not use the data we send them to train their models. Some keep it for a limited time to detect abuse. Material for your public website, such as photos and the words on it, may go to these providers. **Private information collected through your site, such as enquirers' email addresses, is never sent to AI or media providers.**

We will also share information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or with a buyer if our business is sold. In the last case, we will tell you first.

## 5. Where your information is stored

We and all the providers above are based in the United States. Cloudflare serves the Service from data centres around the world. Where UK or EU law requires a safeguard for information leaving the UK or EU, our providers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK Extension) or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum). Images used to make videos may also be processed in China by the video model provider under fal's contract with it.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **While your account is open**, we keep your account details, conversations, material and site so the Service works and your project's history is not lost.
- **When you ask us to close your account**, we delete your personal information, your content and your sites within one month of your request. The only exception is information we must keep by law, and we tell you if that applies.
- **Feedback and beta review material** is kept only as long as we need it to fix the problem or make the improvement it relates to.
- Copies in our providers' backups and logs are deleted on their own schedules.

## 7. Your rights

If you are in the UK or the EU you have the right to:

- **access** the personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
- **correct** it if it is wrong;
- **delete** it;
- **restrict** how we use it, or **object** to our using it where we rely on legitimate interests;
- **take it with you** in a portable format (data portability).

To exercise any of these rights, email [legal@1stcontact.io](mailto:legal@1stcontact.io). We will answer within one month, may ask you to confirm your identity first, and do not charge for this.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority:
- in the UK, the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/);
- in Ireland, the [Data Protection Commission](https://www.dataprotection.ie/);
- elsewhere in the EU, the authority in the country where you live or work.

If you are in California, you also have the right to know what we collect, to delete it and to not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We do not sell or share personal information as California law defines those terms.

## 8. Security and age

We protect your information with encrypted connections, access controls and the security measures of the providers above. No service is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

The Service is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information about children.

## 9. If you are a visitor to a site built with 1st Contact

If you fill in a form on a website built with 1st Contact, the business or organisation that runs that site decides what happens to your information. It is the controller, and its own privacy notice applies. We store and deliver that information for it as its processor. Contact the site owner to exercise your rights. If you can't reach them, write to us and we will pass your request on.

## 10. Changes to this notice

When we change this notice, we publish a new dated version and ask you to read it the next time you sign in. Every earlier version stays available.
