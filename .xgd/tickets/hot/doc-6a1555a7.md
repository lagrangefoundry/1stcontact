## Your right of withdrawal (EU consumers)

If you are a consumer in the European Union, you have the right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 days, without giving any reason, starting from the day you (or someone you name) receive the goods. To withdraw, tell us clearly by email at {{contact_email}}. Send the goods back within 14 days of telling us. We will refund everything you paid, including the standard delivery cost, within 14 days of hearing from you, though we may wait until we have the goods back or proof that you sent them. You pay the direct cost of returning the goods. Some goods, such as personalised items or sealed items unsealed for hygiene reasons, cannot be withdrawn from. You also keep your legal guarantee rights for goods that are faulty.
