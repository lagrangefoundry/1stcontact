## Story
**As an** operator of a business on the platform, **I want** the invite, sign-in and lapse emails to be copy my business owns and can edit without a deploy, rendered so that a message with a missing link is refused rather than sent, and delivered through a provider only when the deployment holds a credential, **so that** the people I invite actually receive a working way in — and no test or development machine can ever mail a real person.

## Description
Covers two cooperating halves landed by REQ-196 and REQ-197:

**Sending (REQ-196).** One send operation takes `to`, `from`, `subject`, `body` and returns the provider's message id. A deployment holding the Resend credential sends through Resend; anything without it (the test suite, `wrangler dev`) gets a local adapter that records the message, delivers nothing, and returns a `local_`-prefixed id. The credential is the only switch. Both adapters refuse the same malformed messages. The From address (`no-reply@1stcontact.io`) is deployment configuration and its absence refuses. A send failure is reported to the caller, never swallowed. The credential is a secret pushed by a deploy hook, never committed, and is redacted from anything the router shows. `MAIL.md` is the operator's SPF/DKIM/DMARC runbook.

**Templates (REQ-197).** A `template` ticket type in the business's own ticket store (`template_key` ∈ invite|signin|lapsed, `subject`, optional `placeholders`, required non-empty body). The newest template for a key is the one in force; a business that has none is given a business-neutral default, once. Rendering substitutes `{{token}}`s in subject and body and refuses — naming template and token — when a declared token is absent from the body, a declared token has no/empty value, or any token is left unsubstituted.

**In scope:** the port, both adapters, configuration and secret handling, the template type, lookup + seeding, rendering and its refusals, the three seed templates.
**Out of scope:** the record of what was sent and the delivery/bounce webhook (REQ-198, plan item 7); any template editing UI; rate limiting; multipart HTML+text; per-recipient personalisation beyond token substitution.

## Reconciliation Decisions
- **Token declaration is tolerant of `{{name}}` and `name` spellings** (decided at reconciliation, 2026-11-04): intent is silent on how `placeholders` entries are written; the code accepts both because hand-authored tickets use both. Formalized in the render-substitution AC.
- **Subject tokens are substituted but the declaration is checked against the body only** (decided at reconciliation, 2026-11-04): stated in REQ-197's implementation decisions; formalized in the render-substitution and declared-token ACs.
- **Seed templates each declare exactly `{{cta_url}}`, and the signin and lapsed seeds carry the same button + pasteable-URL fallback as invite** (decided at reconciliation, 2026-11-04): REQ-197 requires the three-part structure only of `invite`; the code applies it to all three. Formalized in the seed-template AC.
- **Archiving every template for a key re-seeds the default on next lookup** (decided at reconciliation, 2026-11-04): REQ-197 states this as the accepted consequence; formalized in the lookup/seeding AC.
- **The deploy hook warns and exits 0 (rather than aborting) when the mail key exists nowhere** (decided at reconciliation, 2026-11-04): REQ-196's landed notes make this deliberate while nothing sends; formalized in the deploy-secret AC.
- **CODE-ISSUE — body format contradiction between REQ-196's port and REQ-197's templates.** REQ-197 (Decisions taken while implementing): *"The body is HTML, and it is one part … the invite is required to carry its call to action as a button and a button does not exist in plain text."* REQ-196 anticipated exactly this: *"When REQ-197 writes one that wants markup it is one field here and one line in the adapter."* That change never landed. The Resend adapter still submits the body as the provider's plain-text field, so a rendered invite would arrive showing raw `<a href=…>` markup and no button. Per the chain of authority the later intent (REQ-197) governs: the AC "Provider receives the message body as HTML" captures intent, and the fix is the one-field/one-line adapter change REQ-196 itself described.
