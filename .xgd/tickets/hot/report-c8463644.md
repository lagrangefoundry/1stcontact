## Notes for the Editor

- **Nothing to fix at this level.** Findings 2–4 are ledger entries with
  resolution "none"; finding 1 is a warning whose action belongs to the uat
  cycle. No `xgd ticket update` is called for on any of the 14 ACs.
- **Do not set `uat_coverage` on AC-1411 from an ac-level fix.** That field is
  owned by check/fix_uat_coverage. Setting it here would record coverage that
  does not exist and would mask finding 1 from the cycle that can actually
  close it. REPORT-3835 made the same call deliberately and was right to.
- **AC-1411's `status: pending` is not itself drift.** 26 of the store's 662
  acceptance criteria are `pending`, most of them recent and most carrying
  `uat_coverage: pass`; the status is a creation-lifecycle state, not an
  exclusion from the matrix. What singles AC-1411 out is the missing
  `uat_coverage` field, which is finding 1.
- **The AC set absorbed BUNDLE-19 and BUNDLE-20 without structural change, and
  that is by design** — the criteria are written over the declaration's
  discipline, not over its operation count. A future intent that adds
  operations should be expected to move `updated_by` and nothing else, exactly
  as STORY-105's closing Technical Context bullet now states. An ac-level check
  that finds itself wanting to add an AC per new operation has misread that
  bullet.
