# Beta Terms

You have been invited to try 1st Contact before it is generally available. These Beta Terms apply for as long as you take part in the beta, together with our [Terms of Service](/terms) and [Privacy Notice](/privacy). Where these Beta Terms and the Terms of Service disagree, these Beta Terms win.

## 1. It's free

Taking part in the beta costs nothing. We won't ask for payment details. If we ever want to start charging, we will tell you in advance, and nothing will be charged unless you agree to a paid plan.

## 2. It's unfinished

The beta is a work in progress. That means:

- **Things will break.** Features may be missing, behave unexpectedly, or change from one day to the next.
- **Your work could be lost.** We try hard to prevent this, but please keep your own copies of anything important you give us, such as photos and logos.
- **There is no guaranteed availability.** The Service may be slow or unavailable at times, including while we make changes.
- **We may reset or move things.** If we need to make a change that affects your site or your work, we'll try to tell you first.

Because of this, please think carefully before relying on a site built in the beta as your only presence on the web.

## 3. We can see everything in your account

**Taking part in the beta means agreeing that 1st Contact may access, review and use everything in your account to improve the product.** That includes your conversations with the assistants, the material you give us, your sites and their history, the feedback you send, screenshots of your site preview taken when you send feedback, and the timing of your activity. These reviews are carried out by the 1st Contact team, including with the help of AI tools.

There is one limit. Information your site's **visitors** give you, such as enquiries and their contact details, belongs to you and them, not to us. By joining the beta you instruct us to look at it only when we need to investigate or fix a problem with your site, and we never use it for anything else.

Material from these reviews may be copied into our development work-tracking system (hosted by GitHub) so we can fix what went wrong. We tell you this once, here, and do not ask each time. The [Privacy Notice](/privacy) explains how long we keep it.

## 4. Your feedback

We'd love to hear what works and what doesn't. If you send us ideas or suggestions, you allow us to use them to improve 1st Contact, without owing you anything for them. Your own content stays yours, as the [Terms of Service](/terms) explain.

## 5. Please keep it to yourself

The beta isn't public yet. Please don't share sign-in links, or post screenshots or descriptions of unreleased features publicly, without asking us first. The sites you publish are yours to show to anyone.

## 6. When the beta ends

The beta may end, or your place in it may end, at any time. When that happens:

- we will give you **at least 30 days' notice** by email, unless we have to stop sooner for legal or security reasons;
- you can ask us for a copy of your content during that time;
- we will tell you what happens next for your account and your published site. It may move to a generally available version of 1st Contact under its own terms, which you can choose to accept or not, or it may be closed and deleted as our Privacy Notice describes.

You can leave the beta at any time by emailing [legal@1stcontact.io](mailto:legal@1stcontact.io).

## 7. Contact

Questions, problems and feedback: use the feedback button in the builder, or email [legal@1stcontact.io](mailto:legal@1stcontact.io).
