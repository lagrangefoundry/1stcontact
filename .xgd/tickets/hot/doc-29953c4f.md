## If you join our mailing list

If you sign up to our mailing list, we use your email address to send you the updates you asked for. We do this because you agreed to it, and you can change your mind at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us. We keep your address until you unsubscribe. Buying from us or contacting us never adds you to the mailing list.
