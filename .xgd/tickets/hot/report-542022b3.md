- **AC-1740:** an invite writes the person, a registered business, an owner membership and an active grant, all read back from the database.
- **AC-1748:** admission returns a fixed, repeatable list of businesses, with no single top-level account or grant.
- **AC-1750:** a grant's end date is checked both ways; the person is refused only when every business has lapsed.
- **AC-1751:** a grant starting in the future makes its business unselectable rather than refusing the person.
- **AC-1752:** a revoked grant makes its business unselectable even when its dates are still valid.
- **AC-1753:** a withdrawn, inactive or expired membership excludes its business and leaves the grant untouched.
- **AC-2075:** a person with several businesses sees each with its own name and grant.
- **AC-2076:** a business whose grant has lapsed, not started or been revoked stays listed as unselectable.
- **AC-2077:** a business whose membership is withdrawn or expired is left out of the list entirely.
- **AC-2078:** an invite and an extra business produce the same records; an empty owner or name is refused before anything is written.
- **AC-2079:** a person with no business is refused, re-inviting them creates one, and a second re-invite writes nothing.
