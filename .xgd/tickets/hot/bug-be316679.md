## What happened
A delegated builder session wrote 8 changes to the draft (change count 67→75): two new sections on page `home`, text edits, a fill/border change, and site title/SEO meta. The session then ended `aborted` (see the companion bug about silent builder results). A later small builder pass (75→77) completed normally.

The draft holds all of these changes. `describe_page` and a `draft` screenshot taken by the consultant both show them. However, the **client's preview window did not show them**. The tester reports the preview never updated.

Several unrelated dev sessions crashed at the same moment, so the preview may simply have lost its update channel at that point. Even so, the preview should not stay stale silently.

## Expected
The client's preview reflects the draft's current change count, whichever session wrote the change. If the preview misses notifications (worker abort, dropped socket), it should resync on reconnect or poll the change count. It could also show a visible "preview out of date — refresh" state, rather than presenting a stale page as current.

## Investigate
- Are preview refresh notifications sent per writing session, and lost when that session aborts before it flushes or commits a notification?
- Does the preview resubscribe and compare change counts after a connection drop?
- Did the later successful pass (75→77) refresh the preview? If not, the stale state is sticky, not just a missed event.

## Reproduce
Delegate a builder write, kill or abort the worker after its writes but before it reports, then check whether the client preview shows the new change count.
