## Symptom

On prod, 2026-11-04 18:19Z, a consultant turn was shown to the client as "stopped" about 5 minutes in. Its Workflow carried on for 24 more minutes and **completed** (9 consultant rounds and 5 builder passes; outcome ok; wall 1,759 s; CPU 165.6 s). The client saw none of the work after 18:24:29, and the turn reads as lost. The site was a small one with no shop.

Workers Logs for the prompt request at 18:24:29: `job_lost … "its runner stopped writing"`. turn_log `alive_at` stopped at 18:24:24.

## Root cause

`followJob` → `closeDeadJob` in `tools/generate/src/cli/ai/host-core.ts` declares a job dead when both of these hold:
- no job record for `JOB_SILENT_MS` (60 s);
- no live claim (`LEASE_TIMEOUT_MS`, 30 s).

It then **writes** a lost `JOB_END`, releases the turn and closes the stream. Its comment assumes a round holds the session claim through a whole Delegate. Since REQ-627, a builder pass runs as its own step and does not, so a long tool call inside a builder pass looks like death. The turn heartbeat (BUG-288) beats only inside the round step.

## Fix

The rule (DOC-1 item 33, DOC-86 §4): **only the runner declares a job's liveness or ending.**
- The follow stream and the panel never write a job ending from their own inference.
- Every step a job runs, round or builder pass, records signs of life the follower can read. The heartbeat covers builder steps too.
- Prolonged silence is shown as "no news from the job for N min" with the job still running. It ends only when the runner records an ending, or when the Workflow instance itself reports errored or terminated (ask the binding, don't guess).
- Reloading the builder re-attaches to a running job and streams on.

## Test plan

UATs named `test_UAT_FC_<id>_*`, all using the in-process runner with a scripted builder pass that stays silent for longer than the old thresholds:
- The stream does not close and no lost ending is written.
- The job completes and the client receives the final reply.
- A job whose runner really died (the step is killed and its retries are exhausted) still ends lost, recorded by the runner.
- The panel shows "no news" during the silence, and returns to normal when records resume.
