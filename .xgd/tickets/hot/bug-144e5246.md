## Diagnosis (free-coding session, 2026-10-03)

**Issue 1 is a real model gap.** The renderer emits every `container` as `display: flex`/`grid` (no margin collapse inside one), but a `box` with children is a plain block `div`, and the document root is a `box` under `body` (also a block). In CSS a block box with no top padding, no top border and no BFC passes its first in-flow child's top margin up through its own top edge. The model never did this. It placed `section-bg-0` 84px into a root sitting at y 0, while the browser places the root at y 84. So `.l1-0` moves, and every absolutely-placed child of the root moves with it, because the root (`position: relative`, the renderer's rule for every geometry-less box/container) is their containing block.

**Issue 2 is not a model divergence.** Every `escape` in `gate.json` comes from the **content-robustness** probe (`CONTENT_SCALE = 2.5`). The boxes it quotes are the model's boxes with every text run grown 2.5×. At rest the model puts `0.6.0.0.0` (About Me) at **y 1088**, the value this ticket names as correct once REQ-371 issue 3 restores the pill height. Under 2.5× content it is y 1628.75, which is the number quoted. The real defect is legibility. That `gate.json` predates BUG-179 item 7, so its findings carry no `probe`. Even now the finding *sentence* ("at 1280px×768px: …643px below its bottom edge"), which is what the verdict's diagnosis quotes, does not say the copy was grown. So the perturbed magnitude reads as what the browser draws.

## Fix

1. **Model margin collapse** in `evaluateLayout` (`tools/generate/src/l1/probes.ts`). The model follows the renderer's formatting contexts:
   - The root and every child of a `box` sit in a **block** context. Children of a `container` sit in a flex/grid context, where a margin never collapses.
   - A `box` with children, placed in a block context, collapses its first in-flow (non-pinned, non-hidden) child's top margin through its top edge. This applies only when it is not pinned (absolute is a BFC), has no `clip` (`overflow: hidden` is a BFC), has no top padding and no top border, and is not retagged as a link/button. The rule recurses down a chain of such boxes.
   - The collapsed margin is combined the CSS way: the largest positive plus the most negative. It is applied once, at the outermost box of the chain. The inner nodes sit at their parent's content top, and the absorbed margin is not counted again in any advance or content height.
   - **Every `box`/`container` donates its padding-box corner as the containing block** for its absolute descendants, as the renderer's `position: relative`/`absolute` makes it. Before this, only pinned or leading-offset nodes did, so the root could never carry its pinned children with it when it moved.
   - Consequence on this page: sampleFidelity reports the ~84px residual on `backdrop-0..2` / `box-0..1` at ≥1024 that Chromium paints. Once the renderer stops collapsing the margin (REQ-371 issue 2), it reports 0.
2. **Say the perturbation in the finding sentence.** `layoutCollisions` (`tools/generate/src/cli/gate-core.ts`) prefixes a content-robustness finding with the content scale ("at 1280px×768px, copy grown 2.5×: …"). An off-sample finding is already distinguishable by its width. To make this possible, `EnvelopeReport` carries the `contentScale` it was evaluated under when that is not 1.

**Not done: a browser self-check inside `l1-gate`.** `l1-gate` is analytic by design. With issue 2 traced to the perturbation, the only measured model/browser divergence is issue 1, which is fixed in the model. A Chromium launch inside the gate would be a new runtime dependency for that command, and it is out of this ticket's scope.

## What landed

Commit `68dc6f71d5` (`tools/generate/src/l1/probes.ts`, `tools/generate/src/cli/gate-core.ts`, `tests/test_UAT_FC_BUG-188_layout_model_collapses_margins.test.ts`). It implements the Fix section above as written.

- **Margin collapse.** `evaluateLayout` lays every node out in a block or a flex/grid context, matching the renderer's CSS. The root and the children of a `box` are block. The children of a `container` are flex/grid.
  - A box in a block context passes its first in-flow child's top margin through its own top edge. It does not when it has top padding, a top border, an absolute placement, `clip`, or a `link`/`action` retag.
  - Along a chain of such boxes the margins combine as the largest positive plus the most negative. The result is applied once, at the outermost node. Inner nodes sit at their parent's content top, and no advance counts the margin twice.
  - Every `box`/`container` now donates its padding-box corner as the containing block for its absolute descendants. Before, only pinned or leading-offset nodes did.
- **Grown-copy findings say so.** `EnvelopeReport.contentScale` is set when an envelope probe grew the content. `layoutCollisions` (and therefore every `gate.json` finding sentence and the verdict's diagnosis) prefixes such a finding with "at W×H, copy grown 2.5×: …". `escapesOnly` carries the field through.
