## If you contact us

If you send us a message, for example through a form on this website, by email or by phone, we keep what you give us, such as your name, email address, phone number and your message. We use it to reply to you and to deal with your enquiry. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to deal with you, and then delete them.
