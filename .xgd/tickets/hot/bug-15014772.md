## Why

The operator reviewed First-Draft-Test Dina's Diner 7 (2026-10-27) and raised three issues. All three come from the shared framework or its defaults, so they will recur on every site.

## 1. A flash between an intro clip and its loop

**Symptom:** the hero plays `hero-pepper-reveal.mp4` once (`playback: once`), then hands over to `loopSrc: hero-steam-loop.mp4`. A blank or out-of-place frame shows at the join.

**Cause:** REQ-470's handover in the renderer's clip script (`packages/framework/src/l1/render.ts`) swaps the same `<video>` element's `src` at `ended`. That swap empties the element while the loop loads, so the poster shows. The poster is the intro's *first* frame (`hero-steak-eggs-no-pepper.png`, the plate before the pepper), so the join jumps backwards and then forwards.

**Fix:** the intro holds its last frame until the loop can play, and the cut-over is seamless:
- the loop is preloaded in a second element layered under the intro, and is revealed and played at the intro's `ended` (or once the loop can play, if later);
- the intro element is then removed or hidden;
- reduced motion, the pause button (REQ-471), "a repeat view goes straight to the loop", and a browser that runs no script all keep their current behaviour.

## 2. Text hard against the edges of filled boxes

**Symptom:** the hero's text box (`hero-text`, `surfaceFill: text @ 0.45`) and other coloured boxes, such as "Steak and eggs", have no inner padding, so their text touches the box edges.

**Cause:** L1 allows a filled container with no padding, and nothing supplies a default or flags it.

**Fix:**
- **When rendered, a container with a visible fill or border and no padding of its own gets the design's standard inner padding.** That's a space-scale step, larger on a computer than a phone. Settle the step and the widths here.
- **An explicit padding, including zero, is kept,** for example for a full-bleed photo.
- **The layout check flags text sitting against the edge of a filled or bordered box,** naming the node, so the builder sees and fixes it.

## 3. A detailed emblem logo starts too small

**Symptom:** Dina's logo, a detailed retro emblem, was 72px tall in the header. The operator keeps asking for it bigger. Each First-Draft-Test site is a fresh copy, so a size set on an earlier site doesn't carry over, and the starting size is chosen afresh each time.

**Fix:** the guidance the consultant writes `brand.logo` from says a detailed emblem is the header's centrepiece, and gives a starting range: about 96–120px visible height on a computer and 64–72px on a phone, set as keyframes. A plain wordmark starts smaller, sized by width. This goes in:
- the `set_design` / `brand` tool text (`plan-surface.json`);
- the design guide (DOC-75);
- the starting size the host seeds from `read_design`'s measurement, if there is one.

The header and its links stay in proportion (BUG-286).

## What landed

**1. Seamless handover** (`L1_VIDEO_SCRIPT`, `packages/framework/src/l1/render.ts`). When the intro starts, the runtime stages the loop in a copy of the clip, inserted just before it. The copy has the same classes and the loop as its source, `preload=auto`, and is absolutely placed over the intro, `visibility: hidden` (not `opacity`, which an entrance might animate), inert, `aria-hidden` and without the id. At the intro's `ended`, the intro holds its last frame until the copy reaches `canplay` (readyState ≥ 3). In one step the copy then takes the intro's id, label and place in the flow, is played (unless the pause button has paused it), and the intro element is removed. The rest timer, the pause button's pointer handling and its ResizeObserver move to the copy. Unchanged: a repeat view (`once_per_session`) still swaps the source before anything plays, and reduced motion, no-script and the edit render behave as before. `run()` takes a static copy of the clip list, so the staged copy is never armed itself.

**2. Inner padding** (`packages/site-schema/src/l1/design.ts`). The new resolve step `applyInnerPadding` runs after components. A box or container below the document root that paints a visible box (`surfaceFill` that isn't alpha 0, `surfaceGradient`, or a border wider than 0), with no `padding`/`responsivePadding` and no captured `geometry`, takes the space step `L1_INNER_PADDING_STEP = 'md'` on every side. That step is 16px on a phone and 24px on a computer in the seeded scale, written as a per-width track when the step has one. Explicit padding, zero included, is kept. Nothing is added when the scale has no `md`. Text runs and buttons are left to their components. The new layout check `box_edge` (kind `tight_box`, `layout-check.json` `box_edge.minPx: 6`) reports a filled or bordered box, other than a button or a control, whose own words come within 6px of its inner edge. It names the box by its id (`'hero-text'`), or by the text it holds when it has no id, and says to pad it by a space step.

**3. Emblem size** (`tools/generate/src/cli/design-seed.ts`). `EMBLEM_START = { phone: 72, desktop: 120 }` (the top of the range) replaces `EMBLEM_SCALE`. A detailed emblem is now always sized by height, including a wide one with three or more lines of artwork. A plain mark still starts from `LOGO_START`, and a wordmark is still sized by width. The `set_design` `brand` text (`plan-surface.json`) and DOC-75 both state the 96–120px / 64–72px starting range. Header links still step up to `lead` beside a logo 72px or taller (BUG-286).
