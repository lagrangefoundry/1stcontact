## What changes

A published shop never depends on control-app or agent compute to serve a visitor (DOC-1 item 34), and editing inventory does not make a request's work grow with the catalog.

1. **Shop photos become publish-time static renditions.**
   - Today every `/shop-photos/<slug>/<n>` request goes from public-site to control-app's `ASSET_GATE.shopPhoto`, then to D1 `inventory_items`, then to a Library read. It returns the **full-size original** with `max-age=60`, so a 24-card grid makes 24 such calls.
   - Instead, live items' photos are rendered into the revision's output at publish or rebuild time, like every other site image: content-named renditions at the delivery ladder's widths, with `srcset`/`sizes`, served immutably from R2 by public-site.
   - The live `shopPhoto` gate path is deleted.
2. **Item changes are rebuilt as a background job.**
   - Today `catalogItemChanged` re-renders every page of every published site of the business inside the Save, delete or import request.
   - Instead, a change marks the business's sites as needing a catalog rebuild. A debounced job (a Workflow, running its render in a container under the cutover's runtime) rebuilds once for a burst of edits.
   - The Save returns immediately, and the Shop tab shows "Updating your site…" until the rebuild lands.
3. **Checkout re-checks the item on the server:** live, available, and at the current price, at the moment of Buy. A stale static page can never sell an item that is unavailable or at an old price. If this check already exists, record where and add the UAT.

## Implementation status (2026-11-04)

### Item 1 — done

- **Where a published photo lives.** Each live item's photo is a static file under its site's own root in R2: `sites/<site>/catalog-photos/<sha16><ext>`.
  - The name is the first 16 hex characters of the photo's SHA-256, plus its extension.
  - Its delivery renditions sit beside it (`<sha16>-<w><ext>`, plus WebP for JPEG and PNG).
- **How the page names it.** The published page names the photo `assets/s/<sha16><ext>`, with `srcset`.
  - The card and product-page `<img>` also get a `<picture>` `<source>` for WebP.
  - `sizes` follows the site's own rule for every image: it is emitted where the card's image has a pinned width, and omitted otherwise.
- **How public-site serves it.**
  - public-site resolves `assets/s/<file>` straight to that R2 key.
  - It serves the file with the immutable cache (`max-age=31536000, immutable`).
  - It never reaches control-app to do so.
- **Prepared once per site and Library material.**
  - The site's index (`sites/<site>/catalog-photos.json`, keyed by material uid) records what each material became.
  - A publish or rebuild reads the index once and prepares only materials it lacks.
  - A material is read from the Library at most once per site.
  - A rebuild with nothing new reads nothing from the Library and renders no image.
- **Unreadable photos.** A photo whose material can't be read is left out of its item. Publishing and rebuilding continue.
- **Deleted.** The `/shop-photos/<slug>/<n>` path on the published site is gone and answers the ordinary 404. So are `ASSET_GATE.shopPhoto`, its RPC in `worker.ts`, and `shopPhoto()` in control-app's `gate.ts`.
- **Builder preview is unchanged.** It still serves `shop-photos/<slug>/<n>` out of the Library, because it renders live, unpublished inventory.

#### Design decisions

- **Per site, not per revision.**
  - A photo belongs to inventory, not to the site's design.
  - So a Publish never copies photos, and a rebuild never needs to know which revision is live.
  - Erasure still reaches the files: they sit under `sites/<site>/`.
- **Keyed by material uid in the index, not by a lookup per item.** `attachments({uid})` scans all of the business's attachment records (BUG-341). Resolving every photo on every rebuild would make each Save grow with the Library.
- **How the renderer finds a photo's ladder.** `deliveryAssetName` now admits exactly one directory, `assets/s/`, keyed `s/<file>`. No site asset name can contain `/`, so the two key spaces cannot collide.
  - The catalog's photo ladders travel as `SiteCatalog.delivery`.
  - They are merged into the manifest the render uses, but never stored with the revision's own manifest.
  - The catalog component passes them to its card and product fragments.
- **Lazy preparation on rebuild.** A rebuild asks for its catalog only once it has found a live revision, so a never-published site prepares no photos.
- **Interim cost.** Until item 2 lands, preparing photos runs inside the Save or publish request. That covers a new item photo, or every photo on the first rebuild of an existing catalog. Item 2 moves this work into the background job's container.
