## Symptom

First-Draft-Test Dina's Diner 3 (site `site_005ef0d9f996bedab94f48d1147a05e7`),
2026-10-25 21:07 UTC. The client hearted the direction card. That closed "Looking
at other sites" and cued the consultant, which said:

> Got it. You've signed off the direction: … Next I'm checking the first draft of
> the home page and the catering page against that direction, which takes about a
> minute. Then I'll show you both.

Then nothing happened. The site has **no pages**, no builder ran, and no further
turn started. The operator: "and then things seemed to die".

## What happened (junction events, turn `…fbd7ac`)

- 21:07:44, 21:07:47 and 21:07:51: three rounds of `list_pages` + `list_changes`,
  each refused by BUG-278's speak-first gate ("Not run yet … First write one or two
  sentences … Then carry on, starting with this call again"). The model ignored
  the refusal twice and reissued the same calls.
- 21:07:59: round 4 is **text only** (the message above). The turn ends `complete`.

There are two faults.

### 1. The speak-first gate turns "speak, then carry on" into "speak, then stop"

BUG-278 refuses every tool on a cue turn until the turn has spoken. When the model
complies, it does so in a round of its own, and a text-only round ends the turn.
Nothing then re-issues the refused call. REQ-428's continuation doesn't help,
because it only fires for promises recorded in the plan, and narration like "Next
I'm checking…" isn't one. So the gate that exists to make the consultant *say*
what it's about to do also *stops it doing it*.

### 2. The REQ-450 copy carries due checks from a build it removed

`first_draft_copy` cut the source at the handover (2026-10-24T01:24:18) and the
copy has no pages. But the plan still shows build milestones that happened in the
source before the cut:
- `quality_bar_met` and `weakest_point` are `due: first_pass_complete @ 01:18:43`;
- `layout_happy` and `motion_offer` are `due: revision_round_finished @ 01:23:35`;
- task `t1` (Catering page) is `told: "Straight after the first draft of the home
  page."`

The consultant is told the first pass happened, so it plans to *check* a home page
and a catering page that don't exist, instead of building them. That's why it said
"checking … about a minute", not "building … about 15 minutes". (BUG-280 dropped
post-cut tasks; it didn't rewind milestones.)

## Fix

1. **The host continues a turn the gate held.** When a cue turn had a call
   refused by the speak-first gate, and the round that finally speaks ends with no
   tool call, the host continues the turn with a short hidden cue: "You've told
   your client what's next; now do it, starting with the call that was held."
   This is the same continuation mechanism REQ-428 and BUG-241 use, so the
   client's bubble carries on. It happens once per held turn. A held round that
   already went on to a tool is unaffected.
2. **The first-draft copy rewinds what the cut removed.** When the copy drops the
   pages, it also clears every check's `due` whose trigger is a build milestone
   (`first_pass_complete`, `revision_round_finished`, `before_fan_out`,
   `before_publish`), clears `first_pass_at` and `revision_rounds`, and drops a
   task's `told` that refers to a draft. The copied plan reads as "direction
   agreed, nothing built".

## What landed

**1. A held cue turn carries on, once.** `keepClientOriented` (`cadence-core.ts`)
notes, per turn, whether the speak-first gate refused a call and whether any call
was issued after that. If the turn refused one, then spoke (client-visible text),
then ended without issuing another call, it calls a new `speakFirst.stranded` hook.
The host records the site in `strandedTurns`, and `continuedTurn` (`host-core.ts`)
carries the turn on with `heldContinuationCue()` (new `held-continuation-cue`
template in `priming.json`): "your previous reply told your client what you are
doing next, then ended without doing it… do it now, starting with the call that was
held back until you had spoken". Rules:
- It is on REQ-409's terms (`ended`: continuations left, spend ceiling, not
  stopped/errored/exhausted), at most once per client message, and only when
  nothing more specific is owed (a layout, review or promise cue wins).
- The cue is a host cue (kept off the client's transcript) but **does not hold the
  gate**, because the client has just read the consultant's words. Without that,
  the carry-on would be refused again.
- A held round that speaks *and* goes on to a call is not continued.
- The carry-on lands in the same live bubble as a new paragraph (BUG-241).

**2. The first-draft copy rewinds what the source's build set.** `firstDraftPlan`
(`first-draft.ts`) now also:
- clears `due` on every check whose due trigger is a build moment
  (`first_pass_complete`, `revision_round_finished`, `before_fan_out`,
  `before_publish`). A check due on any other trigger keeps its `due`;
- drops a task's `told` when it times the task against a draft (mentions "draft"
  or "first pass"). Other `told`s are kept.
`first_pass_at` and `revision_rounds` live in `milestones`, which REQ-450 already
clears (`DRAFT_HISTORY_KEYS`). The UAT pins that they stay cleared.
