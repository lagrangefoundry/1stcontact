## Your account

These terms apply when you create an account on {{site_address}}, run by {{business_name}} ({{contact_email}}).

- You sign in with a link sent to your email address. Keep access to that email address secure, because anyone who can read it can sign in as you.
- Use your account only for yourself, and keep the details in it accurate.
- We may suspend an account that is used to break the law or our terms.
- You can close your account at any time by contacting us. When you close it, it is hidden straight away and your account information is deleted 30 days later, in case you change your mind, except what the law requires us to keep.
- We may update these terms. If a change is important, we will ask you to accept the new version the next time you sign in.
