## Capability: Fidelity Value-Diff Reporting

How the values-diff fidelity comparison structures, presents, and scopes the
difference between a captured reference site and its reproduction — the report
model and its rendering. Governs *how differences are surfaced to a reader*
(object grouping, position as a first-class column, loud unpaired reporting,
paste-able spec vocabulary, clean-object collapse, non-object tails), as
distinct from the extraction correctness and tolerance policy that decide *what
counts as a difference*.

Established during reconciliation of BUNDLE-5 (REQ-51..REQ-57 free-coded work).
Home for the object-grouped report (REQ-51) and related reporting behaviours.
