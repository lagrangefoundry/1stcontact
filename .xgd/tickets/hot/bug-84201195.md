## Symptom

Operator, 2026-11-08: on a production business whose site has never been published, the Shop tab says "Your site has no Terms of sale yet, so there is no Buy: a buyer has to be able to read what they agree to. They are added when you publish, once selling is set up — see Paperwork → Legal." Payments, shipping and the business details are all set up. The operator wants to run real payments through the site's preview to prove checkout works, without publishing a test site.

## Root cause

`sellerReadiness` (`apps/control-app/src/client-legal.ts`) reads the Terms of sale **on a published site**: `legalOnSite` → the newest `legal_site_facts` record. Those records are written only by `planLegalPublish(...).commit()`, which runs at a site publish or rebuild (`siteLegalFor` in `router.ts`). With no published site there is no record, so `termsOfSale` is `none`. `canSell` is then false in the Shop tab (`orders.ts`), at checkout (`sellerMissing`, `catalog.ts`) and in the preview. The preview's own legal pages (`legalPagesNow`) do show Terms of sale, but no version is written, so an order would have nothing to record.

## Fix: when the version exists, not a relaxation

A buyer must still be able to read the Terms of sale before paying, and every order still records the exact version. What changes is *when* that version exists. Since [[REQ-669]] the documents are the business's, not a site's, so:

- **The business's legal versions are written when its legal facts change, with or without a published site.** That is when selling becomes possible (payments, shipping and seller details all in place) or the details or regions change. The Terms of sale's first version is written then, from the standard text and the business details. The legal publication that already runs after a Paperwork → Legal save does this for a business with no published site, rather than skipping it.
- **`sellerReadiness` asks whether the business has a current Terms of sale version**, not whether a published site carries one. `none` then means only that selling is not set up yet.
- **The preview checkout links and records that version**, exactly as a published site's checkout does. A real payment through the preview works end to end, and the order holds the Terms of sale version the buyer was shown.
- **A later first publish carries the versions already written** and writes no duplicate.
- **`NO_TERMS_OF_SALE_PROMPT` loses "They are added when you publish".** If it can still appear, it says what is missing (for example "Your Terms of sale are written once payments, shipping and your business details are set up").
- **The reminder stays; only the block goes** (operator, 2026-11-08: "I still want to see the reminder prompting me to fill in the biz details etc. I just don't want payments blocked"). The Shop's reminder, and its "Fill in business details" button to Paperwork → Legal, show exactly as now whenever a seller detail is missing. This ticket removes only the publish dependency that kept Buy off once everything was set up. Operator, clarifying: "DO block a buy until biz details are filled in and ToS are ready. DON'T block a buy waiting for PUBLISH if the site itself is unpublished." Buy stays off until the seller details are filled in and the Terms of sale version exists; a site publish is never a condition.
- Every business alike, 1st Contact included. No special case for unpublished or test sites.

Supersedes the [[REQ-666]] statement that Terms of sale are added at publish.

## What was built

- **A legal facts record can be the business's, not a publish's.** `legal_site_facts` gains `published` (absent on older records = true). `planLegalPublish(store, sources, site, facts, { published: false })` builds the documents exactly as a publish would, naming the site the business will publish (its address fills `{{site_address}}`), and `commit` writes the versions and a facts record with `published: false`.
- **The rebuild path commits it when no site is published.** `catalogItemChanged` (`catalog.ts`), after rebuilding every published site, runs the new `legalWithoutSite` step for the business's first site when none was rebuilt, inside that site's lock. `siteLegalFor` (`router.ts`) supplies it (`withoutSite`), recording any notices it starts as a publish does. It writes nothing while a seller detail is missing: the first version is written from the details once they are all in place, so no Terms of sale carries a "not set yet" placeholder. (Found in regression: REQ-666's suite sets shipping before the details, and an ungated write turned its expected v1 into v2.)
- **What re-plans the documents now includes a shipping save.** `rebuildCatalogFor` re-plans legal on `legal`, `payments` and now `shipping` (selling needs a zone, and where it ships sets the regions). So whichever of payments, shipping or the details is set up last writes the Terms of sale's first version.
- **`legalOnSite` returns the business's versions in force before any publish**, with `site: null` (no published site to link to), so `sellerReadiness`, the checkout's order record (`terms_of_sale_uid`, `privacy_uid`) and the preview's legal pages all read that version.
- **The first publish carries those versions.** Same site, same text: no new version is written; a facts record with `published: true` is written because the record's published state changed.
- **`NO_TERMS_OF_SALE_PROMPT`** (`builder/shop.js`) now reads "There is no Buy yet: a buyer has to be able to read your Terms of sale, and they are written once payments, shipping and your business details are set up — see Paperwork → Legal." It no longer mentions a publish. The seller-details reminder and its button are unchanged.

A business already fully set up before this lands has no event to write its version: any save in Paperwork → Legal (or a payments or shipping change) writes it.
