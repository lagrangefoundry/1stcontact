# Site Capture Recording Contract

What a capture of a live site must RECORD, as distinct from what any consumer
does with it. Owns the in-page value extraction, the capture driver's settling
and web-font barriers, the bundle's mirrored substance (assets, fonts, raw and
rendered HTML), the multi-viewport projection ladder plus its height probe, and
offline re-extraction from a written bundle.

Distinct from the fold (CAP-71, which consumes a capture to build an L1
document), from values-diff (CAP-63, which compares two captures) and from the
reproduction gate (CAP-73, which grades a reproduction against a retained
oracle). Those three are all downstream readers of this contract: a fact the
capture does not record cannot be folded, compared or graded by any of them.
