## What happened
A consultant turn commissioned a builder (`Delegate`, role builder) whose scope included `site.json`, `work.json`, `shop.json`. The client interrupted that turn. The builder (worker-builder-6-bp49pl) had already landed two changes (page.add `work` at change 602, page.add `shop` at 603) and was stopped.

In the next turn, two `Delegate` calls scoped to `site.json`, `work.json`, `shop.json` (to delete those two pages) were both refused with `scope_held`, naming worker-builder-6-bp49pl as holding `shop.json` "since 03:21 (2 min)". Both refusals reported the same age, and `list_changes since 603` showed nothing new, so the worker was not running. The hold looks orphaned rather than live.

## Expected
When a turn is interrupted and its worker is stopped (as the Delegate docs say happens), the worker's scope holds are released, so the next turn can commission work on those files.

## Actual
The hold survives the interruption. Any delegation touching `shop.json` (and so any cleanup of the pages the interrupted worker created) is refused. Those half-built pages stay in the site navigation, visible to the client, with no way to remove them from the consultant side, since the consultant has no direct page-delete tool.

## Repro
1. Delegate a builder with scope [`site.json`, `X.json`] that calls `add_page` for X early.
2. Interrupt the consultant turn (client sends a message) after the page.add lands.
3. Next turn: Delegate with scope including `X.json` → `scope_held`, holder = the stopped worker, age frozen.

## Also noted
The "since … (N min)" age in the refusal did not advance between two calls a few seconds apart, which made it hard to tell a stale hold from a live one. The refusal should say whether the holder session is still running.

Follow-up: the hold also blocks the consultant's own `set_design` (refused: "`site.json` is held by builder worker-builder-6-bp49pl since 03:21 UTC (4 min); nothing was written"). The age does advance (2 min → 4 min), but `list_changes` still shows no change after 603, so the worker is not visibly doing anything. So an interrupted delegation can lock the consultant out of design changes as well as further delegations.

## Root cause (from the dev junction + D1, 2026-10-26)
- 03:21:51 consultant turn `72b0e991` delegated the page split; worker-builder-6-bp49pl claimed its scope (D1 `draft_leases`).
- A talker round opened and closed beside it (03:21:54–03:22:02), so the framework's `callerStopped` no longer sees the consultant's open turn and a Stop on the consultant never reaches the builder (known lagrange-framework gap; REQ-436 works round it for the ceiling only).
- 03:22:43 the client pressed Stop. The consultant round ended `aborted` at once, with its `Delegate` call still pending. The builder's third model request was in flight and never recorded.
- The hold's only release is the framework's `finally` around the worker run, and that `finally` never ran. The leases' last renewal was 03:22:37, so they sat until the idle expiry (`lease_idle_seconds` 300) at 03:27:37. That matches both refusals and the blocked `set_design`.
- The refusal printed "since <acquired> (N min)": when the hold was taken, not when it will lapse. A stale hold and a live one read the same.

## Fix
1. **A consultant turn's builders end with it.** Every consultant turn already ends in one host `finally`, whatever the outcome (complete, aborted, error, walked away). That `finally` now goes through the builders this turn started (the REQ-458 worker → turn record). It stops each one by name (`requestStop`) and releases its hold. A builder whose delegation ended normally has already released through the framework, so nothing happens for it. The release runs at most once: the framework's own `finally` calling it later is a no-op.
2. **A refusal says when an idle hold lapses.** A held-file refusal (the consultant's own refused writes such as `set_design`, and the 409 bodies) now reads "…; nothing was written. It is free again when that work finishes, or at HH:MM UTC if it does nothing more." That time is the lease's expiry, carried on `DraftFileRefusal.lapses`. The `scope_held` refusal says "Treat that build as still running: do not tell the client the draft or its pages are ready until it has finished. Its files are free again when it finishes, or at HH:MM UTC if it does nothing more." A hold nobody is renewing keeps one release time; a working build's moves on. The "`file` is held by … since … (N min), started by consultant turn …" prefix is unchanged (REQ-397 and REQ-458 pin it).

Not here: the framework's `callerStopped` (open turn = last `turn_start`, including talker forks). That needs a lagrange-framework ticket. This fix does not depend on it.

- `…a_stopped_turn_releases_its_stuck_builders_files_for_the_next_delegation`: a consultant turn delegates a builder scoped to `site.json` and `home.json`. The client then presses Stop. Once the turn has ended, no lease remains, and the next turn's `Delegate` on the same files starts its builder and is not refused (`scope_held` / "is held by"). Through a real delegation, the `scope_held` answer carries "Its files are free again when it finishes, or at HH:MM UTC if it does nothing more."
