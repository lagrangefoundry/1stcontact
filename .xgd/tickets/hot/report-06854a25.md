## Notes for the Editor

**One cross-cutting pattern: Astro's removal is still visible in prose.** Finding 1
is the load-bearing instance (an AC), but the same retired fact survives in two
other places an editor will be near:

- STORY-118's body, `## Story` → the two-runtimes paragraph ("keeps the Astro
  container-render path the previous single configuration existed for") and the
  `### In scope` list. That is a story-level edit, out of this level's scope, but
  it is the same stale claim and should be repaired in the same pass rather than
  left to be re-derived.
- `tests/support/site-store-contract.ts:48-54` and
  `tests/test_UAT_FC_REQ-142_site_store_port.test.ts:103-106` both explain the two
  render cases' placement by "rendering runs through Astro's container API, which
  workerd has no transform for". The *placement* is still right (workerd has no
  build transform at all); only the reason names a dependency that is gone. These
  are comments, not assertions, so they change no verdict — but they are where the
  retracted fact will be re-read from next.

**What is deliberately NOT a finding.** STORY-118 records a "known divergence
between the tree and its own explanation": the pool-version pin comment in
`vitest.workers.config.mts:28-34` still carries a diagnosis the story says was
retracted after promotion. The story explicitly states that acceptance criteria
were written about the routing convention and the bindings and encode neither the
pin nor its rationale — and AC-1328 and AC-1329 do exactly that. The matrix is
therefore correct here and the divergence is confined to a code comment awaiting
an operator decision; nothing at this level should touch it.

**Quality of the evidence, recorded because it bears on future checks.** These
suites go out of their way to be non-vacuous in ways worth preserving: AC-1393
plants real content outside the assets namespace before asserting confinement;
AC-1395 guards on `bySlug.size > 0` so an empty `storage/sites/` cannot make the
comparison pass by asserting nothing; AC-1398 mutates the TOML and re-runs its own
pairing to prove the check can fail; AC-1385 asserts the question set's membership
against the criterion's own list so a store cannot pass by being asked less than
the surface asks it. A future editor trimming these should know they are the
substance, not ceremony.
