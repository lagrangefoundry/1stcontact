## Symptom

The platform operator opens another customer's business from the Console tab's
**Open** control (REQ-367 — "entering" a business, e.g. faelanwesthead /
faelan.com). They then leave it — the banner's way back, or the business
switcher, or a reload at `/` — and go to their own business. The Site tab keeps
showing the entered customer's site (faelan.com) even though every other surface
has moved to the operator's own business.

## Root cause

The operator's way back lands on their first own business (1st Contact itself),
whose only page is the `/account` portal — and `/api/sites` deliberately lists
only kind `site`, so that business has **no sites**. `selectBusiness` therefore
calls `panel.setSite(null)`, and the display panel's `show()` computes an empty
URL for a null site and `navigate()` wrote it into the iframe as `src=""`.
Browsers do not unload an iframe for an empty `src`, so the frame went on
showing the document it already held — the entered customer's site. A reload
reproduces it too: `restore()` reopens the remembered (entered) site first, then
the switch to the site-less business writes `src=""` over it.

## Fix

`packages`-free, one spot: `apps/control-app/src/builder/panel.js` `navigate()`.
When the pane has nothing to show (empty URL), the frame is pointed at
`about:blank`, so it genuinely unloads. The panel's own bookkeeping (`rec.src`,
`getSrc()`, the `src` event) still reads `''` for "nothing shown", so no
consumer changes.

User-visible behaviour: after switching to a business with no sites, the Site
tab's preview is blank — never a previously shown (possibly another customer's)
site.

## Test plan

`tests/test_UAT_FC_BUG-207_*.test.ts` (jsdom, real `mountBuilder` + real panel):
enter a business that has a site, take the banner's way back to an own business
with no sites, and assert the shown preview frame no longer points at the entered
site (it is `about:blank`); also the remount-from-storage case. Regression scope:
the REQ-367 chrome suite and the panel suites (BUG-79 / REQ-391).
