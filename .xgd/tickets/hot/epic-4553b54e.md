## Goal

A repeatable test of **first-draft production**, the pivot point of a site build. It should answer:
- Is L1 giving us the site we need?
- Is the principal/delegate model working?
- Would different delegate models do significantly better?

Today each run needs a full discovery conversation from scratch. This epic makes it possible to **create a test business whose discovery is already done**, copied from an existing business (e.g. Dina's Diner), and then have the consultant build the first draft. Triggering the draft may be manual, but it must be possible.

Motivation: DOC-67 §1 and the end of EPIC-19's transcript (2026-10-24). Dina's first draft triggered six new tickets under EPIC-19.

## UX (operator's proposal)

On People → **Provision a business** (`apps/control-app/src/builder/people.js:1473`), add:
- a "First-draft test" option;
- a picker for the business to copy discovery from.

Choosing it prefixes the business name with `First-Draft-Test`.

## Feasibility of the data copy (investigated 2026-10-24)

**What "discovery" is, and where it lives.** It is almost entirely in the source business's own ticket store, `ticketStoreFor(..., {businessId})` (`tickets.ts`):

| Data | Ticket type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plan: stages, brief, decisions, asks, comps, story board, public details | `plan` (one per site, keyed by `site_key`) | `comps[].reference` and `decisions[].comps` hold **reference uids**, which need remapping. `site_key` is rewritten to the new site. |
| Uploads and photos, with AI text shadows | `material` + attachments | Labels are stored, so they travel. Bodies (text shadows) travel, so nothing is re-described. |
| Comparison sites, with likes/dislikes | `reference` + attachments | Capture bundles of many members each. |
| The conversation | `chat` + `chat_transcript` / `tool_transcript` comments | `chat-copy.ts` (REQ-294, BUG-137, BUG-159) already exports, imports and **re-addresses** a business's chats to a destination's business id and site key, and strips runtime pointers. |

Nothing else is needed:
- The new business gets its own starter site from `provisionBusiness`. The draft is built into it, so the source's site is not copied.
- The project KB is built from the same four types, so it follows from the copied tickets.

**Existing machinery to reuse:**
- `provisionBusiness` (`identity.ts:778`): business, membership, entitlement, starter site and seeded plan.
- `readChats` / `writeChats` (`chat-copy.ts`).
- The rest needs a sibling for `plan` + `material` + `reference`, with uid remapping.

**Blobs must be re-uploaded, not relinked.** The blob store is tenant-partitioned by `forTenant`, so each attachment is read from the source and attached to the target.

**Size of Dina's Diner** (local dev D1, `biz_5fc6adae…`):
- 1 plan, 11 materials, 4 references, 9 chats;
- 395 attachments, about 102 MB: 14 MB of material and 88 MB across the 4 captures.

That is too many R2 operations to trust to one request, so the copy has to be chunked or resumable (a Queue or repeated calls). It is feasible, but not a single synchronous call.

### The hard part: the source is past discovery
A live business is not a discovery snapshot. Dina's plan has `first_draft` and `refining` in progress, 5 revision rounds, a critique and builder/critic chats. **And Dina's history has no clean seam to cut at:**
- `getting_to_know_you` was done at 01:12;
- the first builder pass was at **01:18**;
- `other_sites` was done at **03:03**.

Discovery and building interleaved (before BUG-269 and REQ-445 enforced the order). Neither cut works:
- cutting at 01:18 loses the comparison work;
- cutting at 03:03 includes about two hours of building.

The ticket change log (`ticket_changes`) keeps each version's **fields but not bodies**, so the plan can only be partly rewound. Its decision log is in the body.

### Decision (operator, 2026-10-24): copy the whole business, then blank the site
Snapshot-then-copy was considered and rejected. The approach is:
1. **Copy the entire source business's ticket store** into the new test business: plan, material, references and chats, with their attachments.
2. **Replace the copied site's contents with the blank starter site** that provisioning creates.

This is deliberately *not* a faithful rewind of the source to its pre-draft state. It meets the two requirements:
- **Sufficient content** to build a first draft from.
- **Reproducible**: copying the same source gives the same starting point, as long as the old test businesses aren't re-edited. Sources are treated as read-only fixtures by convention.

**What still has to happen so the copy is coherent with a blank site:**
- **Plan reset.** Every stage from "First draft" on goes back to not started, and the build milestones and critique are cleared. Otherwise the consultant reads a first draft as already done (Dina: `first_pass_at` set, 5 revision rounds).
- **Business-scoped data stays behind.** People, contacts, domains and sending settings are not copied, so a test business can never email anyone from the source.
- **The client-facing name is the source's.** The `First-Draft-Test` prefix is on the business record only. The starter site's `<title>` and the plan's public details keep the source's name.

**Known, accepted contamination:** the copied conversation includes whatever happened after the source's first draft (feedback, refinements, builder and critic chats). The consultant can read it, so a test draft may benefit from feedback on an earlier draft. Accepted as good enough. Builder and critic chats belong to the old site's build, so leaving them out costs nothing.

**Triggering the draft:** manual is enough. The operator opens the test business and asks the consultant for the first draft.
