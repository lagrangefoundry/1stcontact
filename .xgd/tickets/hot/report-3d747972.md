`0.2.121 → 0.2.122`, not BUG-58 content — the version line is the whole of the

collision: the two sides are not competing claims about the same fact, they are
two points on a sequence, and the resync branch is at the later one. This is the
same ground recorded for every preceding version conflict in this resync run
(`resolve-377b581e-61a0becc61`, `-242a8bf969`, `-b38dbdabf1`, `-164e51cba7`),
applied consistently. The file is flagged for post-merge review as the
