## Symptom

First-Draft-Test Dina's Diner 3, 2026-10-25. The home page opens on the steak and
eggs hero, then **Hal's counter** (the owner's story), then the coffee bell, the
menu teaser, the Dino burgers, Who stops here, reviews, the catering teaser and
Find us. On a phone that's 11.5 screens. The operator: "The AI loves to lead this
section with the Hal's counter story — even with multiple pages that story still
gets top billing."

REQ-460 says the owner's story gets its own page, and the home page has a
6-screen budget. Both were live for this run.

## What happened

- **The direction card fixed the order.** The consultant's direction summary,
  which the client hearted, already said "opening on the steak and eggs … then
  the room with Hal's red stools". The structure decision then copied that order:
  "Home order: steak-and-eggs hero …, Hal's counter, the coffee bell, …". The
  story stayed second because the client had "agreed" to it as a look.
- **The length budget fired and was argued away.** `home_length` reported 16
  screens after the first build. The builder answered "this is content length,
  not a bug". The consultant moved the full menu out (11.5 screens) and kept
  everything else.
- **Then the host called it stuck.** After BUG-256's fix rounds, the finding came
  back as "cannot be fixed this way: do not start another builder for it. Tell
  your client it is left as it is, or take a different approach yourself."
- **The client was told the opposite.** The hand-back said "Your full menu got
  its own page so the home page stays quick to get through on a phone."
- The critic, whose REQ-460 rubric flags this, didn't run. The layout hold was
  still open when the restart aborted the fix turn.

`home_length` is a **structure** fault: no builder working inside a page can fix
it, so routing it through the builder fix loop guarantees it ends as "stuck".

## Fix

1. **A structure fault goes to the principal, not to a builder.** `long_home` is
   never in a builder's fix lines and never counts towards BUG-256's stuck
   rounds. It holds the hand-back with its own cue to the principal: "the home
   page is N screens: change the site structure (`pages_navigation`). Move what
   serves the curious (the story, the full menu, a business within the business)
   to its own page with a teaser, then build that." It clears only when the
   measured home page is within budget.
2. **The direction is a look, not an order.** The direction summary the client
   signs off (REQ-432's `direction_agreed`) says what leads and how the site
   looks and feels. The home page's section order is set by the structure
   decision under REQ-460's visitor-first rule, which the principal states to the
   client in one line. A direction that names the owner's story as a home
   section is reworded to "the story, on its own page, teased from home".
3. **The hand-back can't contradict the check.** While `long_home` stands, the
   hand-back may not describe the home page as short or quick. The host's review
   of the reply (the promise hold) flags it.

## Test plan

`tests/test_UAT_FC_BUG-287_a_long_home_is_restructured_by_the_principal.*`:
- A build whose home page measures 11.5 phone screens: the builder's fix lines
  don't include `long_home`, and the principal gets the structure cue. After
  three builder fix rounds, it's still not reported as stuck.
- The hold clears once a re-plan moves the story to `/story` with a home teaser,
  and the measured home page is 6 screens or fewer.
- A direction summary naming "Hal's counter" as the second home section is
  refused with the look-not-order message. The structure decision for the
  diner's brief puts hours, directions and the menu teaser before any story
  teaser.
- A hand-back saying "quick to get through" while `long_home` stands is flagged.
- Re-run the REQ-460, BUG-256, REQ-436 and REQ-432 UATs.
