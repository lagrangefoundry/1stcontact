The 1stcontact.io marketing site.

One of the two flagship sites in DOC-16 section 4. Unlike the Lagrange Foundry site, this one must be built WITH the product path -- it is the dogfood case and the REQ-19 expressive-ceiling proof. DOC-5 is explicit that there is one kind of site: the 1st Contact marketing site is an ordinary site on the same model, authored, stored and served exactly like a customer site, with no special path. The control app is a separate application and does not pass through the framework renderer.

Evidence: site store at storage/sites/1stcontact with a draft, a README, and one revision in history.

Design bar per DOC-16: Tier-1 expensive and template-free, benchmarked against the comps corpus (DOC-15). Lessons captured to the Design Lessons Log (DOC-17).
