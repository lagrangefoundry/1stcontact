## Symptom (operator, 2026-11-05, dev, a shop site)

The home page's hero video is suddenly **about half its height and much darker**. Nobody edited the page: the site's change log has no edit to `home` since 15:50 UTC that morning. The change came from code.

## Likely cause: BUG-355's overlay-over-a-clip change (3fea82eeee)

BUG-355 changed how an `overlay` section whose picture is a clip is drawn. The clip and its scrim are now **layers filling the section** (new pin `fill`: absolutely placed, `width`/`height: 100%`, `z-index: -1`, with the section isolated), under the text.

This hero is shaped like that, but differently from the case BUG-355 fixed:
- section `hero`: `composition: overlay`, `width: bleed`;
- child 0: a `video` with **a stated height of 720px** (`sizing.height: fixed 720`), `objectFit: cover`;
- child 1: the text group, **itself placed `absolute`** (`geometry` keyframes, with its own top-to-transparent gradient), plus a kicker, a display title and a button.

Once the clip is a fill layer, it no longer gives the section its height. The section is sized by what's left in flow, which is little or nothing because the text is absolute too, so the hero shrinks to a fraction of its 720px. The darkening is probably the text group's own gradient (alpha 0.5 → 0), and/or a scrim now stretched over the whole, shorter hero. Confirm both by rendering this section before and after 3fea82eeee.

## Fix

- **An overlay hero keeps the height its picture states.** When the clip, or picture, that becomes the fill layer has a stated height or aspect, the section takes that height (as a minimum height) at every width. A section can't shrink because its picture became a layer.
- **Its darkness doesn't change unless someone asks for it.** A scrim covers exactly what it covered before. The text group's own gradient stays the size of the text group, not stretched over the hero.
- **Existing sites keep rendering as they did.** Every live and draft overlay hero must look the same before and after, except for the BUG-355 cases that were actually broken (text below the clip).
- **The layout check catches it next time.** A hero whose height changes by more than about 25% with no edit to it shows up in the host's look at the page. At minimum, the regression test below pins this shape.

## Test plan

- UAT (render, synthetic site): an `overlay` section with a 720px `video` and an absolutely placed text group renders 720px tall at 375, 768 and 1280, with the text over the clip. The text group's gradient covers only the text group.
- UAT: BUG-355's own case (an overlay clip whose text used to fall below it) still renders the text over the clip.


## Implementation (free-coded, 2026-11-05)

**Confirmed by rendering** the ticket's shape (720px clip, text group placed by absolute geometry with its own gradient): on the code as it stood the hero rendered **128px tall** at 375 — just the overlay composition's padding — with a full-section 0.55 scrim added and the text recoloured white. Before BUG-355 an overlay section whose picture was a clip was not composed at all (the old picture finder only saw stills), so it rendered as built.

**What changed** (`composeSection`, `overlay` case, `packages/site-schema/src/l1/design.ts`):
- **Text already placed over the clip → the section is left as built.** If every non-picture child is out of flow (has a `pin`, or `geometry` that isn't `place: flow`), the text is already over the clip. That isn't the BUG-355 case, so the composition doesn't touch the section. It renders byte-identical to the same section with no composition: same height, no scrim, the text group's gradient its own size, its text colours unchanged.
- **Text in flow (BUG-355's case) → layers as before, plus a minimum height.** The clip and scrim are still fill layers under the text. The section now gets `sizing.height: { mode: hug, minPx }` from the clip's stated height (fixed `px`, else `minPx`), so it can't shrink below the clip, and text taller than the clip can still grow it.

**Not covered:**
- **A clip that states only a design `aspect`** gives the section no minimum. Containers have no aspect axis in L1, and adding one was out of scope here. Such a hero with in-flow text is sized by its text plus padding, as in BUG-355.
- **No layout-check rule** for "hero height changed >25% with no edit" was added. That needs a before/after baseline the host doesn't keep. The regression test pins this shape, as the ticket allows at minimum.

**Tests:** `tests/test_UAT_FC_BUG-365_overlay_hero_keeps_its_clips_height.test.ts`: the placed-text hero renders the same as built; it renders 720px at 375/768/1280 with the text over the clip, no scrim, and its gradient group at its own 360px; a flowing-text hero renders 720px with the text over the clip. BUG-355's suite still passes (6/6). Of 187 composition/overlay-related test files, the 56 failing tests fail identically with and without the change (pre-existing).
