## Symptom

Ariel's Arc Welding 2 (site_5ddc942a…), dev, 2026-10-26 UTC. The client asked for the heron silhouette
as the favicon and beside the title in the header (03:11). When the consultant's reply ended
(03:20:51), the host held it with the home-length cue: *"the home page is 18.7 screens tall (12461px),
over the home page's budget of 6 … change the site's structure yourself … This is held until the
home page measures within its budget."* The consultant obeyed: it proposed four pages
(`set_decision pages_navigation`) and began adding "Work & prices" and another page. The client
stopped it ("Please don't rearrange the page - just do the favicon as we discussed"). The consultant
then removed the two empty pages and recorded "keep the structure as it was". The operator: "I think
actually it is the right thing to do but it's not what I asked for".

## Cause

BUG-287's home-length hold (`isHomeStructureCue`, `tools/generate/src/cli/ai/host-core.ts`) runs at the
end of every consultant reply on every site whose home page is over the length budget (REQ-460), and
tells the consultant to restructure. It was designed for structuring a first draft. On an established
site with a long home page, every unrelated request (a favicon, an image edit) becomes a forced
restructure. A recorded client refusal doesn't stop it.

## Fix

1. **It holds only when the turn is doing structural work.** The hold fires when the reply is part
   of building the first draft, when the turn changed the site's structure (pages, navigation, the
   home page's sections), or when the turn made the home page longer. Otherwise it doesn't hold.
2. **On an unrelated request it's a suggestion, said once.** When the home page is over budget and
   the turn wasn't structural, the consultant may mention once, in a line, that the home page is
   long and offer to split it. It doesn't change the structure unless the client agrees. Once offered
   on a site, it isn't repeated until the home page changes or the client raises it.
3. **The client's no sticks.** When the client declines a restructure (recorded as the
   `pages_navigation` decision kept as is, or an explicit refusal), the hold and the suggestion stay
   quiet for that site until the client raises the structure again or the home page grows.

## Test plan

UATs named `test_UAT_FC_BUG-297_*`:
- a long home page on an established site, and a turn that only places an image and edits the
  header: no home-length hold; at most one suggestion line; no structural tool call is prompted;
- the same site during a first-draft build, or a turn that adds a section to home: the hold fires
  as today;
- after the client declines (the decision recorded as keep-as-is), a later turn that made home no
  longer gets neither the hold nor the suggestion.

## What changed

- **The hold decides per look** (`homeLengthMode`, `tools/generate/src/cli/ai/host-core.ts`). When the
  host looks at home after a build and home is over its budget:
  - **hold** (BUG-287's structure block in the `Delegate` result, plus the reply held with the
    home-structure cue) when the site is still in its first draft (`!draftReviewed`: the client has
    not yet seen and answered the draft), when the build changed the structure (the served pages, or
    how many sections home has), when home measured more than half a phone screen taller than at the
    host's previous look, or when this message is already held for it;
  - **offer** otherwise, once: the `Delegate` result carries "THE HOME PAGE'S LENGTH, for you and not
    a builder:" with the measurement and the `home-length-offer` note (mention it once, in one line;
    change nothing unless the client agrees). Nothing holds the reply;
  - **quiet** (nothing said) once the offer has been made on the site, or once the client's no is
    recorded — until home grows past the last look, which makes the turn structural again.
- **What the host remembers across messages** is a new plan field, `home_length`
  (`{ screens, shape, offered_at?, kept_at? }`, in `PLAN_KEYS`): home's height at the last look (0 when
  within budget), the structure signature, and when the offer was made / the no recorded. Growth clears
  both the offer and the no; home coming within budget clears both.
- **The client's no**: `set_decision` takes `kept: true` for `pages_navigation` (declared in
  `plan-surface.json`; refused on any other decision). It stamps `home_length.kept_at`. A later
  `pages_navigation` written with a value and without `kept` is the client raising the structure again
  and lifts the no. A no recorded while the reply is held releases the hold at the reply's end
  (`structureKept`). The hold note and the offer both tell the consultant to record the no this way.
- Navigation order alone is not part of the structure signature: it does not change home's length.
