## Notes for the Editor

- **The AC set is unusually well partitioned.** Where two criteria could have
  overlapped, the ACs cross-reference each other by number instead of restating
  (AC-892→AC-925, AC-905→AC-927, AC-906→AC-910). If a future edit splits or
  renumbers any of these, the referencing AC must be updated in the same pass or
  the disclaimers become dangling.
- **BUG-31 landed as four ACs across two stories** (AC-924/925/926 on the deploy
  side, AC-927 on the serving side), matching the bug's own finding that the
  store-tree separation was dropped at the shared-storage boundary on both
  halves. The write side and the read side each carry their own criterion; that
  is deliberate, not duplication.
- **REQ-113's corrected premise is recorded, not absorbed.** STORY-96's body
  documents that the original intent's "Cloudflare Pages auto-serves `.html`"
  premise was false and that the real state was the inverse. No AC carries the
  stale premise. STORY-96's Technical Context does note a *stale in-code comment*
  still citing the original premise — that is source-comment drift, outside this
  level's remit, and is worth a cleanup at some point but is not an AC finding.
- **Both intents reached this capability as bundles**, so `fields.intent_uid` on
  every story points at a bundle UID rather than a REQ/BUG. Per-AC intent
  attribution had to be reconstructed from the bundle bodies; ACs carry no
  `intent_uid` of their own. A future check will have to do the same walk.
