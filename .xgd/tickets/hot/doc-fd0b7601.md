# Social exports: getting a client's own Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and Google Business Profile content

**Audience:** the Site consultant. When the client mentions one of these platforms, give them that platform's entry below, in your own words and briefly: where to ask, what to choose, how long it takes, and "when the email arrives, upload the file here". Never ask for a password and never offer to log in for them; the export is theirs to request. Each export arrives as one ZIP file, and they upload it like any other file (the Library, a question on the panel, or the chat's attach button). The Library unpacks it in the background, and you are told what it added: photos and videos as pictures (the 100 most recent), and the text (captions, posts, profile, reviews) as documents you can read. Private messages and other people's details are never imported. A ZIP can be up to 95 MB; past that, ask them to export a shorter date range or lower media quality, or to upload each part separately.

## Instagram export

Where: in the Instagram app or on instagram.com, **Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information → Download or transfer information**, then choose the business account and **Some of your information**.
What to choose: **Posts** (and Personal information, for the bio); **Download to device**; Date range **All time**; Format **JSON**; Media quality **High** (choose Medium if the account has a lot of photos and the file would be over 95 MB).
How long: from a few minutes to a day. Instagram emails a link back to Accounts Center, and the file is downloaded from there.
Then: "When the email arrives, download the file and upload it here."

## Facebook export

Where: on facebook.com, **Settings & privacy → Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information**, then choose the business's profile or Page and **Some of your information**.
What to choose: **Posts** and **Profile information**; **Download to device**; Date range **All time**; Format **JSON**; Media quality **High** (Medium for a very large account).
How long: from a few minutes to a day, by email, as for Instagram.
Then: "When the email arrives, download the file and upload it here."

## LinkedIn export

Where: on linkedin.com, **Me → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data**.
What to choose: **Want something in particular?**, then tick **Profile**, **Positions**, **Articles** and **Shares** (their posts). LinkedIn asks for their password on its own page; that is LinkedIn's, never ours.
How long: about ten minutes for those items (a full archive can take up to a day). LinkedIn emails a download link.
Then: "When the email arrives, download the file and upload it here."

## Google Business Profile export (Google Takeout)

Where: **takeout.google.com**, signed in as the Google account that manages the business profile.
What to choose: **Deselect all**, then tick only **Google Business Profile**; **Next step**; **Send download link via email**; **Export once**; File type **.zip**; File size **1 GB** (a business with very many photos may still produce a file over 95 MB, in which case upload each part separately).
How long: usually minutes to a few hours. Google emails a **Download your files** link.
What it brings: the business information, the reviews with the business's replies (each review keeps the name it was published under), the posts and the photos.
Then: "When the email arrives, download the file and upload it here."
