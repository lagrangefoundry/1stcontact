## Why this exists

Downstream half of **`lagrange-framework` BUG-58**. Upstream is being fixed in
`webui-chat` so that a per-turn stream which ends without a terminal `done` is
treated as a turn whose fate is UNKNOWN rather than a turn that produced nothing:
the assistant bubble is kept and marked interrupted, no resend control is offered
(the resend is what duplicated the operator's prompt), the composer is released,
and the host is told via a new `opts.onTurnLost({markdown, error})`.

That stops the builder duplicating a prompt. **It does not repaint the reply**,
because the widget has no origin to ask. Asking is this side's job, and nothing
here does it yet.

## The gap

`apps/control-app/src/builder/chat.js` rejoins a live turn in exactly one place:
`setSession`, at MOUNT, using `live` + `cursor` from `POST /api/ai/session`
(BUG-46). A page that *loads* mid-turn reattaches and paints the tail.

**A panel already mounted, whose live stream dies, never reattaches.** There is
no mid-life recovery at all. The reply carries on being produced and drained —
`streamTurn`'s `ctx.waitUntil` sees to that — and the operator sits in front of a
panel that has stopped showing it. Their only recovery is a page reload, which is
how they came to lose a long prompt (see BUG-122).

The transport cannot currently tell the two endings apart either:
`readEventStream` (`builder/api.js:652`) yields frames until the response body
ends and says so deliberately — *"a chat turn that stops is a turn that
stopped."* That reading is what BUG-58 disproves.

## What this ticket should do

1. **Notice the loss.** Wire `onTurnLost` when mounting the panel in `chat.js`.
   Optionally tighten `readEventStream`/`postEventStream` so an unterminated
   chat stream is distinguishable from a finished one at the transport seam
   rather than only at the widget.

2. **Ask the origin what happened.** Re-read the conversation
   (`POST /api/ai/session`, which already answers `turns` + `cursor` + `live`).

3. **Recover according to the answer.**
   - `live` → `streamChatReattach(sessionId, cursor)` and hand that stream to
     `resume()`, which adopts the lost bubble so the reply is ONE message
     rather than two.
   - not `live` → the turn is over; repaint the pane from the fresh transcript
     rather than inventing a text-offset partition the junction cursor cannot
     express (the cursor is a junction offset, not a byte offset into the reply).

4. **Bound it.** A few attempts with backoff. When recovery genuinely fails, say
   so in the conversation — the operator is owed the reason, not a silent stall.

## Constraint worth deciding explicitly

`streamTurn`'s own comment states that an abandoned SSE *aborts* the turn:
*"ENQUEUEING ONTO A STREAM THE CLIENT MAY HAVE ABANDONED THROWS, and that throw
is load-bearing on the happy path — it is how an aborted SSE stops the turn."*

So depending on when the socket died, the thing being rejoined may already have
been closed as `aborted` with a partial reply. Reattach then paints a truncated
answer and calls it finished. Whether a turn *should* survive its watcher
leaving is a product decision this ticket should make deliberately rather than
inherit — it is the difference between "reload is lossy" and "walking away is
lossy."

## Dependency

Needs the `webui-chat` change from `lagrange-framework` BUG-58 to be installed
here (`bin/install --lang js --component all` upstream). The dependency is
implicit — nothing in this repo's `package.json` records it — so a `WEBUI_INSTALLED`
skip is the expected failure mode on a machine that has not installed it.
