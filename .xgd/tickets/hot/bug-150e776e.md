## Symptom

On a new business's plan panel, under "Getting to know your business", the features question (REQ-379's opening "Which of these does your site need?") shows as a list of checkboxes. **Ticking the first box collapses the whole question** into the answered list, holding that one feature, so the client can't pick more than one without reopening it each time.

## Root cause

`plan-panel.js` `editor()` (~L246–268) treats `multi_choice` like `single_choice`: every checkbox `change` calls `save(ask, { action: 'answer', answer: picked })` immediately. The ask becomes `answered` on the first tick, and `render()` moves answered asks to the done list (`doneBlock`, ~L425/448), where it shows collapsed. Saving on pick is right for a single choice and wrong for a multi-select, where the first pick is never the whole answer.

## Fix

1. **A multi-select stays open while the client picks.** Ticking and unticking don't move or collapse the question.
2. **The client finishes with an explicit "Done" button** on the question (or "None of these" when nothing applies). Only that saves the answer and moves the question to the answered list.
3. **The picks survive a reload or a panel re-render before "Done"**, by saving them as a draft that doesn't count as answered: the ask stays `open`, the progress count (REQ-379) still includes it, and the consultant's change notice doesn't report it. The consultant is told once, when the client presses "Done", with the full list.
4. **Reopening an answered multi-select** ("Change") shows the boxes ticked as saved and works the same way.
5. Single choice and typed inputs keep saving on pick, unchanged.

## Test plan

UATs named `test_UAT_FC_BUG-200_*`:
- Ticking several boxes on a `multi_choice` ask leaves it in the open list with all of them ticked, and doesn't mark it answered.
- Pressing "Done" saves the full selection, moves the ask to the answered list and decrements the progress count.
- "None of these" answers with an empty selection.
- Picks made before "Done" survive a re-render.
- A `single_choice` ask still saves on pick.


## What landed

- **A new client action, `draft`, on `POST /api/plan/ask`** (`plan-core.ts` `clientAnswer`, `CLIENT_ACTIONS`). It stores the ticked options on the ask as `draft` and changes nothing else: not `status` (an open ask stays open and counted; an answered ask being changed keeps its answer), not `answered_by`/`answered_at` (so the consultant's change notice never reports a draft). Answering or skipping, by the client or by the agent's `fill_ask`, clears the draft.
- **A draft is refused (400) on anything but a `multi_choice` ask, or when it names an option the ask doesn't have**, and nothing is stored. Single choice and typed inputs have no draft: they still answer on pick.
- **"None of these" is an answer**: a `multi_choice` answer may now be an empty list. It shows as "None of these" on the panel and reaches the consultant as `answered <id>: "none of these"`. For the features ask it marks every previously wanted feature not wanted.
- **The panel** (`plan-panel.js`): a multi-select draws its boxes from the draft if there is one, otherwise from the saved answer. Each tick sends a draft (only the latest draft's reply is drawn, so quick ticks can't be redrawn back to an earlier set). Under the boxes is one button labelled **Done** when something is ticked and **None of these** when nothing is; pressing it answers with every tick and moves the question to the answered list. "Change" on an answered multi-select opens the same editor.
