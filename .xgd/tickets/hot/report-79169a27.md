## Notes for the Editor

**One cause behind findings 1–5, and it is not the production code.** The reconciliation UATs
were authored against the AC set as it stood when BUNDLE-19 merged (`b18b859d7`,
2026-08-20 05:49). The ac-level fix loop then ran five repairs between 15:43 and 16:32 that
**added AC-1353 and AC-1354, widened AC-1321, narrowed AC-1327 and widened AC-1329** — all
after the tests were frozen. Findings 1–5 map one-to-one onto those five edits. Nothing
observed suggests a code bug, and **no `code-issue` was raised**, deliberately: every
suggested edit is test-side, and every claim the ACs make is reachable from code that already
exists.

**One near-miss worth recording so it is not re-derived as a code bug.** `createL1Toolbox`
(`main:tools/generate/src/cli/ai/toolbox.ts:493-505`) spreads `opts` and *then* overrides
`store: fsSiteStore(ctxOf(opts))`, so an injected store handed to it is discarded. That looks
like a violation of AC-1354 until you read the AC: the toolbox naming the filesystem adapter
once, at start-up, on the operator's machine is precisely what AC-1354 *requires*. The
injectable seam AC-1354's Verification asks for is one level down — `l1Operations(slug, opts)`,
exported at `toolbox.ts:176` with a comment saying it is exported exactly so it can be
exercised without a runtime AI import. Finding 2 should target that, not `createL1Toolbox`.

**Ordering, when unblocked.** Finding 4 first — it is a deletion, it is the claim AC-1327 was
repaired five times to expel, and leaving it in keeps CAP-85's evidence duplicated inside
CAP-101's. Then 3 and 5 (extensions to existing tests), then 1 and 2 (new tests, the larger
authoring jobs). Warnings 6–8 are cheap once their host tests are open.

**Findings 1 and 2 are smaller than they read.** AC-1353's substance already exists verbatim
under an FC name and needs lifting plus one new bullet. AC-1354 is genuinely new authoring, but
every seam it needs is exported and reachable.

**Blocked-on.** Finding 9 gates all of it. Nothing in findings 1–8 is disputed or deferred on
its merits; they are deferred on the branch.
