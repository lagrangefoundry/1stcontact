## Notes for the Editor

**Nothing here blocks the level.** Zero violations and zero needs_review — the three
warnings are wording, not drift in what the matrix demands, and may be taken
opportunistically.

Two cross-cutting observations for whoever picks them up:

1. **Findings 1 and 2 are one edit, not two.** Both live in AC-705's treatment of form
   controls, and both stem from the same slip: describing the exclusion in terms of *who
   owns the control's box* rather than in terms of *which leaves enter the two pairing
   queues*. Fixing one clause without the other leaves the AC half-corrected. Read
   `probes.ts:318-331` (slot pushed as a leaf, not recursed into; control pushed with its
   own box), `:543-550` (oracle classifier excludes `control`/`unknown`/`empty`) and
   `:678-685` (reproduced queue admits only non-synthesized `image`/`box`) together before
   editing — the three sites are what make the AC's operative rule true.

2. **`uat_coverage` is not an ac-level lever, but it is worth flagging what the next level
   inherits.** Eleven of STORY-84's 26 ACs currently read `uat_coverage: fail` (AC-689,
   AC-691, AC-694, AC-1345, AC-1346, AC-1347, AC-1348, AC-1349, AC-1350, AC-1351,
   AC-1352) — the whole REQ-88 cohort plus three older ones. STORY-86's 16 ACs all read
   `pass`. Attempt 9's own summary also predicted three newly-opened UAT gaps (AC-736's
   captured-surface corollary; the third envelope violation across AC-710/706/707; AC-731's
   self-painting-run chain). The uat-level cycle should expect the STORY-84 side to carry
   most of the work.
