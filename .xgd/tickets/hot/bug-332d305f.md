# values-diff: ten of 64 deltas on www.hearingzone510.com compare a stacking level, a no-op clip and a text node that the two sides do not have in common

Round: `repro-console:repro-www-hearingzone510-com#1` · bundle
`storage/references/www.hearingzone510.com/index` · evidence `storage/tmp/repro-console/repro-www-hearingzone510-com/iteration-1/diff/`.
One bundle. Companion gap ticket: REQ-370.

## Item 1 — `zIndex` compares a raw level across different stacking structures · `instrument-asymmetric`
Six HIGH deltas `zIndex` on "(generic)" expected `z:13` actual `z:0` (3050.93 each). The six are
expected-manifest elements 19, 39, 44, 56, 73, 92 — Zyro's `.block-background{z-index:13;…}`
(mirrored `assets/_..Bf-H_B6P.css`), one per section, each the ground of its own section's stacking
context. The fold deliberately writes no level on a backdrop (`fold.ts` ~4053-4064: "A BACKDROP'S LAYER
IS THE FOLD'S DECISION, NOT THE CAPTURE'S … The level a backdrop paints at is already stated, by where
the fold puts it"). So the reproduction is right by its own contract and the comparator reports HIGH
because it compares absolute numbers rather than order among the elements that overlap. *Defended:* the
two sides express paint order by different procedures (z-index vs document order), so the delta is the
instrument's own. And it misses the one ordering defect that matters on this page (REQ-370 issue 1: the
hero photo above its 45% scrim), which it reports only as `z:2` vs `z:0` on the scrim.
**Proposed:** compare relative paint order of overlapping pairs (above/below), not raw `zIndex`.
**See:** `python3 -c "import json;print([d['expected'] for d in json.load(open('values-diff.json'))['deltas'] if d['property']=='zIndex'])"`
in the diff dir → **now** `['z:13'×6, 'z:2']`; **fixed:** only the scrim-vs-photo inversion remains.

## Item 2 — `clip-path: inset(0px)` reads as a mask · `instrument-blind`
Three MEDIUM deltas `mask` expected `present` actual `none` (2040 each) on expected elements 19, 39,
44, whose `maskEdge` is `inset(0px)` — a clip to the element's own box, which removes nothing. The
instrument reports a treatment that paints nothing. *Defended:* measured the wrong thing.
**Proposed:** normalise `inset(0…)` (all four zero, no round) to no mask on both sides.
**See:** `grep -c '"mask"' values-diff.json`-style count of `property=="mask"` → **now 3**, **fixed 0**.

## Item 3 — a bare text node beside a styled span is recorded with its parent's box · `instrument-asymmetric`
HIGH `renderedTextBox` on "F": expected `667×157`, actual `11×22`. raw.html:
`<p dir="auto" class="body-large">F<span style="color: rgb(0, 30, 66);">or over 20 years…</span></p>`.
expected-manifest element 13 is text `"F"` with box (43.05,1362,673.9×162) and renderedTextBox
667.2×157 — the whole paragraph; actual-manifest element 15 is `"F"` at 11.08×22 — the glyph. The fold
did the right thing (one run with styled sub-runs `[{text:'F',color:#1d1e20},{text:'or over…'}]`). The
reference side measures the text node via its parent element, the reproduction side via the renderer's
own span. *Defended:* different procedures per side. Also the reason my reference-matching in the
companion l1-gate bug missed 3 buried pairs.
**See:** expected-manifest element 13 vs actual-manifest element 15. **Fixed:** both sides measure the
text node's own range (or both the paragraph), so the delta disappears.
