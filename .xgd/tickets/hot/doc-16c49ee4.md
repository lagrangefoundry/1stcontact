## Prices and payment

Prices are shown on the website and include any taxes we are required to charge, unless we say otherwise. Delivery costs are shown before you pay. Payment is taken by card through Stripe when you place your order. If we have made an obvious mistake in a price, we will tell you and you can cancel the order for a full refund.
