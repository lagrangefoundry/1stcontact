Invoicing and payments (DOC-4 MVP item 4, DOC-5 Payments and Invoicing).

Scope: quotes, invoices, Stripe payment links, payment status tracking, subscription payments, invoice and payment history in the portal.

Stripe handles processing. The platform stores only the Stripe customer ID, invoice and payment IDs, payment status, invoice metadata and subscription status -- never card data.

The platform stops at the point where money is collected. Deliberately excluded: accounting, payroll, inventory, ERP, fulfillment.

Evidence of design intent: packages/site-schema/src/l1/schema.ts:1064 already names payments as an intended behavior-module slot consumer, so the L1 seam exists ahead of the implementation.

State rationale: aspiration rather than concept -- provider chosen and boundaries drawn, but no code exists and no date is set.
