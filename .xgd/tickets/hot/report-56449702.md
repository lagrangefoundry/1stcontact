# Cross-Capability Overlap Survey

**Anchor report**: report-31234d67
**Matrix surveyed**: the 3 live capabilities remaining after the 2026-08-05 structural
rebalance (8 prior capabilities carry `merged_into` and hold no current stories).
**Stories surveyed**: 12 (STORY-75 .. STORY-86)
**Clusters identified**: 7

## Matrix as surveyed

| Capability | UID | Stories |
|---|---|---|
| CAP-70 Framework Substrate: L1 Layout, Values & Behavior Modules | capability-ae9d65d6 | STORY-80, 81, 82, 83, 85 |
| CAP-71 L1 Reproduction Pipeline: Fold & Acceptance Gate | capability-2049c9ec | STORY-84, 86 |
| CAP-63 1c Capture & Diff Fidelity | capability-aa030c83 | STORY-75, 76, 77, 78, 79 |

All three boundaries are exercised. The dominant seam is CAP-63 <-> CAP-71 (the `1c`
toolchain both *produces* the pipeline's inputs and *duplicates* several of its
mechanisms), followed by CAP-70 <-> CAP-71 (the substrate defines the language the
fold emits and the gate's evaluator re-implements).

## Clusters

### Cluster 1: Per-viewport geometry keyframes — typed axis (substrate) vs emitter/evaluator (pipeline)
**Capabilities**: CAP-70 Framework Substrate (capability-ae9d65d6), CAP-71 L1 Reproduction Pipeline (capability-2049c9ec)
**Stories**:
- story-3569e1a4 (STORY-81): Responsive dials: length parameters vary per breakpoint and the nav collapse point is configurable
- story-8acc338d (STORY-84): Fold a multi-viewport capture into one L1 reproduction document with advisory structural hints
- story-24098299 (STORY-86): End-to-end 3-probe reproduction acceptance gate

**Overlap**: STORY-81 is filed under CAP-70, but after the REQ-84 pivot its entire
remaining in-scope statement is "per-viewport value variation via L1 geometry keyframes
with `interpolate|snap` segments" — and its own description sources that delivery from
the pipeline: "During capture, `foldToL1` folds the 6-width sampled ladder into a single
L1 document, emitting these per-viewport keyframes directly." The keyframe *axis* is
STORY-83 (CAP-70); the keyframe *emitter* is STORY-84 (CAP-71); the keyframe
*interval semantics* (half-open `[a.at, b.at)` resolution mirroring the renderer) are
re-specified in STORY-86 (CAP-71). STORY-81 could be read as belonging to either
capability, and the `interpolate|snap` contract is currently stated in three stories
across two capabilities with no single owner named.

### Cluster 2: Gradients — captured/diffed value (1c) vs authored L1 surface axis (substrate)
**Capabilities**: CAP-63 1c Capture & Diff Fidelity (capability-aa030c83), CAP-70 Framework Substrate (capability-ae9d65d6)
**Stories**:
- story-82eb6908 (STORY-76): Gradients as a first-class value: stop positions and panel surface gradients — captured, authored, and diffed
- story-d0a8cfad (STORY-83): L1 layout substrate rendered safe by construction
- story-c490f1cf (STORY-80): Absolute values re-homed in L1

**Overlap**: STORY-76 sits in the capture/diff capability but is explicitly titled
"captured, **authored**, and diffed" and carries an authoring third: "a standalone
`gradient` content-field value type whose direction and stops resolve, via the shared
surface-gradient resolver (`resolveSurfaceGradient`), to a panel/card
`background-image: linear-gradient(...)` surface fill". Authoring a surface fill is a
framework-substrate concern — STORY-83 already lists `surface gradient` (box) and
`gradient fill` (text) as L1 typed axes, and STORY-76's own note that "the gradient's
stop colours resolve literal-or-role identically to the value system's colour dial"
places its stop-colour rule inside STORY-80's absolute-or-overlay value system. The
capture and diff halves are unambiguously CAP-63; the authoring half could equally sit
in CAP-70.

### Cluster 3: `1c` CLI surface — hygiene story owns commands belonging to the pipeline capability
**Capabilities**: CAP-63 1c Capture & Diff Fidelity (capability-aa030c83), CAP-71 L1 Reproduction Pipeline (capability-2049c9ec)
**Stories**:
- story-e15a19ef (STORY-79): 1c CLI: flags parse correctly, propagate into sub-commands, and --json emits a clean scriptable document
- story-24098299 (STORY-86): End-to-end 3-probe reproduction acceptance gate
- story-8acc338d (STORY-84): Fold a multi-viewport capture into one L1 reproduction document

**Overlap**: STORY-79 is shared CLI infrastructure, not a diff-fidelity axis. Its ACs
name pipeline commands directly — the "Missing pages directory" suppression is
specified "by any command — including the commands that never render (`help`, `list`,
`repro`, `l1-gate`, `capture`, `values-diff`)". `l1-gate` is CAP-71's acceptance gate
and `repro` is the reproduction driver; only `capture`/`values-diff` are CAP-63's.
CAP-63's scope statement claims "the CLI surface that makes it scriptable", which would
make CAP-63 the owner of argument parsing and stdout hygiene for commands whose
behaviour it does not own. This is the classic shared-infrastructure-parked-in-one-consumer
pattern: it could belong to CAP-63, to CAP-71, or to a CLI capability of its own.

### Cluster 4: Element/occurrence pairing contract — specified independently in both capabilities
**Capabilities**: CAP-63 1c Capture & Diff Fidelity (capability-aa030c83), CAP-71 L1 Reproduction Pipeline (capability-2049c9ec)
**Stories**:
- story-d5de22a5 (STORY-75): Values-diff closes capture blind spots ... and duplicate-text pairing
- story-2c7069fe (STORY-78): Responsive-diff: cross-size N-way node analysis with change classifier
- story-24098299 (STORY-86): End-to-end 3-probe reproduction acceptance gate

**Overlap**: "Which two elements are compared" is specified three times across two
capabilities. STORY-75 (CAP-63) owns pairing repeated text by rendered position;
STORY-78 (CAP-63) aligns nodes "by join key (normalized text, or accessibility role for
text-free nodes) in document order, **mirroring `values-diff` pairing** — including
occurrence-by-occurrence alignment of repeated identical text"; STORY-86 (CAP-71)
independently specifies "the sample-fidelity **pairing contract** for text and non-text
leaves", with per-occurrence attribution so "drift on one occurrence of a repeated key
is still attributed to that occurrence". CAP-63's scope explicitly claims "the
element-pairing rules that decide which two elements are compared", yet CAP-71's gate
re-specifies the same rule for its own probe. Ambiguous whether the pairing contract is
one CAP-63-owned rule consumed by the gate, or two independent contracts.

### Cluster 5: Fidelity-verdict ownership — "0 value-diffs" gate vs 3-probe acceptance gate vs round-trip identity gate
**Capabilities**: CAP-63 1c Capture & Diff Fidelity (capability-aa030c83), CAP-71 L1 Reproduction Pipeline (capability-2049c9ec), CAP-70 Framework Substrate (capability-ae9d65d6)
**Stories**:
- story-24098299 (STORY-86): End-to-end 3-probe reproduction acceptance gate
- story-d5de22a5 (STORY-75): Values-diff closes capture blind spots ...
- story-d0a8cfad (STORY-83): L1 layout substrate rendered safe by construction

**Overlap**: Three separate "is this reproduction faithful?" verdicts live in three
capabilities. CAP-63's animating invariant is "**0 value-diffs <=> pixel-faithful** — a
clean diff must mean the reproduction genuinely renders like the reference" (STORY-75
closes its blind spots). CAP-71's STORY-86 probe 1 is "sample-fidelity against the
retained oracle at captured widths" — the same question asked of the same captured
ladder, but answered by a browser-free analytic evaluator instead of `values-diff`.
CAP-70's STORY-83 carries a third: the round-trip identity gate
`capture(render(L1)) ~= L1` on the authored axes, which is a fidelity verdict computed
with the CAP-63 capture tool but filed as a substrate property. A reader cannot tell
from the matrix which capability owns "the reproduction fidelity verdict", and a
regression in the shared notion of fidelity has three plausible homes.

### Cluster 6: Persisted viewport ladder / acceptance oracle — produced in one capability, definitionally load-bearing in another
**Capabilities**: CAP-63 1c Capture & Diff Fidelity (capability-aa030c83), CAP-71 L1 Reproduction Pipeline (capability-2049c9ec)
**Stories**:
- story-16f2793c (STORY-77): Size-aware diffing: compare a captured site at a chosen viewport across the persisted ladder
- story-8acc338d (STORY-84): Fold a multi-viewport capture into one L1 reproduction document
- story-24098299 (STORY-86): End-to-end 3-probe reproduction acceptance gate

**Overlap**: Lower confidence than clusters 1–5, flagged per the err-toward-flagging
rule. The persisted multi-viewport ladder is a single artifact whose *shape* is
specified on both sides of the boundary. STORY-77 (CAP-63) does more than read it — its
scope item 4 **adds** capture-side persistence ("per-viewport reference screenshots at
capture time"), i.e. it changes the artifact's contract. STORY-84 and STORY-86 (CAP-71)
define the same artifact as "the retained multi-state oracle" and "the raw ladder is
retained unchanged as the acceptance oracle the fold is gated against" — the oracle
*is* the ladder. CAP-71's out-of-scope note pushes "the `1c` capture/values-diff axes
the fold consumes" to CAP-63, which supports resolving this to CAP-63; but the
retention/immutability guarantee the gate depends on is currently stated only in
CAP-71's stories, so a change to capture-side persistence has no owner obliged to
protect it.

### Cluster 7: Reproduction treatments — authored L1 look (substrate) vs fold-reconstructed surfaces (pipeline)
**Capabilities**: CAP-70 Framework Substrate (capability-ae9d65d6), CAP-71 L1 Reproduction Pipeline (capability-2049c9ec)
**Stories**:
- story-46e3b3c7 (STORY-82): Reproduction treatments: card veil/border, placeholder & inline contact form, and footer copyright/colour overrides
- story-8acc338d (STORY-84): Fold a multi-viewport capture into one L1 reproduction document

**Overlap**: STORY-82 is filed under the substrate capability but is a *reproduction*
story by title and by origin (the gigabytealchemy reproduction), and the specific
treatment it owns — the translucent "frosted" card veil / band and its border — is the
same pixel concern STORY-84's fold independently reconstructs: "the capture composites a
card/panel/section fill *onto* each run rather than emitting a standalone box, so the
fold recovers it — ... every run whose surface differs from the band (or carries a
gradient the body cannot paint) gets a backing box". One story says the veil is authored
as L1 leaf axes (CAP-70), the other says it is reconstructed by the folder (CAP-71).
Both are true, but the matrix does not say which capability owns the card-surface
treatment's correctness, so a veil regression could be triaged to either.

## Not flagged (checked and dismissed)

  STORY-82 and the behavior-module contract in STORY-85 genuinely overlap, but both now
  live in CAP-70 after the rebalance. Intra-capability, out of scope for this survey.
- **STORY-84 / STORY-86 consuming `1c capture` output** — ordinary producer/consumer
  dependency, explicitly excluded by CAP-71's out-of-scope note. Only the
  contract-changing edge is flagged, as cluster 6.
- **STORY-80 / STORY-83 both describing L1 leaf literals** — same capability (CAP-70);
  a content-duplication question, not a boundary question.
- **The 8 merged-away capabilities** (CAP-64/65/66/67/68/69/72/73) — all carry
  `merged_into` pointing at one of the three survivors and hold no current stories. Their
  stale index entries are not overlap.
