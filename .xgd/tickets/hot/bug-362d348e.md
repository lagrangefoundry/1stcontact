## Symptom

First-Draft-Test Dina's Diner 3, desktop (1280px). The header reads as everything
on the left: logo (133×80), then Home, Menu, Catering and Get directions packed
against it, with the right two-thirds of the bar empty. The operator: "Header
layout is weird everything on the left, Menu font seems small".

## Cause

The builder built the header correctly: `site-header` is a row with
`distribution: between`, holding the logo and `header-right` (a row holding the
nav and the button). But the renderer gives `header-right` the section measure.

`withDefaults` (`packages/site-schema/src/l1/design.ts`, DOC-67 §4) gives every
group child of a document's root `width: content`, because "a top-level section
takes contentWidth". In a page, the root's children are sections. In a **part**
(header, footer), the root *is* the bar, and its children are the bar's items.
So `header-right` is drawn `width: min(1120px, 100% − gutters)` with auto
margins. It shrinks to fill the whole remaining row (963px), so `between` has
nothing to distribute, and the nav and button sit at its start.

The same default applies to the groups REQ-462's `arrangeHeader` creates
(`-start`, `-centre`, `-end`). `logo_left` looks right only because its end group
says `distribution: end`. In `logo_centred`, three grid cells each holding a
1120px-wide group overflow.

The links are set in `body` (18px). Beside an 80px logo and a 64px display role,
they read small. Nothing in the design says what the header's links are set in,
so every builder picks body.

This site was designed before REQ-462, so it has no `brand.header` and isn't
arranged at all.

## Fix

1. **A part's own groups aren't sections.** In a header or footer part, the
   root's group children don't take `contentWidth`. The part's root takes the
   measure (with the gutter, as BUG-276 pads it), and its items size to their
   content. Pages are unchanged.
2. **The brand names the links' role.** `brand.header.links` is a type role
   (default `body`). The seed sets it a step up (`lead`) when the header logo is
   72px or taller at desktop, so the links stay in proportion with the logo. The
   renderer sets the header nav's items in it unless an item names its own role.
3. **A site designed before REQ-462 gets an arrangement.** When `brand.header` is
   missing on a site that has a logo, the host seeds it the way REQ-462 seeds a
   fresh design (emblem → `logo_centred`, wordmark → `logo_left`) the next time
   the principal reads or writes the design. It reports what it seeded.
4. **The layout check sees a packed header.** At desktop, a header whose items
   all sit in its first half while the rest is empty is a fault ("the header's
   items are packed at its start; let the brand's arrangement place them").

## Test plan

`tests/test_UAT_FC_BUG-286_the_header_spreads_across_its_bar.*`:
- Dina 3's stored `header.json` renders at 1280 with the nav and Get directions
  ending at the measure's right edge. `header-right` isn't given the content
  measure.
- `logo_centred` renders three columns inside the measure at 1280 with no
  overflow. `logo_left` puts the end group at the right edge.
- `brand.header.links: lead` sets the nav links at the lead size. The seed for
  an 80px emblem logo is `lead`.
- A design with a logo and no `brand.header` gets one seeded, and the read says
  so.
- The packed-header check fires on the old render and is silent after the fix.
- A page's top-level sections still take `contentWidth`.
- Re-run the REQ-462, BUG-276, REQ-447 and REQ-456 UATs.
