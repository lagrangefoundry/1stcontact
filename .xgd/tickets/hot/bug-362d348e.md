## Symptom

First-Draft-Test Dina's Diner 3, desktop (1280px). The header reads as everything
on the left: logo (133×80), then Home, Menu, Catering and Get directions packed
against it, with the right two-thirds of the bar empty. The operator: "Header
layout is weird everything on the left, Menu font seems small".

## Cause

The builder built the header correctly: `site-header` is a row with
`distribution: between`, holding the logo and `header-right` (a row holding the
nav and the button). But the renderer gives `header-right` the section measure.

`withDefaults` (`packages/site-schema/src/l1/design.ts`, DOC-67 §4) gives every
group child of a document's root `width: content`, because "a top-level section
takes contentWidth". In a page, the root's children are sections. In a **part**
(header, footer), the root *is* the bar, and its children are the bar's items.
So `header-right` is drawn `width: min(1120px, 100% − gutters)` with auto
margins. It shrinks to fill the whole remaining row (963px), so `between` has
nothing to distribute, and the nav and button sit at its start.

The same default applies to the groups REQ-462's `arrangeHeader` creates
(`-start`, `-centre`, `-end`). `logo_left` looks right only because its end group
says `distribution: end`. In `logo_centred`, three grid cells each holding a
1120px-wide group overflow.

The links are set in `body` (18px). Beside an 80px logo and a 64px display role,
they read small. Nothing in the design says what the header's links are set in,
so every builder picks body.

This site was designed before REQ-462, so it has no `brand.header` and isn't
arranged at all.

## Fix (as implemented)

1. **A part's own groups aren't sections.** In a header or footer part, the
   root's group children no longer take `contentWidth`; they size to what they
   hold. The part's root is held to the measure by a new typed L1 primitive,
   `inset: 'content' | 'wide'` (box and container): its side padding is
   `max(gutter, (100% − measure) / 2)`, so its items line up with the page's
   sections while its own fill still runs edge to edge. The host sets
   `inset: 'content'` on a part root that says nothing of its width (no `width`,
   `inset`, `geometry` or `sizing.width`) when the design has a gutter — this
   replaces BUG-276's px gutter padding. A root that sizes itself (Dina 3's
   says `width: content`) keeps it. `inset` owns the left/right sides: the
   node's own side padding gives way to it. Pages are unchanged: a page's
   top-level sections still take `contentWidth`.
2. **The brand names the links' role.** `brand.header.links` is a type role
   (`body` when absent). The renderer sets every header nav's item in it unless
   the item names its own role. A links role the design doesn't define is a
   design issue (`brand.header.links`). The seed sets `links: 'lead'` when the
   header logo stands 72px or taller at a desktop (visible height; a logo sized
   by width is divided by its aspect) — `headerLinks()` / `seededHeader()` in
   `design-seed.ts`. A header the design already states is left as it is.
3. **A site designed before REQ-462 gets an arrangement.** When the principal
   reads the design (`read_design`), or after a successful `set_design`, the
   host seeds `brand.header` where the site shows a logo and the design sizes it
   (`brand.logo`) but has no `brand.header`: emblem → `logo_centred`, wordmark →
   `logo_left`, plus `links` as above and `location` when the positioning card
   is location-led. It answers `seeded: { "brand.header": …, why }`. A design
   with no `brand.logo` yet (never designed) is not touched. The plan surface's
   design description says so.
4. **The layout check sees a packed header.** New check `packed_header`: on a
   screen ≥1024px, a header whose shown logo, texts and buttons all end within
   its first half is a fault ("has its items packed at its start … Let the
   brand's arrangement place them (brand.header), and give the header's own
   groups no width of their own"). Thresholds in `layout-check.json`.

## Superseded assertions in earlier UATs

- BUG-276: the gutter padding is now `padding-left: max(var(--l1-gutter, 24px), …)`,
  not `padding-left: 24px`; a design written with a brand but no header is now
  given one on write.
- REQ-462 / REQ-456: a seeded header now carries `links: 'lead'` where the
  logo stands 72px or taller (Dina's 126px emblem; REQ-456's 240px wordmark at
  3.2:1 is 75px tall).
