## Story
**As a** site reproducer chasing pixel fidelity against a captured reference, **I want** a screenshot-to-screenshot perceptual diff that ranks and pre-crops the places my reproduction visibly diverges — plus a magnifying-glass crop of any image on disk — **so that** composition and geometry deltas the value-manifest diff cannot see (wrong rotation/scale/position, ellipse-vs-circle portraits, few-pixel offsets, stray mask artifacts) are surfaced mechanically, ranked by severity, and landed on directly rather than hunted by eye.

## Description
Provides two new CLI capabilities that complement the mechanical value-manifest diff (STORY-62) and the page-screenshot primitive (STORY-58):

- **`1c diff`** — renders → serves → shoots the site's draft (reusing the screenshot seam) *or* accepts a pre-shot PNG, crops both the reference and the reproduction to a common top-anchored rectangle, and diffs them per-pixel by max-channel difference. It emits a full-resolution heatmap, a block-averaged (de-noised) heatmap, a stdout summary (mean diff / 255, % pixels over threshold, horizontal band profile), and a severity-ranked `regions.json` of automatically-derived regions of interest — each with a zoomed ref / ours / diff crop triptych. Regions are derived, never hand-authored: the diff is block-averaged into a grid, thresholded, flood-filled into connected components, and each cluster scored by the sum of its block diffs (so a large-faint delta and a small-intense delta compete fairly), then ranked descending and capped at top-N. Exits non-zero when any region of interest remains.
- **`1c crop`** — extracts a box from an *existing* image on disk (a reference, a reproduction, or a diff) for close inspection, with the box clamped to image bounds. Deliberately distinct from the live screenshot command.

**In scope**: the two commands, their emitted artifacts, the region-derivation contract, human and `--json` output, and exit semantics.

**Out of scope**: value-level/computed-style comparison (STORY-62, the sibling value-diff); live screenshotting itself (STORY-58); any framework module or site-definition change — this is CLI/vision tooling only.

## Technical Context
- Belongs to CAP-52 (Reference Capture: Headless-Browser Vision). Sibling of the mechanical value-manifest diff (STORY-62) — the two share the render→compare→rank→report spine but read different inputs (pixels vs computed styles); they are intentionally separate commands, not one overloaded command with a flag.
- Reuses the page-screenshot render→serve→shoot seam (STORY-58) for the reproduction side; a pre-shot PNG short-circuits the browser for offline re-diff.
- Core diff and region derivation are pure and browser-free; image decode/encode/extract is delegated to the `sharp` image library, declared as a dependency of the generate tool.
- The value-diff records section-level anchor only, not per-element geometry (per DOC-19), so it is structurally blind to the composition/geometry deltas this command targets — this command was productized after that blindness let visibly-wrong reproductions pass the value-diff on the faelan import.

## Dependencies
None. (The sibling value-diff STORY-62 is complementary, not a prerequisite; the screenshot primitive STORY-58 pre-exists.)
