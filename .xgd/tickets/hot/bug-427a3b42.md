**This is a deliberate test ticket. No defect is being reported. Safe to close immediately.**

## Purpose

Verifying end-to-end that a consultation session can file a ticket against the product from inside the session.

## Background

In earlier turns of this session the ticket grant was read-only — `TicketQuery`, `TicketGet`, `TicketComments`, `TicketBacklinks`, `TicketHistory` and nothing else. Checked twice by pulling full tool definitions rather than from memory, and reported to the operator as a hard limitation both times. Real defects found during the session had to be handed over as prose for the operator to paste in manually.

The write capability (`ReportBug`, `RequestCapability`, `AddTicketDetail`) is present in this session's grant now. This ticket confirms it works.

## What to check when triaging

1. The ticket arrived, with title and body intact.

2. It landed as a draft in triage rather than scheduled.

3. It is attributed to an agent session rather than to a human reporter.

## One observation worth keeping

The grant appears to have changed mid-session without the session being restarted. If that is expected, good — it means a capability fix reaches a live consultation immediately. If it is not expected, it is worth knowing that a session can acquire tools partway through, because an agent that has already told its operator "I cannot do that" will not generally re-check unless prompted.

Close this ticket. Nothing here needs action.
