A developer on a fresh clone can populate the local store with a known cast of people and businesses — including the identity states that clicking cannot produce — and sign in as any of them through the real Access gate without a Cloudflare account. Development-only: it never seeds a privileged operator and never reaches a remote store unless explicitly asked. (REQ-192)
