## Symptom

First-Draft-Test Dina's Diner 4 (site_99aa2f2e…, tenant biz_782fff7f…). The Debug tab's turn-health
pane shows the alarm *"The last 1 turn of this business died without finishing. Something is
killing them; the customer is being told nothing."* while the turn was alive: the consultant's
turn opened at 23:55 UTC on 2026-10-25 and was still writing junction heartbeats every 5 s and
"still working" progress every minute at 00:34 — about 40 minutes in. Inside that one turn the
consultant ran builder round after builder round (first draft; Menu and Our story pages plus
header/footer fixes; tightening home; lining up Menu), and each round's result reached the client
as a cued message. The customer was being told things; the turn simply never closed.

Dina's Diner 3 shows the same alarm for a turn that really did die: the 21:20 layout-fix turn was
killed by the dev redeploy at 21:51. The alarm is right there, but its words read as an outage.

## Cause

`turnLost` (`apps/control-app/src/turn-log.ts`) judges a turn by **age**: any `turn_log` row with no
`ended_at` that started more than `TURN_LOST_AFTER_MS` (15 min) ago is `lost`. The constant's own
comment rests on "fifteen minutes is comfortably past the longest turn this product has taken".
Delegation broke that: the first draft alone is billed to the client as "about 15 minutes", and a
consultant turn that chains builders runs far longer.

The same ceiling drives BUG-249's watchdog, `sweepOpenTurns`, on the 10-minute cron: in production
it writes `outcome = lost`, `detail = watchdog: no close recorded within 15 min` into every live
long turn's row. The turn's real close later overwrites it (`UPDATE … WHERE turn_id = ?`), but until
then every reader — the Debug tab, `consecutiveLost`, the client's reload check — sees "Died".

## Fix

1. **A turn is dead by silence, not by age.** A turn that is still showing signs of life — junction
   heartbeat or progress — is open however long it has run. It is lost when it has been silent for
   a short window (a few minutes, well above the heartbeat and progress cadence), or when its
   junction is gone. The ledger row records the turn's last sign of life (or the reader asks the
   junction), so readers that hold no junction can still judge it.
2. **One judgement for every reader.** The Debug tab's state and `consecutiveLost`, and the
   watchdog sweep, all use the same silence rule; the sweep never closes a turn that is still
   showing signs of life.
3. **A death at a redeploy says so.** A turn that died at the moment the worker was replaced
   (its last sign of life at or just before a deploy/isolate restart) reads as ended by a restart,
   distinct from an unexplained death, so the alarm's "Something is killing them" is kept for the
   unexplained case.

UATs named `test_UAT_FC_BUG-288_*`:

## What changed (implementation)

- **Sign of life on the row.** `turn_log` gains `alive_at` and `deployed_at` (migration `0033`).
  The prompt route opens the row with `alive_at = started_at` and `deployed_at` = the running
  Worker version's timestamp (`[version_metadata]` binding `CF_VERSION_METADATA`, declared at top
  level and under `env.production` / `env.dev`). While the turn runs, the route stamps `alive_at`
  every `TURN_ALIVE_EVERY_MS` (60 s) from a timer in the isolate running it, cleared in the
  turn's `finally`. Rows from before the migration fall back to `started_at`.
- **One silence rule.** `turnLost` = unclosed and no sign of life for `TURN_SILENT_AFTER_MS`
  (5 min). `TURN_LOST_AFTER_MS` is gone. `turnState`/`turnHealth` (Debug tab, `consecutiveLost`)
  and `sweepOpenTurns` (cron watchdog) all use it; the sweep re-checks each candidate with
  `turnState` and only closes rows still open (`AND ended_at IS NULL`), so a turn still stamping
  its row is never closed however long it has run.
- **Restart is its own word.** New outcome `restarted`: an unclosed, silent turn whose last sign
  of life is within `RESTART_WINDOW_MS` (2 min either side) of a deploy NEWER than the version
  that opened it. Deploy times come from `turnDeploys` = distinct `deployed_at` across the
  ledger plus the running version's own. The sweep writes `restarted` for these. The Debug tab
  shows it as "Ended by a restart", counts it separately, and `consecutiveLost` skips it (neither
  breaks nor extends the run), so "Something is killing them" is kept for unexplained deaths.
  The client's reload notice (`sessionTurnFailure`) treats `restarted` like `lost`.

## Design decisions

- A timer in the isolate, not stream events, is the sign of life: a consultant parked in a long
  `Delegate` can yield nothing for minutes. A wedged-but-alive turn is still ended by BUG-249's
  progress deadline, which closes the row.
- Redeploys are recorded on the ledger rows themselves rather than in a new table; the
  current version's timestamp covers the deploy that killed the last turn before any new
  turn has run on it. A turn opened by the new version is never read as killed by it.
