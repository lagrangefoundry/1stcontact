## Symptom

The operator, on First-Draft-Test Dina's Diner: "Its a beautiful logo. It
captures so much, and it's squished up in the corner, barely legible. We have to
fix that. It is a systemic problem." It is the third site where the logo was too
small (Ariel's, Dina's first draft, this one). The cold read, done as a stranger,
said the same: "it's small in the header and very small in the footer. The brand
presence comes mostly from the photos." At 900px and 375px the logo is also flush
against the left edge.

## Cause

Every layer had a chance to get this right, and none of them owned it.
- **The consultant's brief set the size:** "the logo … about 56px". The builder
  placed a **93×56 box at every width**, a square-canvas logo whose visible
  lettering is smaller still.
- **The builder's own priming says something else** ("make the logo the header's
  dominant element: start at about 80px tall on desktop", BUG-267). A more
  specific brief overrides it.
- **The design has no `brand` section.** REQ-447 provides one, but the
  consultant's `set_design` left it out, and REQ-448's validation accepted the
  design without it. So nothing said how big the logo should be.
- **The layout check flagged it** ("header logo 56px, under the 64px minimum"),
  but the build was abandoned before anyone acted (BUG-275). The check's floor is
  also low for a logo meant to be the header's dominant element.
- **The footer logo** has no rule at all.

## Fix

**The logo's size is a property of the brand, decided once, applied everywhere,
and checked by what you can see.**
1. **`site.design.brand` is required** (REQ-448's validation refuses a design
   without it). It states the logo's **visible** size per width (header and
   footer), measured on the painted ink (BUG-267). The host **seeds it from the
   logo itself**:
   - its visible aspect: a wide wordmark is sized by width, a tall or square mark
     by height;
   - a starting size that makes it the dominant element of the header (desktop
     roughly 72–96px visible height, or 200–260px visible width for a wide mark;
     phone roughly 48–56px or 140–180px). Settle the exact numbers against real
     headers in this ticket.
2. **The renderer applies the brand size** to the header (and footer) logo by
   default. The header's height and padding follow the logo, not the other way
   round. The logo sits inside the page gutter (no flush edge).
3. **Briefs don't set logo pixels.** The consultant's priming and the `Delegate`
   description say the logo size comes from the design's brand. A brief that names
   a logo size in px is answered with a pointer to `brand`. A builder that types a
   size on the logo node is off-system and reported.
4. **The check holds it.**
   - The layout check's brand rule measures the visible ink against `brand`, and
     against the header: the logo is the header's largest element, and its visible
     height is at least a set share of the header's height.
   - The footer logo has its own minimum.
   - A shortfall holds the hand-back, like the other layout faults.

## What was built (BUG-276)

- **Schema.** `brand.logoHeight` is replaced by `brand.logo = { by: 'height' | 'width', header, footer }`.
  Each size is the logo's **visible** px along `by`: one px value or a per-width track. The
  renderer declares them on `:root` as `--l1-logo-header` and `--l1-logo-footer`.
- **The logo names the system.** An L1 image may say `logo: 'header' | 'footer'`. It then takes
  the brand size along `by`. The other axis follows the file, and the logo does not shrink in a
  row. A `logo` named on a design with no `brand.logo` is a dangling design reference, and the
  page write is refused.
- **The default.** On the site's header or footer part, the logo is the first image that names
  `logo`, else the first image. If it sets no sizing and no geometry, it takes `logo: <part>`
  (`resolveL1Design(…, { part })`, applied in assemble and in the site renderer's parts). The
  box equals the visible size because a placed picture is trimmed to its artwork (BUG-267). The
  check measures real ink, so an untrimmed file is still reported.
- **The gutter.** A header or footer part whose root holds its logo, links or text directly is
  padded by the design's `layout.gutter` at its sides, unless the root sets its own padding.
  Sections inside the root already take the measure and its gutter. This is what left Dina's
  logo flush at 900 and 375.
- **Required at `set_design`.** On a site whose header shows a logo, the host refuses a
  `set_design` whose resulting design has no `brand.logo` (`DESIGN_INVALID`). The refusal
  carries the brand the logo seeds. A design that is unsound for another reason gets the site's
  own validation reason first. A site with no logo has nothing for a brand to size, so it is not
  held to one.
- **The seed.** `read_design`'s seed gains `brand`, proposed from the logo's ink aspect. A mark
  at least 2:1 wide is sized by width: header 160→240px and footer 120→160px, phone→desktop.
  Any other mark is sized by height: header 52→84px and footer 40→56px. Numbers are in
  `design-seed.ts` (`LOGO_START`).
- **Briefs don't set logo pixels.** `Delegate` refuses a brief whose goal or checks give the
  logo a px size. The refusal is `logo_sized`; nothing runs, and it points at `brand.logo`.
  The `Delegate` goal's description says the same. The off-system census reports a size typed
  on the logo node on a new `brand` axis, so the page map, the critic's counts and eval-builder
  all see it.
- **The check (`brand`).** With a design brand, the header logo's visible ink along `by` must be
  at least `brandShare` (0.95) of the brand size. Without a brand, the floors rise to 72px tall /
  48px on a phone, or 200px / 140px wide for a wordmark. The logo must be the header's largest
  element (`logo_not_dominant`): no text's letters, button or other picture may cover more area
  than its drawn artwork. Its height must be at least `headerShare` (0.5) of the header's. It
  must sit `edgePx` (12) or more from the screen's edges (`flush_logo`). The footer logo has a
  minimum (`small_footer_logo`): the brand's footer size, else 40/32px tall or 120/96px wide.
  Every new kind holds the hand-back, like every other layout fault.
- **Priming.** The consultant's design section says the logo's size is the brand's and never
  goes in a brief. The builder's header guidance says to name `logo: "header"` / `"footer"`,
  give it no size, and keep it in the gutter. The 80/52px starting sizes remain only for a site
  with no brand.
