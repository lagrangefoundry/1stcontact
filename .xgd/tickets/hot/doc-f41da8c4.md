## Subscriptions and services

If you buy a subscription, membership or other service from us:

- **The price, how often you will be charged, and how to cancel are shown before you pay.** A subscription renews automatically at the end of each term until you cancel. If the price is going to change, we will tell you before the change applies to you.
- **You can cancel at any time** by contacting us at {{contact_email}}. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep what you have paid for until the end of the current term.
- **If you are a consumer in the UK or the EU**, you can also cancel within 14 days of buying, without giving a reason. If you asked for the service to start during those 14 days, we refund the price less the part you have already received.
- Your other legal rights as a consumer are not affected.
