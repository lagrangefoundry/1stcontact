corpus, that instruction points at documents the AI cannot open, and the entry
must be rewritten to name the new set in the same edit.

## Purpose

Decide the composition of the **system knowledge base** — the corpus the builder
chat AI searches when advising a client. Specifically: which existing `doc`
tickets should carry `doc_kind: system_kb`, which contain relevant material but
are written for the wrong reader, and which system-KB documents need authoring
from scratch.

Driven by [[DOC-39]] §3.5 / §10, which deliberately deferred "which system-KB
documents get written" as an authoring exercise to run once the machinery was in
place. The machinery is in place.

## Findings — the KB is connected

- Membership is `fields.doc_kind == 'system_kb'` on a `doc` ticket
  (`tools/generate/src/cli/kb.ts`, `MEMBER_KIND`). Single-valued and exclusive
  with `architecture` by design ([[DOC-39]] §3.1).
- `1c kb build` exports members to `kb/system/*.md`, indexes them, and writes
  `awareness.md`. `1c assets` inlines the result into
  `apps/control-app/src/generated/kb.js`, which ships in the Worker bundle.
- The builder session searches **both** KBs — `SESSION_KBS = [PROJECT_KB,
  SYSTEM_KB]` (`apps/control-app/src/session-knowledge.ts`).
- Current corpus: four authored documents (DOC-17, DOC-31, DOC-33, DOC-35) plus
  three generated projections (REF-l1, REF-surface, REF-behaviors).

**Defect found:** `kb/system/awareness.md` is stale — dated 2026-08-31, describes
"4 documents in 2 territories", and does not mention the REF projections at all,
though all seven are in the index manifest. The awareness map is what primes the
AI on *what kinds of knowledge exist*, so the assistant is currently unaware that
the tool manual, the L1 vocabulary and the behavior catalogue are searchable.

## Requirements the corpus must cover

1. What 1st Contact is, what it sells, and where the front office stops.
2. The differentiation: no templates, not four minutes, consultation-led.
3. How to run the consultation (covered — DOC-33).
4. How to build a page in L1 — craft, not vocabulary.
5. How to wield the tool surface — practice, not the manual.
6. Product behaviour a client will ask about: drafts, publishing, addresses,
   what they can edit themselves.
7. **What actually exists today vs. the aspirational product** — a living
   beta-reality document, so the AI does not promise unbuilt capabilities.
