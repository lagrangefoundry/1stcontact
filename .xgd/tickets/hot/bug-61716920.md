## Source

The consultant's own reports from production: PLATFORM_ISSUE-1, -4, -6 (part 3), -13, and the 2026-11-09 conversation where the host re-sent the same two crop findings five turns in a row while the consultant was waiting on the client's answer. In each case the host's layout check (the findings appended after a builder writes a page) reports something that isn't wrong, or that no builder round can change, and then tells the consultant "A page with layout faults is not done. Fix them now", turn after turn.

## False findings

1. **Generated style text measured as content (PLATFORM_ISSUE-13).** A list or grid component writes an inline `<style>` block inside its section. The spacing check measured from a heading to that block's *text* (".latest-grid-list-l1-0 { display: gri…") and reported a 196–334px gap where the drawn gap is about 20px. The check must measure painted elements only: `<style>`, `<script>`, `<template>` and other non-rendered nodes are never measurement targets.
2. **A drawing's own lettering checked as page text (PLATFORM_ISSUE-1).** An inline SVG wordmark (`parts: true`) had its glyphs reported as overlapping text, text cut off by its box, and sizes off the type scale. Text inside a picture or drawing is never checked against page type rules.
3. **Right-aligned content called off-grid (PLATFORM_ISSUE-1).** A content-width section whose row puts one small item at the end (`distribution: end`) was reported as "content from 1165px to 1240px, off this site's grid", although it sits on the column's right edge. Alignment to either edge of the measure is on the grid.
4. **Business name taken from the wrong element (PLATFORM_ISSUE-4).** The header check named an 18px button ("Text a photo") as the business name when the name is the 44px `h2`. The business name is found from the site's own name (`application-name`, REQ-422) or the logo's text alternative, not from whatever text sits first.
5. **A crop warning that ignores a stated subject (PLATFORM_ISSUE-6).** The "cropped to its frame … nothing saying where its subject is" finding fires even when the picture (or its slideshow) has an `objectPosition`, which is exactly the statement it asks for.
6. **A persistent 24px inset nobody can find (PLATFORM_ISSUE-4).** On three pages of one site, every section's content started 24px inside the measure at every width, even after builders set `width: content` and removed all padding and inset on the sections and their stacks. `get_l1`, `describe_page` and `get_page_style` showed no source. Find out whether the render really insets the content (then fix the render) or the check measures wrongly (then fix the check). Either way the source must be visible through the consultant's tools.

## Nagging

7. **No repeat while nothing has changed.** When the draft hasn't changed since the findings were last sent (no new changes), the host doesn't send the same findings again.
8. **No repeat while a finding waits on the client.** When the consultant has asked the client a question about a finding (for example "should I rebuild the hero shorter?"), the finding is held until the client answers. It isn't re-sent each turn, which made the consultant repeat the question to the client.
9. **A finding marked "unchanged after a fix round"** isn't sent with "Fix them now" again. It's reported once as something to tell the client about or leave, as the host already says.
10. **Kept findings stay kept** (the `layout_kept` decision, PLATFORM_ISSUE-1 part 3): a finding matching a recorded keep is never re-sent until the page changes in a way that changes the finding.

## Test plan

UATs `test_UAT_FC_BUG-<n>_*`, through the real layout check on rendered fixtures:
- a section with a heading followed by a component that emits an inline `<style>` reports no spacing finding;
- a page with an inline SVG wordmark reports no text findings for the wordmark's glyphs;
- a right-aligned single item in a content-width row reports no off-grid finding;
- a header with a large name heading and a smaller button names the heading as the business name;
- a cropped picture with `objectPosition` gets no "nothing saying where its subject is" finding;
- after findings are sent once, a turn with no new draft change and a turn where the consultant's last message asked the client about the finding re-send nothing;
- the 24px-inset fixture (sections with `width: content` and no padding) draws its content at the measure's edge, and the check agrees.


## Added 2026-11-09: check the widths where phone faults hide

The layout check renders each page at 1280, 900 and 375px only. On 2026-11-09 three phone faults reached the operator before the consultant: a hero title collapsed to body size (BUG-401), a spam-trap field showed (BUG-402), and the header was crowded. All of them showed **below the site's first per-width value (375px)** or **between the checked widths**, which is where the builder's Fit pane and real phones (320, 360, 390, 414…) actually land. The site's own width ladder starts at 320, but no check ever looked there.

11. **The check also renders each page at the site's smallest ladder width** (320 on the starter ladder), or 320 when the ladder has nothing narrower, and reports findings there like any other width.
12. **And at one width between phone and tablet** (for example the midpoint of the ladder's phone and tablet widths, about 560px), where interpolated values and wrapping change most.
13. **Findings name the width as the width control does** (Phone, Tablet, Desktop, or the pixel width for the extra two, see BUG-404), and they count towards the same "not done" rules, no-repeat rules (items 7–10) and `layout_kept` keeps as the others.
14. **The cost stays bounded:** the two extra widths are rendered in the same browser session as the existing three, and only for pages the build changed.

Extra UATs: a fixture page with a heading whose per-width size starts at 375 gets a finding at 320 (text far smaller than its role) when the renderer reverts that size, and none when it's correct; a fixture whose element overflows only at about 560px gets a finding at that width; the check's widths for a page with the starter ladder are exactly 320, about 560, 375, 900 and 1280.

## What was built (2026-11-09)

**False findings (items 1–6).**
1. The in-page check never measures or quotes what is not painted: `<style>`, `<script>`, `<template>`, `<noscript>` are not shown, have no own text, and their text is left out of every quote (a finding about a list block quotes "Print 1…", never ".latest-grid-list-l1-0 { display: grid…").
2. Lettering inside an inline SVG is part of the drawing: it is not page text for the overlap, clipped or type-scale checks.
3. With a design, a section's content is on the grid when it meets **either** edge of the content or wide measure. A block touching neither edge is still reported. A section drawn as a box (an overlay's picture) is measured by its box, not by the padding inside it.
4. The business name is text that carries every word of the site's own name (`application-name`), or is made only of its words (a name set in two runs). It is never a button's label. "Text a photo" no longer counts as "Photo Repair Studio".
5. A stated `objectPosition` on a picture, or on a slideshow (for every slide's photo and clip), is the subject's place. With no focal point on the material, the crop is not judged (no "nothing saying where its subject is", no "subject near the edge").
6. **The inset was the render.** A `width: content` (or `wide`) box inside a box already held to a measure took the gutter off both sides a second time: `min(measure, 100% − 2·gutter)` of a parent that was already the measure. Every section a builder wrapped, or set to `content` inside a content-width wrapper, drew its content one gutter inside the measure at every width, and no setting showed it. Inside a measure the renderer now emits `min(measure, 100%)`. The check agrees.

**Nagging (items 7–10).**
- The host remembers the findings it has said, across messages and jobs, on the site plan (`layout_said`: each finding's identity and the line it was said in, newest 300). A finding said before and unmoved since (same identity, every measure within 2px) is said once more as "unchanged by a fix round" and not held. After that it is not listed at all. The look says "home: N findings already reported and unchanged since, not repeated." A finding that moves, or disappears and comes back, is new.
- A reply whose last paragraph asks the client a question is not held for layout faults: they wait on the client's answer. Nor are the review, the promise or the stranded-round cues run on that reply. The host's own italic status lines after the reply are ignored.
- The layout cue is not sent again with the same lines when nothing has been built since it was sent.
- Kept findings (`layout_kept`) are still filtered before anything is said (BUG-313), unchanged.

**Widths (items 11–14).**
- Each page is checked at desktop, tablet and phone, then at the midpoint of the ladder's phone and tablet widths (572px on the starter ladder: (375 + 768) / 2), then at the ladder's smallest width (320, or 320 when the ladder has nothing narrower than the phone width). The two extras are named by their pixels (`home, 320px: …`). They use the thresholds of the nearest smaller named width. They count towards the same hold, no-repeat and keep rules.
- New finding `shrunk_heading` (in `type_scale`): an `h1`, `h2` or `h3` drawn under 60% (`headingShare`) of its role's size at that width (display/h1, h2, h3). This is how a title that lost its per-width size below 375 shows up at 320.
- Cost: the extras run only where the check already runs (pages the build wrote), through the same driver factory as the other three widths, one navigation per width. In production that factory shares the one browser.

## Design decisions made during implementation
- Item 6 was a render fault, not a check fault, so the render was fixed. An overlay composition's own gutter padding is deliberate (text kept off the picture's edge), so the check now measures an overlay by its drawn box instead.
- "Asked the client" is detected from the reply's last paragraph holding a question. The finding's text is not matched against the question.
- The extra widths surfaced real faults in existing fixtures at 320px: a header too narrow for its row, and a wide emblem squeezed below its brand size. Those UATs now assert their original intent at the named widths only.
