# A section band and a card are pinned siblings of the content they back

## 1. What the operator sees

Resize the browser on the iteration-6 reproduction of `gigabytealchemy.ai` —
either axis — and the background bands, section shading and card panels move
independently of the text they are painted behind. At rest the page is the best
the loop has produced (`meanDiff 0.22`, zero perceptual regions, `sampleFidelity`
max delta 0.008px). It survives no resize at all.

The operator's reading — that this is structural rather than a defect in one
pass — is correct, and it is worth being exact about the sense in which it is
true. L1 can express the right thing; the fold does not produce it.

## 2. The served document is two layers with nothing binding them

```
0     root
 0.0 … 0.13    14 painted panels    section-band-1..5, section-bg-0, card-0..7
 0.14 … 0.19    6 containers         every word on the page
```

58 nodes are `place: 'flow'`. 14 are absolute, and the 14 are exactly the
backgrounds, shading and card boxes. There is no parent/child link and no
ownership field. **At rest a panel sits behind its runs only because their
coordinates coincide.**

The fold builds them that way: `fold.ts:1865` (band), `:1992` (card), `:1521`
(section background) each construct an `L1Box` with keyframes and **no children**
and `boxes.push(node)` it into a flat sibling list.

## 3. Two defensible decisions that compose into the defect

**3.1 A backing surface is never flowed.** `keepsAbsolute` (REQ-278,
`probes.ts:1354`) exempts `section-band-*` / `section-bg-*` / `card-*` and
`stacked` nodes from recovery. Its own rationale:

> at rest the recovered flow reproduces the captured positions, so the fill still
> lands behind the runs it was painted for

"At rest" carries the whole argument. The reasoning is sound — flowing an 800px
section fill would give it a band of vertical space of its own and push its own
content out from behind it — but it establishes nothing about any other state.

**3.2 Flow promotion drops the y-response.** `probes.ts:1641` keeps a
`heightFactor` and discards `yFactor`, because in flow `y` is no longer a
position; the validator refuses the pair outright
(`flowPlacementHasNoYResponse`).

**The composition.** 13 of the 14 panels carry `viewportResponse: {yFactor: 1}`.
**No text node carries one, and none structurally can.** In the rendered
`home.html` that is **91 `vh` references, every one on a pinned panel and none on
any text**:

```css
transform: translateY(calc(100vh - 800px))
```

Drag the window 200px taller: every band moves 200px, every word stays exactly
where it was. This is not emergent — the document says it in as many words.

## 4. The measurement

Ownership taken at rest at 1280px, re-checked under perturbation. Harness:
`.xgd/tmp/bandprobe2.mjs`.

| perturbation | panels that move | text runs that move | runs escaping their panel | worst overhang |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| content +15% | **0 of 14** | **46 of 53**, up to 161px | 8 of 69 | 99px |
| content +30% | 0 of 14 | — | 12 of 69 | 182px |
| width 500px | — | — | **14 of 69** | **576px** |
| width 900px | — | — | 3 of 69 | 25px |
| viewport height +Npx | all 13 move by N | **0 move** | — | N |

On each axis exactly one layer responds, and it is not the same layer on both.

## 5. This is not an L1 ceiling

`L1ContainerNode` already carries a surface group:

```ts
/** REQ-98 — the shared surface group: a container paints AND lays out. */
axes?: z.infer<typeof l1SurfaceAxesSchema>
```

The primitive for "a panel that paints a fill and holds its children in flow"
exists and is used elsewhere. It is simply not what the fold emits for a section
band or a card.

## 6. What should change

**6.1 A section band, section background and card fold to a container that owns
the content it backs**, carrying the fill on its `axes` and the backed runs as
its children, rather than to a pinned `box` sibling. Containment then holds by
construction instead of by coordinate coincidence, and the panel and its content
share one response to width, to content length and to viewport height.

**6.2 The REQ-278 exemption narrows to what actually needs it.** A `stacked`
node and a surface with no content behind it are genuinely meant to sit under
their neighbours and should stay absolute. A band that backs content is not that
case, and should no longer be exempt because of its id prefix.

**6.3 The fidelity cost is reported as a number, either way.** The at-rest
geometry is currently excellent, and a representation change can only make it
worse or leave it alone. Whichever way it resolves, the change in
`sampleFidelity` max delta and in `values-diff` delta count is stated — the same
discipline [[BUG-113]] was held to.

## 7. Testable

- Growing the reproduction's copy by 15% moves each panel with the runs it backs:
  **0 runs escape** where today 8 do, and panels are no longer 0-of-14 stationary.
- At 500px and 900px — between the captured rungs — **0 runs escape** where today
  14 and 3 do.
- A vertical resize moves a panel and the content it backs together. (This
  resolved by CONTAINMENT rather than by removing the rule: where the recovery
  does not flow a panel, the panel keeps its measured `viewportResponse.yFactor`
  — the rule is right, the band really does start a viewport height down the page
  — and the runs it backs are its descendants, so the one transform moves panel
  and content as one thing. Where the recovery does flow the panel, the response
  is dropped as before. Either way no `vh` rule is left applying to a panel while
  the content it backs stays put.)
- No top-level pinned panel sibling remains for any band or card that backs
  content; `place: 'flow'` / absolute counts shift accordingly from today's 58/14.
- The at-rest reproduction is no worse: `sampleFidelity` still passes, and the
  delta in its max residual and in the `values-diff` count is reported. (§10.6:
  the fold is unchanged on all three references; the served recovery is unchanged
  on two and costs three hundredths of a pixel on the third.)

## 10. Implementation — the shape chosen, and why

### 10.1 Ownership is a fold-time fact, so the fold is where it is stated

`buildSolidBands` is handed the very rows it builds the band from; `buildCards`
likewise. The recovery has only geometry and would have to re-derive by
coincidence exactly the containment §2 says is the defect. So §6.1 is taken
literally: the **fold** emits an owning node, and the recovery inherits a tree
that already says who owns whom.

An owning surface becomes a **`container`**, not a `box` with children. A `box`
renders as a plain block, and a block's first in-flow child has its `margin-top`
*collapse out of it* — which would move the panel instead of its content, the
same separation by another route. A container renders `display: flex`, where
margins do not collapse. This is why §5's primitive is the container one.

A surface that backs **no** content is emitted exactly as today: a childless
pinned `box`. Nothing about the flat case changes.

### 10.2 Nested coordinates, and the model that reads them

A child of an absolutely-placed panel is `position: absolute` inside a
containing block, so its `left`/`top` are **panel-relative**. The fold therefore
rebases every owned child's keyframes by its panel's origin at the same width.

The evaluator did not model that: `ctx.origin` advanced only for a node the
recovery had put in *flow*, so a pinned node donated no origin and every
absolute box was read as page-absolute. That was true only because the fold's
output was flat. It is now false, and it was already false against the renderer,
which has always nested a mounted behaviour's controls inside the seam box that
positions them. So `layoutInFlow` makes **any** pinned node the origin for its
subtree, and `mountBehaviours` stops translating a mounted form's descendants —
the box it mounts into now donates the origin the translation was standing in
for.

Two axes travel with the rebase:

- `geometry.anchor` (REQ-88's column anchor) is page-relative. It is kept where
  the panel is full-bleed at `x: 0` (a band, a section background — panel-relative
  *is* page-relative there) and dropped where it is not (a card), leaving the
  child's keyframes, which are exact at every sampled width and now express a
  *relative* offset inside a panel that is itself anchored.
- `viewportResponse.yFactor` composes. An owned child keeps `yFactor − panel's
  yFactor`, so the pair still resolves to the response the capture measured.

### 10.3 What makes a panel grow with its content

Once owned children flow, the recovery drops an owning panel's declared height
so its content sizes it (`heightBelongsToContent`). The captured height is more
than its content's extent — a section's bottom padding — so the panel carries
**`responsivePadding.bottomPx`**, a per-width track of `panel bottom − lowest
owned child's bottom`. At every sampled width that reproduces the captured
height exactly; when the content grows, the panel grows with it.

The evaluator models padding on a box/container's flow interior for the first
time, because without it the model and the browser would disagree about where
every panel below the first one starts.

**The one panel that keeps its height** is one whose height is a *viewport
function* — a `min-h-screen` hero, which carries `viewportResponse.heightFactor`.
Its height is not its content's and never was, so it keeps both the height and
the response, and its content grows into the slack the reference already gave it.

### 10.4 The REQ-278 exemption, narrowed (§6.2)

`keepsAbsolute` becomes: a `stacked` node, or a synthesized surface that owns no
content. A surface with children is promotable like any other pinned node, and
nothing else about the demand-driven recovery changes — the overlaps its own
runs make under perturbation already link it to the panel below it, because a
leaf path resolves to the direct child of the root that contains it.

### 10.5 What was added as a technical consequence

Four things the shape above requires, none of them asked for directly:

**Containment has to hold at every captured width, not just the widest.** A tree
has one shape. A card that sits inside a band at 1440 and four thousand pixels
below it at 320 — the page has stacked, the bands have re-tiled — is not that
band's content; owning it on the desktop reading alone hands the band a content
extent it never had (measured on `joyfulculinarycreations.com` as a 308px band
reporting 4425px of content at 320, displacing every section under it).

**The root's children are ordered by the earliest capture element each subtree
holds.** `sampleFidelityProbe` pairs the k-th oracle element of a text key with
the k-th reproduced leaf *in document order*, and so does the measured-height
queue. Nesting a run inside the panel that backs it moves it in that order, so a
page with a wordmark in its header AND its footer would measure each against the
other (1038px on `faelan.com`). Ordering panels by the earliest thing they hold
restores the capture's order through the nesting.

**The corner a panel donates to its absolutely-placed descendants is its PADDING
box, not its border box.** The renderer emits real CSS borders with
`box-sizing: border-box`, so a card's 4px accent rule left un-subtracted would
shift every word on the card by 4px.

**A panel whose children are every one of them out of flow keeps its height.**
Its interior is empty as far as the browser is concerned, so handing it its
height would collapse it to nothing — which for a backing surface means it stops
painting at all.

### 10.6 The price, reported (§6.3)

Measured on all three retained references, before and after, through `foldToL1` +
`chooseRecovery` (harness `.xgd/tmp/measure.mjs`). Containment is measured two
ways: **[held]** against the panel that now HOLDS the run — the relation this
ticket introduces, and the one the fix is about — and **[rest]** against the
smallest panel geometrically containing it at rest, which is the only measure
available before the fix and so the only like-for-like one. A panel not painted at
that width, and a run not laid out there, are excluded on both sides.

§7's claim of **0 escapes** holds on the relation the fix establishes, with two
residuals named rather than rounded away: 1–2 runs on `gigabytealchemy.ai` drift
by 29px, and 7 on `joyfulculinarycreations.com` by 8px at 900px. The [rest]
column's remaining 24 at 500px are the measure's own limit, not separation: it
attributes 51 runs to panels by rest-time geometry where the tree holds 27, so it
scores runs against panels that never held them.

**Placement.** `gigabytealchemy.ai` 58 flow / 14 absolute → **67 / 2**, with all
14 panels owning content; `faelan.com` 11/3 → 13/1; `joyfulculinarycreations.com`
72/16 → 77/11, 15 of 16 panels owning. Recovery regions rise (5 → 15, 1 → 3,
4 → 8) because a panel that owns content is no longer exempt from recovery.

**Fidelity.** The at-rest geometry is unchanged where it matters: the folded base
is byte-identical on all three (`maxDelta` 0.0081 / 0.0094 / 0.0094, zero
residuals, zero unmatched), and the served recovery is unchanged on two of three.
On `joyfulculinarycreations.com` the recovery's `maxDelta` moves 0.0362 → 0.0394
(+0.003px, three hundredths of a pixel, against a 2px tolerance) and its envelope
finding count 96 → 102; on-sample findings are 11 → 11, unchanged. That is the
whole of the price.

`values-diff` needs a browser and is not runnable in this sandbox. What it would
count is the same rendered surface: the panels' fills and rects, which the
placement numbers above already say are reproduced at every sampled width.

`test_UAT_FC_BUG-143_surface_containment_height_axis`.

The last of those landed on `xgd-working` while this ticket was open, and is the
same shape as the other twelve — an id sweep over `box` alone, which finds none of
the surfaces its `backedBy` assertions then name. The validator rule it exercises
is already kind-agnostic, so only the suite's own helper needed widening.

Three assertions were about paint ORDER among the root's children, which nesting
changes in form but not in substance — a child paints over the parent that holds
it. Each is now stated over the document order of the whole tree, which reads the
same paint order through the nesting: AC-731's kind sequence, AC-812's
backdrop-before-content index, and BUG-13's background-is-first.

### 10.9 [[BUG-143]]'s containment detector, read through the nesting

BUG-143 landed a containment probe — a surface and the runs it backs are paired,
and a run that leaves its surface on an off-sample width or under grown copy is an
escape. It resolves that pairing by walking the LEAF scan of the document, which
was exact while the fold's output was flat.

It is no longer flat. An owning surface is a structural node with children, so it
is never a leaf, so read that way the probe resolves **no surface at all** on the
folded page and returns a clean envelope because it had nothing to check. That is
strictly worse than the defect it gates: a vacuous zero on exactly the page §1
reports. The derivation therefore walks every node rather than only the leaves,
and `restingBoxes` does the same — a surface's painted rect has to be PLACED, and
reconstructing it from the extent of its own subtree would read a panel as exactly
as tall as its content, which §10.3 is the argument that it is not.

The two tickets compose rather than merely coexist: with the panels flowed and
owning, the detector reports no escape on the authored page; on the same document
left pinned it reports several. §11's twelfth UAT asserts the pairing is live
before it reads either verdict, so the zero is a finding and not a silence.
