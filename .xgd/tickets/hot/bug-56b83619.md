## What happened
A `builder` was briefed to (1) remove duplicate form controls, (2) turn the header section into a full-width dark bar, and (3) swap two images. Its scope was `home.json`. The brief said: "Do not change any words, colours or fonts except where stated."

The worker also changed the **page style's document background** from `cream` to `near-black`, and then recoloured text in two other sections to cream to compensate. That left 5 items with `broken` (contrast) attention. It did report the change honestly, and its own containment check came back failed with "Page background color was changed to near-black".

## Gaps this shows
1. **The account record left out the page-style change.** The `account.changed.differences` list showed element-level changes but no entry for the document background. Only the worker's own summary mentioned it. The host's record should include page-style changes (`set_page_style` / document background), because that is what the caller relies on.
2. **The host has no fixed wording for "page style unchanged" containment.** The documented forms cover pages, elements and fields. A check like "page home's style has no changes" could catch this kind of change.
3. **The worker made a site-wide paint change to get one section looking right.** It probably wanted the header to be dark, and painted the whole page instead of filling just the header box. Guidance for builders might say: give a section its own fill rather than changing the page background.

## Repro
Brief a builder to make the header section "a full-width near-black bar" on a page with a cream background, where the header box has no surface fill yet.



## Notes (EPIC-19, 2026-10-17)

- **Gap 1 is the real bug and is ours:** the account a delegation hands back must list page-style changes (document background, page fonts, viewport widths) as well as element changes. The caller trusts that record, and here it was silent about the one change that broke the page.
- **Gap 2 belongs with EPIC-24's scopes.** EPIC-24 already separates a page's frame (page-wide settings) from its sections, and gives the frame one owner at a time. A builder scoped to sections should be refused a page-style write outright, which is stronger than a containment check after the fact. Until then, a fixed check form "page <name>'s style is unchanged" is a reasonable stopgap.
- **Gap 3 is builder priming:** to colour one section, fill that section's own box; never change the page background to get one section right.
