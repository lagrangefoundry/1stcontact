## Goal

A consultant building a selling site's purchase pages (checkout, order page, order cancelled) gets them right **on its first build, with no help from the owner**. Anything a buyer depends on is guaranteed by the platform: the defaults, the checkout component, and the data. The consultant's guidance covers only taste. The defaults are modelled on the operator's finished reference pages (their test shop after a review round on 2026-11-09), reproduced generically, with no customer wording or data.

## Symptom

A site rebuilt its three purchase pages from scratch, using only the current guidance (DOC-47 "The purchase pages") and the component. The operator then had to correct every page:
- **Order page:** line photos at their original sizes (huge, mixed shapes, page wider than a phone). The money summary has no labels. The order number has no "Order" label.
- **Checkout:**
  - no photo of the item;
  - the price/total sits unlabelled under the region selector, left-aligned;
  - the region prompt is cut off on a phone;
  - no space is held for the payment form, so the page jumps;
  - the terms line is in the wrong place and in the browser's default typeface.
- **Order cancelled:** "Back to the shop" draws nothing; no way back to the checkout.

Some of these the consultant could not fix at all: the terms line's place, the cancelled page's Return to checkout, and the button style of Try again and Request cancellation.

## Root cause

1. **The consultant never starts from the defaults.**
   - `checkoutPagePreset` (`packages/framework/src/l2/checkout.ts`) already has labelled money rows, 72px square line photos, and a photo and price on the checkout. But it is drawn only when a site has no purchase page of its own (`checkoutTemplate`, `tools/generate/src/render/render.ts`).
   - `add_component` with no `presentation` leaves the component with no `page` slot ("Module 'checkout' has no slot 'page'").
   - So the consultant composes each page from DOC-47's field list, which names fields, not labels, sizes or arrangement.
2. **The terms line belongs to the component, not the page.**
   - `checkoutLegalLine` (`modules/checkout/component.ts`) is inserted by the component just *before* the payment control (`beforePayment`). It is not a slot node, so the page cannot move it.
   - It is added after the design is resolved, so its `small` role never gets the site's type: 16px, in the browser's default typeface.
3. **The cancelled page is not told which item was being bought**, so it can't offer Return to checkout. Its `back` field draws nothing.
4. **Component controls take no button style.** Try again and Request cancellation render unstyled ("font, size, padding").
5. **The reference and guidance contradict each other.** The component reference still lists the old `complete` mode and Continue settings. The component's slot sits off the page grid (96–1184px wide instead of 48–1232px).

## Fix

### A. Builds start from the defaults

Adding a `checkout` component (or a purchase page) with no presentation fills its `page` slot with that mode's default, resolved in the site's design. DOC-47 says to restyle the default, not rebuild it.

### B. One order-summary block, shared by the checkout and the order page

They show the same thing: what is being bought and what it costs. One default block, used on both pages:
- **Line rows:** one per line. Each has a square thumbnail (72px), the name (linking to the item's product page while it has one), and the amount right-aligned.
- **Money rows:** label on the left, amount right-aligned in the same column as the line amounts.
  - **Subtotal** (only when there is more than one line), **Shipping**, **Total**.
  - Total is emphasised.
- The block never makes the page wider than the screen at 375, 900 or 1280px.

### C. The checkout page, top to bottom

1. "← Back to <item>" link (as BUG-396).
2. Item description.
3. The order-summary block (B).
   - Before a region is chosen: Total equals the items' sum and is labelled **Total**; the Shipping row reads as not yet chosen ("choose a region below").
   - Choosing a region fills Shipping and updates Total.
4. Region selector, centred; its prompt fits uncut on a phone.
5. Payment form, with its space held (minimum height) so the page does not jump while Stripe loads.
6. **Terms line, directly under the payment form** (the operator's choice: it belongs to the act of paying).
   - In the site's own small type.
   - A page may place it itself with a `terms` field node.

### D. The order page

Heading, "Order <number>" (labelled), state, the order-summary block (B), delivery address, history, Request cancellation.

### E. Order cancelled

The cancelled page is given the item the buyer was buying. Its default draws:
- a primary **Return to checkout** for that item, while it is still available;
- a working **Back to the shop** link;
- room between those and the footer.

### F. Labels are the site's wording

Default **Shipping** on both pages (operator, 2026-11-09). Every label in the block (Subtotal, Shipping, Total, Order) is ordinary page wording the consultant may change, e.g. to "Delivery" for a British or Irish business. The checkout and the order page use the same wording. DOC-47 says the labels are the site's and the figures are the component's.

### G. The component and the reference

- Try again and Request cancellation take the site's button style (secondary).
- The slot sits on the page grid.
- The component reference drops `complete` and Continue.
- DOC-47 describes each page's default as above.

## Out of scope

- The consultant's delegation scope omitting a page's style file, so `remove_page` is refused. Not filed (operator to decide).
- Emails: BUG-414.

## Implementation (2026-11-09) — what landed

Not a duplicate. BUG-408 (free_coded) already gives a control inside a `button.*` box that component's type, and makes the layout check stop flagging the cancelled page's hidden parts. BUG-410 builds on it.

**A. Builds start from the defaults.** `add_component` with no `presentation` fills the `page` slot with `checkoutPagePreset(mode, design)` (`presets.ts` gains `checkout`; `editModuleAdd` hands the preset the site's design). Loaded back, the stored slot equals that default resolved in the site's design.
- A default names the `label` type role only where the design defines it, else `body`. A seeded design has no `label`, and a stored page may only name roles its design defines.
- The line photo's stored `src` is `#`, as in the catalog starters; the bound record supplies the picture.

**B. One order-summary block** (`orderSummary`, `l2/checkout.ts`) on the checkout and the order page:
- Line rows: 72px square thumbnail (`objectFit: cover`), the name (linked to the product page, else the shop), the amount on the right.
- Labelled money rows, figures right-aligned in the line amounts' column: Subtotal (on the order page; the one-item checkout omits it), Shipping, Total (`h3`, emphasised).

**C. The checkout default, top to bottom:**
1. Back link (the module's, as BUG-396).
2. "Checkout" heading, then the item's description.
3. The summary, bound to the item. Before a region is chosen, Shipping reads "Choose a region below" and Total is the item's price, labelled Total. Choosing a region fills Shipping and updates Total. The script puts the wording back whenever the quote has no shipping.
4. The "price changed", "sold" and dispatch notes.
5. The region choice, centred. Its prompt fits uncut at 375, 900 and 1280px.
6. The payment form's place. Once shown, it holds 480px (`PAYMENT_HOLD_PX`), so the page does not jump while Stripe loads. It holds nothing before a region is chosen, so there is no empty box. The place the module inserts into a site's own template holds the same.
7. The terms line, under the form, in the site's `small` type. A text node bound to the new `terms` field is the line wherever a page puts it: it keeps its role and look and takes the business's wording and links. A page without one gets the line directly under the payment form. The edit channel shows the node's placeholder; with no documents, it is hidden.

**D. Order page:** heading, the confirmations, "Order" and "Status" (labelled), the summary (B), delivery address, history, Request cancellation.

**E. Order cancelled:**
- A new `return` control (`<a>`, "Return to checkout", `returnLabel` config) in the design's primary look. It stays hidden until the page's script hears from the Worker's `cancel` which live item the order was for; it then points at `checkout/<slug>`.
- "Back to the shop" links to the shop, then to the item's page once known.
- The page root's bottom padding leaves room above the footer.

**F. Labels are the site's wording.** Shipping on both pages. Each label is an ordinary text node: reworded (e.g. "Delivery") it reads so, and the figure still fills in.

**G. The component and the reference:**
- **Buttons:** Try again (any checkout page, unless the page's own Continue lent its look) and Request cancellation (the default) are drawn as the design's `button.secondary`: its role, colours, border, radius and padding, written onto the control, since a control takes no `component`.
- **Page grid:** the platform's own purchase page (no section of its own) sits in the design's content measure. A default stored in a site's page carries no width of its own, because the section its seam sits in is already on the grid; a second measure took the gutter off both sides again (96–1184px of 1280, not 48–1232).
- **Preview:** with no item chosen (the builder's preview), the checkout draws the first item still for sale, not `items[0]`, which may be sold and have no price.
- **Reference:** behaviour meta gains `retired` (a setting or control) and `retiredValues` (enum values): still accepted on stored pages, never offered. `mode: complete`, `continueLabel` and `continue` are retired, so `list_behaviors` and the projected component reference no longer list them.
- **Guidance:** DOC-47's purchase-pages section is rewritten (restyle the default, labels are the site's, no `complete` or Continue). The `add_component` tool description gains a paragraph on purchase pages.

**Other tickets' UATs adjusted to this design:**
- REQ-643 and BUG-396 read the item from `lineName`/`lineAmount`.
- REQ-643's restyle check varies `border`, since Try again no longer paints `action`.
- REQ-666 expects the terms line under the payment form.
- The REQ-165/AC1639 reference-completeness checks skip retired settings.

The consultant's prompt measured 202,603 against 202,798 on clean xgd-working: already over its 191,200 budget before this change, and slightly smaller after it.
