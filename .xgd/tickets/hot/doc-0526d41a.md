# Joyful Culinary Creations: site upgrade analysis

**Subject:** Chef Sarah Joy's personal chef and postpartum meal business in Richmond, CA.
**Old (live):** https://joyfulculinarycreations.com/ (WordPress 7.1.3, Elementor Pro, LiteSpeed host, built by Jess).
**New (first draft):** https://joyful-culinary-creations.vercel.app/ (Next.js static export on Vercel, set to `noindex`).
**Captured:** 2026-10-25, every page on both sites (7 old URLs, 6 new), plus robots.txt, the sitemaps, the hero videos and sample images.
**Why this matters to us:** this is a real client who needed what 1stcontact sells and went elsewhere because we weren't ready. The draft shows what a competent human builder with AI tools delivers today. That is the bar to beat.

## 1. The client's own brief (her words)

> "Keeping the feel, but he knows more how to manage a website. Jess was amazing at creating one … but didn't know how to run it, so **we were hacked multiple times**, **the inquiries were not coming directly to me**, so I would miss them until she sent them to me a month later, we are going to **add more fields to contact, set up calls** etc."
> "He will use some of my photos and use **AI photo app** to help. He has professional tools to upgrade my photos."

None of her complaints are about design. All four are about operations: security, lead routing, lead qualification and booking. The builder kept the design familiar ("keeping the feel") and is being trusted to run the site afterwards.

## 2. Headline findings

1. **The live site is still compromised today.** Every one of the 7 old pages has a hidden SEO-spam link injected after the footer: a `<div style="position:absolute; left:-20914565266523px">` holding `<a href="https://slot-mahjong.joyfulculinarycreations.com/">`. The name is a gambling-spam subdomain of her own domain, but it **does not resolve**: Google DNS returns NXDOMAIN from her Namecheap nameservers. Either the attacker's subdomain was removed and the injected link wasn't, or it never existed. In both cases the injection in her WordPress footer is still live (re-confirmed with a cache-busted fetch on 2026-10-25), so the site is still compromised, and whoever can write that markup can write anything else. The "hacked multiple times" is not historical.
2. **The redesign keeps the content and replaces the operations layer.** About 90% of the copy carries over almost word for word, copy-edited. Every service, the $500 + groceries pricing, the testimonials, the 5-step process and both menus survive. What changed is the platform (WordPress plus about 19 plugins became a static site with a strict CSP), the form, structured data and the media.
3. **Video on the home page is now done by AI image-to-video.** The new hero has two silent looping clips made from stills: tahini pouring from a spoon (4.3 s) and hands sealing meal-prep containers (10 s). Both are 900×1200 H.264 at 24 fps, 70 KB and 630 KB. They are the "living photograph" idea in practice. See §5.
4. **The photos are AI-upgraded or AI-generated.** The chef portrait (`chef-plating.jpg`) is clearly Sarah's original 2021 iPhone 12 photo (`IMG_8708`), enhanced and outpainted: same pose, same plate, same mint. The fridge, postpartum tray, dinner party, cooking class and market images look like AI-generated scene photography. The old site had about 5 images per page, many low-res; the new one has about 21 on the home page, all consistent in style.
5. **Her actual asks are only partly met so far.**
   - *Inquiries direct to her:* the new form POSTs to the site's own `/api/contact/` endpoint, which presumably emails her. That is better than WPForms going into a WordPress inbox. But there is no storage, no confirmation she'll see, and no follow-up. If that email lands in spam, she is back to missing leads.
   - *More fields:* phone, city and a required six-way "I'm interested in" radio were added (personal chef / postpartum / private gathering / cooking class / gifting / other). Name, email and message were already there. However the client JS flattens the structured fields into the message text before sending, so they arrive as prose, not data.
   - *Set up calls:* **not done.** No Calendly or other scheduler. The copy still says "we schedule a complimentary 20-minute phone consultation" once the questionnaire arrives.
   - *Questionnaire:* still a **Google Form**, though a new one (a public `viewform` link rather than the old site's `/edit` link, which was itself a leak). The main intake therefore stays off-site and in a separate inbox.
   - *Spam:* a honeypot field (`company_website`) only. No Turnstile or captcha. The old site had reCAPTCHA.

Old URLs all carry WordPress `-2` suffixes (`/faq-2/`, `/our-services-2/`, `/meet-the-chef-2/`). The new site uses clean slugs. **There is no sign of 301 redirects being planned** (old → new), so existing Google rankings and Yelp/Instagram links would 404 at cutover. This needs checking before launch.

## 4. Platform, security and SEO

| Aspect | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Stack | WordPress 7.1.3, Elementor 4.3.5 + Pro, Essential Addons, BdThemes Element Pack, AIOSEO, MonsterInsights, Simple reCAPTCHA, WPForms, jQuery. About 19–28 scripts per page | Next.js static, about 11 chunks, no CMS at runtime |
| Attack surface | `xmlrpc.php` exposed, generator and plugin versions advertised, author handle "JSDADMIN" leaked via `twitter:data1` | No server-side CMS. Strict headers: CSP (`default-src 'self'`, `form-action 'self'`), HSTS, X-Frame-Options, nosniff, Permissions-Policy |
| Structured data | Generic AIOSEO `WebPage`/`Organization` | `FoodService` + `Person` + `PostalAddress` + 24 `Offer`s + `FAQPage` |
| robots.txt | Default WP | Explicitly **allows AI crawlers** (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended). A deliberate bet on AI search / answer engines |
| Analytics | GA4 (`G-NMF80EG812`) | None detected yet |
| Brand | Mauve `#89727a` theme colour, logo PNG | Cream `#F1ECDE` / forest-green buttons, Sofia Pro (Typekit), cream logo variant. Same "feel", modernised |
| Motion | None | Scroll-reveal via IntersectionObserver. Videos honour `prefers-reduced-motion`, `preload="none"`, fade in over a poster still |

The builder's security story rests on **having no server to hack**. That works for the brochure, but the contact endpoint and anything dynamic (bookings, intake) are where the next problems will appear.

## 5. "Live photos" from stills: tools

Yes, this is now a mature category. The new site's hero clips are exactly this: a still photo plus a short prompted camera or subject motion, exported as a silent loop. Options, roughly by fit:

- **Image-to-video generators** (subject motion: pouring, steam, hands moving): Google **Veo** (Gemini / Flow), **Runway** (Gen-4 class), **Kling**, **Luma Dream Machine / Ray** (has an explicit *loop* toggle), **Midjourney Video** (animate any image, with a loop option that matches first and last frames), **Pika**, **Adobe Firefly Video**, **Hailuo / MiniMax**. Typically 5–10 s clips at 720p–1080p. Seamless looping either comes built in (Luma, Midjourney) or by setting the end frame equal to the start frame.
- **Cinemagraph tools** (one region moves, the rest stays frozen, which is the truest "Harry Potter newspaper" effect): **Motionleap / Pixaloop** (Lightricks, phone app; draw motion paths and freeze masks), Plotaverse, Canva's animate effects. Cheap and controllable, best for steam, liquid and fabric.
- **Apple-native:** a real Live Photo exported as video, or "Loop"/"Bounce" in the Photos app. Free, but it needs the moment to have been captured live.

Treat these product names as a snapshot. The market moves monthly; check current pricing and terms before choosing one.

Practical recipe the draft follows: a 4–10 s, silent, `muted loop playsinline`, H.264 MP4 under about 1 MB, a poster still shown first, lazy-loaded, and skipped under reduced motion. That is directly reusable in our framework.

## 6. Lessons for 1stcontact

1. **The ops gaps are our opening.** Sarah did not leave her old site over design. She left over hacks, missed inquiries and no booking, and the human rebuild still only half-solves those. These map one-to-one onto our architecture: static output with no CMS to hack (DOC-5 #9–10), structured leads in CRM Lite with Turnstile (#17, #22–23), "Manage this inquiry" magic links (#24), and monitoring that checks the contact form actually delivers (#28). A form that emails and hopes for the best is exactly the failure she described.
2. **Booking a call is a first-class ask.** "Set up calls" came up unprompted from a small business owner. A consultation scheduler should be a standard form component, not an integration the owner has to find for themselves.
3. **Intake belongs on the platform.** Her real lead funnel is a long food questionnaire in Google Forms. A structured multi-step intake form (household size, restrictions, cadence, area), stored as lead fields rather than flattened prose, is a clear product feature.
4. **AI media is now table stakes for this tier.** A hobbyist builder delivered AI-enhanced portraits, AI scene photography and AI image-to-video hero loops in a first draft. We need (a) photo enhancement of the owner's own photos, (b) on-brand generated scenes, and (c) still-to-loop video as a typed L1 media primitive (poster, loop, muted, reduced-motion fallback, size cap), not raw `<video>` HTML (Security Policy §3).
5. **Clean up compromise at onboarding.** Any WordPress migration should scan for injected links and spam subdomains and report them to the owner. We found one here in minutes.
6. **Migration hygiene:** a 301 map from old to new URLs, keeping the GA property, carrying over the Yelp, Instagram and Facebook links, and re-verifying Google Search Console. The draft doesn't yet show these.
7. **Content carried over, then sharpened.** The builder's value was mostly editorial: one pricing section framed as a search question, concrete process facts (24–48 h reply, a trial dinner), fixed inconsistencies and typos, and FAQ schema. Our consultant should do the same in the first draft: keep the owner's voice, fix the contradictions, and surface facts that build trust.
8. **AI-crawler stance.** The builder explicitly opted her site *into* AI crawlers. We should have a default position on this and make it visible to the owner.

## 7. Open checks (not done here)

- No rendered screenshots were taken (headless Chromium can't launch in this sandbox), so the visual comparison here rests on the markup, assets and stills, not full-page renders.
- I did not POST to the new `/api/contact/`, to avoid sending Sarah a test email. Where it delivers (email provider, recipient, spam handling) is unverified.
- `slot-mahjong.joyfulculinarycreations.com` DNS: checked via Google DNS-over-HTTPS. **NXDOMAIN** (no A or CNAME record); the apex is 199.188.206.63 on Namecheap hosting. A "502" seen earlier came from the sandbox proxy failing to resolve the name, not from a live server.


## 8. Against our persona (DOC-35 §5.1)

Sarah is already our persona §5.1 ("Underperformer, supply-capped"), and an earlier capture of the old site is in `storage/references/joyfulculinarycreations.com/`. Checking DOC-35's diagnosis against what the human builder actually did:

- *"Only call to action is book a consultation, no lower rung"*: **partly addressed.** The new site adds a general contact form with an interest picker as a cheaper first step, a "Get started" in the header, and an "Email Sarah" in the footer. There is still nothing lighter, such as a sample menu download or a gift enquiry, and the heaviest step (the long Google Form questionnaire) is still the primary CTA.
- *"No local search presence"*: **addressed in markup.** `FoodService` + `PostalAddress` JSON-LD, an explicit service-area list (Oakland, East Bay, SF, Marin, Peninsula), and a title of "Personal Chef, SF & East Bay". There is still no Google Business Profile link or per-area landing pages.
- *"Testimonials carry credibility but sit low on mobile"*: **addressed.** A quote marquee sits mid-home-page, above "How it works", with short pull-quotes ("Sarah Joy is a WIZARD.").
- Not in our persona, but her top three in her own words: **hacks, inquiries not reaching her, booking calls.** These are operational, and our persona read missed them because a capture can't see them. The lesson for onboarding is to ask about operations, not only diagnose the page.
