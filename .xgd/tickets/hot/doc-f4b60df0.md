## Your right to cancel (UK consumers)

If you are a consumer in the UK, you can cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of the day you receive the goods. Tell us by email at {{contact_email}}. Send the goods back within 14 days of telling us; we will refund the price, including the standard delivery cost, within 14 days of receiving them back (or proof that you sent them). You pay the cost of returning the goods unless they are faulty. Some goods, such as personalised items or sealed items unsealed for hygiene reasons, cannot be cancelled. You also keep your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
