## Symptom

On Ariel's site (2026-10-23) all four critic reviews (REQ-427), C1 to C4, began by
saying they could not see the pages. For example:

> "Every desktop and tablet tile, phone tile 1 and all of the classes page reached
> me only as placeholders… Please resend…"

Each review judged only phone tiles 2 to 4 of the last page (y 667 to 2668). The
consultant was still held until it answered every finding, and each review cost
a full `claude_critic` (Opus 5.5) call.

## Cause

- `criticFor` (`tools/generate/src/cli/ai/host-core.ts` ~1612) sends one opening
  turn with up to 3 pages × 3 widths × 4 tiles = 36 images (about 15 to 30 in
  practice).
- The framework's Claude API backend calls `boundDialogue` before every request
  (`lagrange-framework/components/ai/js/src/backends/claude_api.js` ~470 and
  `api_tools.js` ~977–1026). With `DEFAULT_MAX_LIVE_IMAGES = 3` (`content.js:128`),
  it replaces every image except the newest three with a text pointer:
  `[image: image/png, N bytes, fp:…]`.
- The critic role sets no `max_live_images` (`roles.js` ~611 maps it to
  `maxLiveImages`), so the default of 3 applies. The newest three are the last
  page's phone tiles 2 to 4, which is exactly what every review could see.

## Fix

- The critic's session keeps **every image of its brief live**. Set the critic
  role's `max_live_images` to at least the most images `criticFor` can send.
  Derive it from the same settings (`CRITIC_PAGES` × widths × `max_tiles_per_call`)
  so the two can never drift apart, not as a separate literal. A one-turn session
  needs no age rule change.
- `criticFor` must not send more images than the critic keeps live. If a future
  change makes the count exceed the ceiling, it fails loudly in tests rather than
  silently blinding the review.
- Check the same ceiling for any other role that is handed pictures in bulk in
  one turn (for example the consultant's `screenshot` of `tiles: 'all'`), and
  note any other affected role in the ticket.

## Test plan

`tests/test_UAT_FC_BUG-258_the_critic_sees_every_picture.*`:

- With a recording backend standing in for the API, a review of three pages sends
  a request whose content holds every critic image as an image block and no
  `[image: …]` pointer.
- The live-image ceiling for the critic role equals or exceeds
  `CRITIC_PAGES × widths × max_tiles_per_call`.
- Re-run the REQ-427 critic UATs.

## What landed

- `critic-core.ts`: `CRITIC_MAX_IMAGES = CRITIC_PAGES × CRITIC_WIDTHS.length ×
  picture-tiles.json max_tiles_per_call` (36 today), derived, not a literal.
- `roles.ts` `criticRole`: `session: { max_live_images: CRITIC_MAX_IMAGES }`.
- `host-core.ts` `criticFor`: counts the image blocks of the brief and throws
  (`the critique holds N pictures; the critic keeps M live`) rather than send
  more than the ceiling, so a future overshoot fails the review loudly instead
  of blinding it.

## Other roles checked

The consultant and the builder hold `screenshot` (up to `max_tiles_per_call` = 4
tiles per call) and are **not affected**: the framework's ceiling
(`liveImages` in `api_tools.js`) counts only image blocks at the top level of a
message's content, never images nested inside a `tool_result`. A full 4-tile
screenshot reaches the consultant whole at the default ceiling; a UAT pins that.
Only a role handed pictures directly in a user turn (today: the critic) needs a
ceiling of its own.
