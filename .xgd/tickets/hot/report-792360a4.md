## Finding — the coverage caveat is real but MIS-ATTRIBUTED (recorded, not a FAIL trigger)

STORY-99 and STORY-101 both disclose that the `@gendevlabs/webui-*` evidence
does not run here. The disclosure's *substance* is accurate and prominent
(evidence unproven, skips loud and reported, nothing mocked). Its *cause* is
stated wrongly, in a way that understates the operational impact:

> "A fresh clone therefore has none of them… treat the component-mounting
> evidence as unverifiable until a private registry exists."

The components are **installed on this machine**:
`/Users/martin/lagrangefoundry/node_modules/@gendevlabs/{webui-shell,webui-split,webui-fields}`.
The main checkout `/Users/martin/lagrangefoundry/1stcontact` sits *under* that
directory and resolves them by ordinary upward resolution. The XGD worktree
this reconcile runs in — `~/.xgd/worktrees/…/reconcile-BUNDLE-16` — sits
outside it and structurally cannot.

So this is not "a fresh clone has none" and not something a private registry is
merely the *eventual* fix for: **the chosen consumption route can never reach
an XGD worktree**, which is the only environment in which XGD gates this work.
On a fully provisioned machine, the evidence passes in the operator's checkout
and skips in every workflow run.

I demonstrated the difference rather than asserting it. With the store placed
on `NODE_PATH`, `reconciliation-builder-workspace-origin` goes from **6
`NOT VERIFIED` halves to 11/11 passed with zero unverified** — newly proving
for real: AC-975 (frame-height measurement in a real browser, 0ms → 1086ms),
AC-964 (Worker verbatim forwarding over the chrome route), AC-961 (served bytes
byte-identical to the installed copy), AC-963 (import map derived from
`exports`), AC-977 (no-store on the chrome document and a served component),
and AC-978's installed-components tree.

Genuinely unverified in this worktree after that exercise: the 10 jsdom
component-mounting UATs in the chrome suite (AC-959, AC-960, AC-967, AC-968,
AC-969, AC-970, AC-971, AC-973, AC-974, AC-976), AC-1002, and the dialog halves
of AC-994, AC-1000, AC-1001, AC-1003. Those import the components as ESM
through Vite, which does not honour `NODE_PATH`, so they cannot be forced from
outside.

**Why this does not fail the review.** The UATs are real, unmocked,
well-targeted and skip loudly — they are *unrun*, not *defective*, which is the
class of defect Step 5b exists to catch. The stories do disclose the gap. The
remedy is out of reconciliation's reach (a private registry, or a Vite
resolution alias for the shared store) and a fix cycle cannot produce it.
**Recommended follow-up, as its own ticket against STORY-99**: either record the
worktree-topology cause accurately in both stories' Technical Context, or make
the store resolvable from XGD worktrees so the gating environment can actually
prove this story.
