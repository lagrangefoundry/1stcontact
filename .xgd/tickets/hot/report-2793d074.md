### What is good, stated explicitly

- **BUG-34** — `readGlyphFill` (`page-style.js:203`) conditions on `background-clip: text`, which is the correct discriminator between "a surface behind the copy" and "the paint that *is* the copy"; it reuses the existing `withAbsoluteUrls` and `isTransparent` helpers rather than inventing new ones; the three CSS declarations each fall back to the property's **initial** value (`none` / `border-box` / `currentColor`), so a run with no glyph fill computes byte-for-byte what it computed before. The transparent-foreground withhold is a general backstop, not a one-page patch, exactly as the ticket scoped it.
- **REQ-137** — `steps` is deleted outright: no reader accepts both shapes, no fallback, no mode detection (`CLAUDE.md` → "No Legacy Modes"). `grep` finds no `steps`/`step` survivor in `site-schema`, `generate`, `control-app` or `storage/` (the `steps` in `framework/src/modules/dials.ts` is the unrelated legacy spacing scale). `shadeHex` short-circuits `shade === 0` to the entry's own hex, so an unshaded reference is byte-identical *by construction*; the retrofit fits over that same exported function, so the drift it measures is the drift that paints. `SHADE_FIT_TOLERANCE` and `SHADE_SEARCH_STEPS` are named constants with their derivation documented, not magic numbers. The round loop carries a `rejects.length >= pending.length` progress guard that throws rather than spinning. Retrofitted palettes on disk match the ticket's claims exactly (`xgd` 7 entries, `gigabytealchemy` 15, zero steps).
- **Evidence validity** — the five suites contain **no** `vi.mock` / `vi.fn` / `vi.spyOn` / stub of any internal component. `test_UAT_FC_BUG-34_*` drives the real `cmdRender --edit` bytes, the real edit bridge and the real modal, opened by dispatching a click on the words, and candidly documents the one hop jsdom cannot compute (`var()` resolution) rather than skipping it silently. The 15 REQ-137 UATs cover all six ACs including the two that are easiest to skip — fixpoint re-runnability and the family-change refusal.
