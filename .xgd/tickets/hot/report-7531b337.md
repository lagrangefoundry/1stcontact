## Step 0 — Intent

**BUG-34** declares one defect and one backstop. The defect: a `gradientFill` run is painted by its own background clipped to the text with a transparent fill colour, and the copy modal read the two halves separately — `readTypography` copied `rgba(0,0,0,0)` into `--preview-color`, and `paintedBehind` starts one element past the run because its question is what sits *behind* the copy — so only the invisible half survived. The backstop, stated in the ticket's Fix section: when the resolved foreground paints nothing and no glyph fill was found, fall back to the chrome's own foreground rather than writing a transparent one. The ticket explicitly scopes this as general to the axis, not to the one page that exposed it.

**REQ-137** declares a model change split out of REQ-133 §2, revising REQ-114: an entry becomes one colour, `steps` is deleted with no legacy mode, and the position within a light↔dark family rides on the reference as a continuous Oklab `shade` on [-1, +1]. §3 records the operator-approved supersession: REQ-114 AC3's pixel-identical retrofit guarantee is replaced by a measured bound (≤8/255 on genuine ramp members, zero elsewhere), because seven of the 22 stored steps are more saturated than their base and a mix can only remove chroma — so they are not shades of anything and become their own exact entries. §4 declares the footprint: `packages/site-schema/src/l1/palette.ts`, `tools/generate/src/cli/colors.ts`, and the two stored sites. §6 records what implementation added beyond the original ACs: base-by-reach rather than base-by-lightness, round-based grouping, and the family-change refusal that makes the retrofit a fixpoint.

## Step 3b — Intent scope vs implementation footprint

STORY-97's AC-944 and AC-932, and STORY-80's AC-928, are the ACs that guarantee carried; items 2 and 3 modify them rather than adding parallel criteria. This is supersession, not lost work.

**Case 3 (undeclared reach).** Four existing suites were edited by `7a02616` — `req114-palette-model`, `reconciliation-colour-census-and-retrofit`, `reconciliation-colour-palette-overlay`, `reconciliation-beyond-l1-authoring` and `test_UAT_FC_REQ-130_beyond_l1`. These are test updates tracking the model change, which §4/§6 declare; they carry no independent behaviour and get no plan item of their own — their owning stories' ACs are the ones being modified in items 2 and 3.
