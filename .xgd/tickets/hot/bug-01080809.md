## Symptom (operator, 2026-11-05)

The client asked for the checkout's delivery prompt to be changed. The consultant answered: "The consultant is changing the checkout's delivery prompt to 'Select region for delivery' — about 20 minutes left." Twenty minutes to change one string. It actually took 2–3 minutes: the estimate was roughly 7× too high (operator: "20 mins is ridiculous - we need to get tighter on these estimates").

The consultant also chose its own wording. The client asked for "Please select a region for delivery". Since BUG-367 that wording is the framework default, so the right edit was simply to *remove* the site's `countryPrompt` override.

## Cause

1. **The consultant can't edit a component's settings itself.** In the delegating deployment (`primary_writes` false, REQ-343), the consultant holds no L1 write groups, `ManageComponents` (`configure_component`) included. So every change goes through `Delegate` → a builder pass, even a change to one non-visual setting. The settings in question are what a form's button says, a prompt, or where enquiries are sent.
2. **The estimate prices that pass as a construction pass.** `estimate-core.ts` costs a `section` pass at its p80 (4.6 min), adds the consultant's time, and adds `fix_rounds_per_pass` (1.73) layout fix rounds at 7.4 min each, then rounds up to the next 5 minutes. That comes to ~20 minutes for a change that can't move the layout.

## Fix

1. **The consultant changes a component's settings directly.** `configure_component` is granted to the consultant in every deployment, delegating or not, because it is the working half of a component, not construction. Its own description says how a component *looks* is page content (`set_l1`). It is applied immediately: the draft rebuilds the affected page and the consultant reports the change in the same reply. No `Delegate`, no builder pass, no estimate.
   - The consultant's method framing (REQ-343's "construction is commissioned, not performed") names this as the exception: settings are changed directly; layout and look are commissioned.
   - Clearing a setting so the framework default shows is one of its operations (`configure_component` with the setting removed), and the playbook says so. It uses this when the client's wording matches the default.
2. **When a pass is needed only for settings or text, the estimate doesn't add fix rounds.** A `Delegate` whose steps are text-only or settings-only (no layout change) is estimated without `fix_rounds_per_pass`. If the host's layout check does find something, the fix round is added then, as now. (Superseded in part by 4 below: no step carries fix rounds up front, layout or not.)
3. **The consultant uses the client's words.** When the client gives exact wording for a label or prompt, the consultant uses it verbatim. The playbook states this.

4. **Estimates are tight, not padded.** BUG-360 chose the slow end, rounded up, so the client wouldn't be disappointed. Here that produced a quote 7× the actual time, which spends trust as surely as being late does. The estimate becomes the measured **median** for that kind of step, not the 80th percentile.
   - **Steps are costed by their size:** a text- or settings-only step gets its own measured kind, separate from a section build.
   - **Fix rounds are added only when the layout check actually finds something,** never up front.
   - **Rounding follows the size:** to the next minute under 10 minutes, and to the next 5 only above that. A 2–3 minute job is quoted as "about 3 minutes".
   - **The time left is re-worked as each step finishes,** from what has actually elapsed, so a job that runs fast says so.
   - **The operator sees drift.** Each finished piece of work already reports its estimate against what it took (BUG-360's outcome). `1c estimates survey` now also reports, per kind, the share of jobs quoted at more than twice or under half their actual time. The target is that most jobs land inside that band, and a kind outside it is re-measured.

## Implementation (as landed)

- **Grant.** `instances.json` grants the consultant `configure_component` by name (`l1.operations`), beside its groups. `readOnlyGrant` (REQ-343's narrowing) removes write *groups* only, so the named operation survives in the delegating deployment and `ManageComponents`' other operations (`add_component`, `remove_component`, `hold_upgrade`) do not. The talker still withholds it (REQ-638).
- **Method framing.** `priming.json` `delegation-method-commissioning` ends: settings are changed directly with `configure_component`, at once, no builder and no estimate; a setting given as `null` goes back to the framework's default; layout and look are commissioned. Rule 8's "at the slow end and with the fixes its checks add" is replaced by "how long each kind of step takes".
- **Playbook.** DOC-75 "refinement" gains a bullet: settings changed directly; the client's exact wording used verbatim; wording that matches the default is a cleared setting (`null`), not a copy.
- **Estimates** (`estimate-core.ts`, `slow-tools.json`, `cadence-core.ts`): each kind (and each slow tool) is estimated at its measured **median**; `p80` and `fix_rounds_per_pass` stay in the table for reference only. New step kind `settings` ("a text- or settings-only change"), seeded at median 2.5 / p80 3 from the observed run until surveyed; `Delegate`'s `steps` description names it. `minutesFor` adds no fix rounds; a fix round is a step only when a pass is handed off as one (after a layout-check fault report). `roughly`: to the minute under 10 ("about a minute", "about 3 minutes"), to the next 5 at 10 and above. `StepQueue.progress()` re-works the time left: steps still to do are scaled by the job's pace so far (actual/estimated of finished steps, bounded 0.25x–2x), and a running step counts only what is left of it.
- **Survey** (`1c estimates survey`): a hand-off whose first declared step is `settings` is measured as that kind; it reads `estimate_outcomes` in the window and reports per kind the share of quotes over 2x, under 0.5x, and outside the band (JSON `drift`, and a table section "Quotes outside 0.5x-2x of the actual time").

## Design decisions

- Fix 2 vs fix 4: fix 4 (no fix rounds up front, ever) is the later and stricter rule, so it governs layout steps too.
- The grant uses the framework's existing `operations` escape hatch rather than a new group or a list of kept groups, so REQ-343's derived narrowing stays one rule.
- Pace is bounded so one freak step can't swing the figure more than 4x down or 2x up.
