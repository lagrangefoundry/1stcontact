The website for XGD (Extreme Generative Development), the dev methodology and toolchain behind the platform.

Evidence: site store at storage/sites/xgd with a draft and an import. The REQ-108 PROOF work lives here -- storage/sites/xgd/draft/pages/home.json -- covering L1 pointer accent, invisible grid bands, random edge flicker and amoeba region, and coarser 32px spacing.

Built ahead of a complete web builder, by design. DOC-16 section 4 sets the order as framework, then flagship sites, then the builder; the sites are the R and D vehicle that generates the design intelligence rather than a consumer of a finished tool. The relationship to the builder is therefore soft and is deliberately NOT filed as a depends_on edge -- a hard dependency would falsely park this site off the ready frontier.

Design bar per DOC-16: Tier-1 expensive and template-free. Lessons captured to the Design Lessons Log (DOC-17).
