## Story
**As a** client working with the consultant on my site, **I want** the assistant to be able to look at a picture of my draft, the page I am editing, a published revision, a captured reference, any web page, or a picture in the store (one it generated, one I uploaded, a drawing it made) — and actually see it, including a state of my draft that a visitor only reaches by clicking or typing — **so that** its judgements about how the site and its pictures look rest on what is there rather than on guesswork or a description.

## Description
Before REQ-157 the assistant's whole tool surface was site-editing operations; nothing it could call returned an image. This story gives it a **second, read-only declared surface** ("Looking and judging", group `SeeSite`) composed beside the site-editing surface rather than merged into it, and the part of it that lets the assistant *see*:

- **One picture vocabulary.** Every operation names a picture the same way: a `kind` of `reference` (captured bundle), `draft`, `edit`, `revision` (published, frozen), `url` or `image` (a stored picture, REQ-218), plus the fields that kind needs, a viewport (`mobile` / `tablet` / `desktop`, default `desktop`) for the five kinds that are a page, for `draft` only `after` (REQ-216), and for `image` only `image` and `original` (REQ-218). The offered tool schema shows the model that exact shape.
- **`screenshot`** returns a short text line naming what was shot and at what size, and the picture itself as an image content block the model receives inside the same tool loop — on both hosts, with the call still validated, gated and audited by the Toolbox (REQ-206).
- **Driving the draft before the shot (REQ-216).** A `draft` picture may carry `after`: an ordered list of short phrases in exactly two forms — `click "Sign in"` and `fill "Email" with "someone@example.com"` — carried out on the live draft page before the shutter opens, so a modal, a carousel's later slide, a disclosure or a form's success state can be looked at. Controls are named as a person names them and matched against the page's own accessible names; no selector can be named. Every step that cannot be done — no such control, several, not visible yet, a click that changed nothing, a field that did not take the value, a page that never settled — is an `INTERACTION_FAILED` refusal naming what failed, and **no picture is returned**. The other kinds refuse `after`, each for its own stated reason, before a browser is leased.
- **A picture says which channel it is of (REQ-216).** An `edit` picture carries a caption, on the same text block that names the picture, saying the channel ships no behaviour and that a `draft` picture shows what a visitor gets; `compare` reports the same caveat when either side came from the edit channel.
- **A stored picture is a sixth kind (REQ-218).** An `image` picture names a picture in either store by any name it goes under: a site asset's filename, its `/assets/…` handle, or the bare stem a drawing was written under; a Library item's record id, its Library title, or its upload filename. One rule decides across both stores (exact spelling first, then case- and surrounding-whitespace-insensitive), and fonts are not pictures. A name meaning several pictures is refused listing each one's exact name; a name meaning none is refused pointing at the handle returned when the picture was made, its Library name, and `list_assets`. There is no Library listing operation. A stored PNG comes back as stored; anything else is put in front of the browser inside a bare document with its bytes inlined (the browser fetches nothing) and comes back as a PNG of itself, so the reduction, `compare` and the reported size all work for a photograph. A format the browser will not decode is refused naming its stored type rather than returned blank. A drawing (SVG) carries a caption that it was photographed outside the site's own page in the browser's default face and that `measure_drawing` reads its geometry. `original: true` is accepted and the label says "as originally stored"; until an edit recipe exists it is the same picture. `viewport` does not apply. A stored picture cannot be driven.
- **Bounded pictures.** Images are reduced (by averaging, not sampling) to a 1024px longest edge and refused over a byte ceiling; the operator's live activity stream carries the text but not the image bytes.
- **A published revision is pictured as it was** — rendered from the frozen snapshot with that revision's own asset bytes.
- **Bound to one site**, read-only, granted to the consultant.
- **Mounted on the builder's Worker for the request's business (REQ-206).** References are stored in, and finished captures adopted from, that business's own private bucket; pictures of our own channels come from the `/preview/*` renderer at the deployment's own origin. The Worker's stored pictures are the site's assets and the business's Library (REQ-218). No HTTP route is added — the surface is reachable only inside an admitted, business-scoped turn.
- **Browser spend is budgeted (REQ-206).** On the Worker a session may make forty live-page browser acquisitions; reading something already captured or rendered spends nothing; the forty-first is refused as one readable refusal while every other tool keeps working.
- **Absent, not broken**, where the deployment has no browser: the session opens and edits with no looking operations. A Worker with no private bucket opens no chat at all, because the transcript store needs it (REQ-160). The looking surface's own private-bucket check is defence in depth (see Reconciliation Decisions). The local `1c` host answers `image` for the site's own assets only, because it holds no Library (REQ-218); a deployment wired with no image store at all refuses every `image` picture as `ENVIRONMENT`.

**In scope:** surface composition and grant, Worker mounting per business, picture vocabulary and resolution of all six kinds, driving a `draft` picture with `after` and its refusals (REQ-216), the edit-channel caption on pictures and comparisons (REQ-216), the stored-image kind: naming across both stores, ambiguity and not-found refusals, PNG pass-through and browser rasterisation, the drawing caption, `original`, the per-host image store, and the `.gif`/`.avif` content types (REQ-218), `screenshot`, image bounding and content-block recovery through the Toolbox, the Worker's browser budget, SSE stripping, read-only guarantee, both hosts.
**Out of scope (sibling stories in this bundle):** `compare` / `check_fidelity` verdicts (plan item 5, story-47fb84e6) — this story covers only that a driven `draft` is read in the same driven state by `check_fidelity`'s value gates, that `compare` carries the edit caveat, and that `check_fidelity`'s value gates refuse an `image` picture; that `compare` accepts an `image` on either side is story-47fb84e6's; `capture_site`, `list_references`, `describe_reference` operation semantics and the egress guard (plan item 6) — this story covers only where their captures are stored and adopted on the Worker. Also out of scope: driving the edit channel or carrying page state across a channel switch (REQ-215); a third interaction verb or any selector-based naming (REQ-216 "Deliberately not in scope"); a Library listing operation (`list_images`, retracted in REQ-218); edit recipes and the renderer that makes `original` differ (REQ-219). Named by REQ-157 itself: redacting the capped base64 from the durable session transcript (upstream follow-up), attaching ingestion descriptions to assets, prompting a backdrop-or-subject question.

The refusal code is deliberately not in the shared declaration, which the unmetered `1c` CLI also uses.
- **Driving the draft (REQ-216, surface_version ≥ 3).**
  - `picture.ts` `drivableSteps` runs at the top of `resolvePicture`, before any kind is resolved, so both refusal by kind and refusal of an unreadable phrase (`PageStepSyntaxError` re-raised as `PictureSourceError`) happen before a browser is leased or the budget spent.
  - `capture/interact.ts` holds the closed grammar (`parsePageStep`: `click X`, `fill X with Y`, one matching pair of surrounding quotes stripped), the page-scope step script (a string, its only instance data the step as JSON), and `drivePage`, which runs steps one at a time through `driver.query` and stops at the first failure with `PageInteractionError` (`code = 'INTERACTION_FAILED'`).
  - `capture/screenshot.ts` navigates at the requested viewport when there are steps (`driver.navigate(url, viewport)`), drives, then opens the shutter; an undriven shot keeps its load-wide-then-resize behaviour.
  - Matching: clickables are `a[href]`, `button`, `summary`, `[role=button|link]` and button-type inputs, named by `aria-label`, visible text, a button input's value, `title` and a contained image's `alt`; fillables are `input`/`textarea`/`select` excluding hidden/submit/button/reset/image/checkbox/radio/file inputs, named by `<label>`, `aria-label`, `aria-labelledby`, `placeholder` — and also by the `name` attribute and element `id` (see code issue below). Names are whitespace-collapsed and case-folded; an exact match wins, otherwise a substring match. Visible controls are considered first; only when none matches are hidden ones checked, to say "not visible".
  - Visibility is an ancestor walk over `hidden`, `aria-hidden="true"`, and computed `display: none` / `visibility: hidden`.
  - "Changed nothing" compares a fingerprint (hash of the document markup, scroll offset, focused element) taken after focusing the control and before clicking, against the fingerprint once settled. Settling is two consecutive unchanged 50ms samples within a 2000ms budget (`SETTLE_BUDGET_MS`).
  - A menu is matched by option text, then value; a fill dispatches `input` and `change` and then verifies the field reads the value.
  - The driven picture's label names the steps (`…, after \`click "Sign in"\` then …`).
- **Edit caption (REQ-216).** `EDIT_CHANNEL_NOTE` is set as `note` on every `edit` picture `resolvePicture` returns. `fidelity-core.ts` appends it to the screenshot's leading text block after the size; `compare` returns `notes` carrying each side's note when either has one.
- **Stored image (REQ-218).**
  - Naming: `tools/generate/src/cli/image-library.ts` `resolveStoredImage(name, images)` is the one rule. Each picture declares its canonical `name`, optional `title` and `aliases`; the asked name is trimmed, an exact match over all spellings wins outright (one → match, several → ambiguous), otherwise a lowercase/trim comparison runs. `mergeImageLibraries({site, library})` flattens listings and dispatches `read` by `where`.
  - Site half: `edit.ts` `siteImageLibrary(slug, store)` lists `store.listAssets` filtered to `assetKind === 'image'` (fonts and other files excluded), canonical name = filename, aliases = `/assets/<name>` handle and the extension-less stem, media type from the shared type table. `original` is ignored: a site asset has nowhere to carry a recipe.
  - Library half (Worker only): `apps/control-app/src/material.ts` `materialImageLibrary(store)` lists material rows of kind `image` (captures excluded), canonical name = record uid, `title` = Library title, alias = upload filename when it differs from the title; reads `materialFile`. `original` is the REQ-219 seam and today returns the stored bytes.
  - Hosts: the Worker (`router.ts` / `shot.ts`) wires both halves; the CLI (`tools/generate/src/cli/ai/host.ts`) wires `mergeImageLibraries({ site: siteImageLibrary(...) })` only. With no `images` dep at all, `picture.ts` `storedPicture` throws `PictureEnvironmentError(NO_IMAGE_STORE)` → `ENVIRONMENT`.
  - Resolution: `picture.ts` `case 'image'` → `storedPicture`: `needed(source.image, 'image', 'image')`; refusals in `storedImage` (`'<name>' names N pictures: '<a>', '<b>'. Ask for one of those names…` / `no stored picture called '<name>'. Name it by the handle you were given when it was made, by the name it appears under in the Library, or by anything list_assets shows.`, or `This deployment holds none yet.` when the store is empty). Label `<site|library> image <name>[ (<title>)][, as originally stored]`. Bytes sniffed (not trusted from the stored type): PNG passes through with its own dimensions as `viewport`/size and no browser lease; anything else → `capture/screenshot.ts` `rasterizeImage`, which navigates a `data:` wrapper document carrying the bytes as a `data:` image (laid out within a 4096px `MAX_RASTER_EDGE` box on a white background), awaits `img.decode()`, refuses with `ImageNotRenderableError` (`… is stored as <type> and the browser would not decode it …`) when it does not decode, and screenshots at the laid-out size. SVG gets `note: DRAWING_RASTER_NOTE`, appended to the screenshot's text block like the edit caption. The PNG result then goes through the ordinary reduction in `fidelity-core.ts`.

### Code issue flagged — a field can be named by its `name` attribute or `id` (REQ-216)
- REQ-216 "What was built": *"matched against what the page actually calls itself — a field's label, `aria-label`, a control's visible words, a placeholder. **There is no way to name a selector, an id or an attribute**"*; and Constraints: *"The AI should ask to activate 'the Sign in control', not a CSS selector or an internal attribute."*
- `interact.ts` `fillNames` also adds the field's `name` attribute and element `id` to its candidate names, so `fill "email_input" with …` reaches a field by an internal attribute. This contradicts the intent. The AC (Controls are matched by accessible name) states the intent: `name`/`id` are not names a field can be reached by. For `fix_uat_coverage`: drop `name` and `id` from `fillNames`.

### Code issue flagged — the CLI host does not say it holds no Library (REQ-218)
- REQ-218 "Corrections to 'The design, settled'": *"The local builder answers for the site's own assets **and says it holds no Library**."* "The design, settled": *"the local `1c` has the site's assets and no Library … **Each says which**, the same way a deployment with no browser says it cannot take pictures."* EPIC-1's review (2026-09-11) accepted exactly that shape: *"Answering for site assets and saying plainly that it holds no Library is the honest shape."*
- In the code the CLI host simply has a site-only library. A Library record id or title asked of the CLI matches nothing and gets the generic not-found refusal, which tells the model to name it "by the name it appears under in the Library" — on a host that has no Library. The AC (CLI and no-store hosts) states the intent: on the CLI host a name that matches no site asset is refused saying this host holds no Library pictures, only the site's own assets. For `fix_uat_coverage`: make the CLI's not-found refusal for `image` say the host holds no Library (e.g. a host-declared namespace list consulted by the not-found message).

- **A Worker with no private bucket opens no chat at all; the looking surface's BLOBS check is defence in depth** (decided at reconciliation, 2026-11-08, reconciliation review item 2a). **This diverges from REQ-206**, which says a deployment missing the private bucket still opens the session and edits with no eyes.
  - In the running code, `router.ts` `chatHost` opens the transcript store through `ticketStoreFor` before composing anything, and `ticketStoreBase` throws `BlobsNotConfiguredError` without `BLOBS` (`tickets.ts`). So `/api/ai/session` and `/api/ai/prompt` answer 500 naming the missing binding. No session opens, no browser is leased, and the model is never asked.
  - No `BLOBS` therefore means no chat at all, per REQ-160, which made the ticket store the transcript's home. `sessionFidelity`'s own `BLOBS` check (it returns `null`) is never what decides on a real deployment. It is defence in depth: it guarantees no capture could be kept outside the private bucket if the transcript store ever stopped needing `BLOBS`.
  - Production code is frozen in this reconciliation, so AC-2024 is narrowed to match rather than the code changed. The no-`BLOBS` UAT now asserts the real refusal with no router seam replaced. A separate, explicitly labelled defence-in-depth case keeps the `tickets` seam injection to prove that `sessionFidelity` withholds the surface on its own.
  - Follow-up for intent: either REQ-206's "still opens the session" sentence is revised to match, or the chat gets a transcript home that does not need `BLOBS`. The second is a production change outside this reconciliation.
- **Outside addresses go over the network** (decided at reconciliation, 2026-11-08, reconciliation review item 2c). AC-2372 said that a capture or screenshot of an outside address "gets no in-process answering: a page that names our host is not answered out of our store". In the code (`shot.ts`), a `url` screenshot uses the same driver as our channels, with the origin resolver. So an outside host goes to the network, but a `url` screenshot of our own host would be answered in process. Only `capture_site` (the guarded driver, with no resolver) sends even our-host requests to the network. AC-2372 now states the two separately, as the code behaves.
- **Budget scope and form** (decided at reconciliation, 2026-11-07): REQ-206 states the ceiling (forty, a constant), the unit (acquisitions, counted at the lease) and the burst-not-lifetime scope. Intent is silent on two points the code settles, and both are formalized in AC-2373:
  - a launch that fails still spends one, so a failing loop cannot spend forever;
  - the refusal's `BUDGET:` prefix and its list of what still works, which is the text the model acts on.
- **Captures marked third-party; adoption idempotent** (decided at reconciliation, 2026-11-07): REQ-206 states the third-party marking. Re-adoption returning the existing reference (`created: false`) is code behaviour on which intent is silent. It is formalized in AC-2372 because a repeated capture must not duplicate the client's material.
- **REQ-216 points the intent is silent on** (decided at reconciliation, 2026-11-08). Intent states the two forms, ordering, stop-at-first-failure, accessible-name matching, the six failure kinds, draft-only and the caption. The code settles these further points, and each is formalized in the REQ-216 ACs because it is what the model reads or relies on:
  - Matching is whitespace- and case-insensitive; an exact name wins over a partial one, and a partial name is accepted when it is the only match. Without this the model would have to reproduce a label's exact casing.
  - Click names also include a control's `title` and a contained image's `alt`. These are accessible names (what assistive technology reads), not markup, so they are inside the intent's "what the page calls itself".
  - The "what is there" list in a no-match refusal is capped at eight distinct names, so a refusal stays readable on a large page.
  - The settle budget is 2000ms, sampled every 50ms, and the refusal names the 2000ms. Intent says "says how long it was given" without a number.
  - A step during which the page navigated away or stopped answering is also an `INTERACTION_FAILED` refusal, saying these steps drive a page's own state and that a different page is named as a different picture. It is the same "no plausible wrong picture" rule applied to a seventh way a step can fail.
  - A driven picture's label names the steps it was taken after, so the transcript records which state was photographed.
  - `compare` reports any side's caption (the edit caveat here), as a `notes` list; a draft side contributes nothing.
- **REQ-218 settled by the ticket's own body and transcript** (2026-11-08). The operator's words on REQ-218 (transcript, 2026-09-11): *"This should understand both namespaces - the AI should have access to all the images in the store, however they are referenced."* The ticket body's later sections, which the free-coding turn reconciled against EPIC-1's answers and which EPIC-1 then reviewed and accepted (2026-09-11), settle the rest: no `list_images` (retracted); raster normalised now through the browser with bytes carried inline rather than waiting for REQ-219; the CLI keeps the kind for site assets. The matrix follows those final positions, not the superseded "The design, settled" paragraphs.
- **REQ-218 points the intent is silent on** (decided at reconciliation, 2026-11-08). Formalized because each is what the model reads or relies on:
  - Exact spelling wins before the case/whitespace-insensitive pass, so `Logo.png` and `logo.png` stay two answerable pictures rather than one ambiguous name.
  - A Library item also answers to its upload filename (when it differs from its title) — that is what a client says about their own upload, and it is inside "however they are referenced".
  - A stored PNG is passed through untouched and does not lease a browser (intent: "A picture that is already a screenshot is left alone"); the format is sniffed from the leading bytes rather than trusted from the stored type.
