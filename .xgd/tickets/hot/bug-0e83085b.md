## Symptom

1. **Order confirmation is broken in the Mailbox.** Opening it shows, instead of the email: "the email text must keep {{terms_of_sale}} (the terms of sale this order was placed under, at the address that version keeps for good)".
2. **Confirmations are probably being sent without the legally required paragraphs.** The operator's first live order may have had its confirmation sent from the old text; not yet confirmed.

## Root cause

- On 2026-11-07, REQ-666 (commit 5055a8c44d) added two `kept` placeholders to the `order_confirmation` trigger (`message-triggers.ts`):
  - `{{terms_of_sale}}`: the Terms of sale version the order was placed under, the lasting copy.
  - `{{cancellation}}`: the UK/EU right-to-cancel summary.
- A business's saved copy of the email made before that lacks both.
- **The Mailbox** runs `messageRefusal` on the saved copy (`message-routes.ts`) and refuses to show it.
- **Sending** (`message-send.ts`, around line 105) only checks a one-off override, never the saved copy. So the confirmation goes out from the old text, without the Terms link or the cancellation summary, which the law asks for in a distance-sale confirmation.

The same gap applies to any placeholder made `required` or `kept` in future, on any email.

## Fix

1. **A saved copy is brought up to the trigger's current requirements.** When a saved email lacks a `required` or `kept` placeholder, the platform adds the missing paragraph, using the seed's own wording for it, in the seed's position. This happens when the copy is loaded, for the Mailbox and for every send. The owner's other wording is kept.
2. **Sending never uses a copy that would be refused.** After step 1, a send checks the saved copy with `messageRefusal`. If it is still refused, the send uses the seed and records why, rather than sending an email that is missing required content.
3. **The Mailbox shows Order confirmation normally** for a business whose copy predates REQ-666, with the Terms of sale link and cancellation paragraphs present.

**Also: "Work is ready" (`job_done`) copy.** The operator read "The work on {{business}} that you were waiting for" as a buyer or commission email. Say what the work is:
- subject: "Your {{business}} site is ready to look at";
- body: "The changes you asked for on your {{business}} site are finished."

Everything else stays the same.

## Test plan

UATs `test_UAT_FC_<this ticket>_*`:
- A business store holding an `order_confirmation` copy without `{{terms_of_sale}}` / `{{cancellation}}`:
  - the Mailbox route returns the email, not the refusal, with both paragraphs;
  - a confirmation sent for a paid order contains the Terms of sale link and, for a UK or EU address, the cancellation summary;
  - the owner's other wording is unchanged.
- A saved copy that is still refused after the top-up is never sent: the seed is sent instead.
- The `job_done` seed's subject and body read as above.
