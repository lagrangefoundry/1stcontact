## Symptom

After the Shop import, the preview panel shows `{"error":"D1_ERROR: too many SQL variables at offset 281: SQLITE_ERROR"}` instead of the site. The draft and edit previews both return 500 (dev, 2026-11-03 22:44 UTC). That business has more than 100 live items.

## Root cause

`hydrate()` in `apps/control-app/src/shop.ts` loads prices and categories with one `WHERE item_id IN (?, ?, …)` per call, binding every item id. D1 accepts at most 100 bound parameters per statement. `liveItems()` (BUG-333: the published site follows the Shop) passes every live item of the business, so any shop with more than 100 live items fails to render. Offset 281 is exactly where the 101st `?` falls in the prices query.

A single item and a paged list of 100 or fewer stay under the limit, which is why the Shop tab itself works and only the site render fails.

## Fix

- Load prices and categories for any number of items: split the ids into batches of at most 100 and merge the results, keeping today's order (prices by currency then market; categories by position).
- The preview and the published site render every live item, however many there are.

## Test plan

UAT `test_UAT_FC_<ID>_*` in the Shop suite: seed a business with 150 live items, each with a price and a category, through the real import or the inventory API. Then:
- The draft preview returns 200 and shows all 150 items, each with its price and categories.
- The Shop API's item list still returns the same prices and categories as before.


## Also reported as BUG-337 (duplicate)

The consultant filed the same failure from the builder side: its screenshot checks of the draft and edit views show only `origin resolver failed: D1_ERROR: too many SQL variables at offset 281` at every width, on the pages carrying the `catalog` component (Home, Preloved). The catalog loads its products through `liveItems` → `hydrate`, so the builder can't check its own work on such a site until this is fixed. BUG-337 put the cause on the site's ~1,600 library items. That's wrong: what matters is the number of live shop items. The fix must also make the builder's draft/edit screenshots render again; the UAT's draft-preview check covers that path.


## What landed

- `hydrate()` (`apps/control-app/src/shop.ts`) now runs its prices and categories lookups through `inBatches()`: the ids are split into batches of at most 100 (`MAX_BOUND_IDS`, D1's per-statement bound-parameter limit) and the rows concatenated. Each id falls in exactly one batch, so every item keeps today's order — prices by currency then market, categories by position. One code path for any item count; no separate small-shop path.
- Every caller of `hydrate()` benefits: `liveItems()` (site render, draft/edit preview, the builder's screenshots — BUG-337), the Shop item list, and single-item reads.
