## If you have an account with us

If you sign in to this website, we keep an account record with your email address, when you signed in, and what you have chosen to receive or access. Signing in works with a link sent to your email address; there is no password. We use this information to give you access to your account, because we need it to provide the service you signed up for. If you close your account, we delete your account information, except what the law requires us to keep.
