## If you buy from us

If you place an order, we collect your name, email address, billing and delivery addresses and the details of your order, so we can take payment, deliver your order and deal with returns. Payments are taken by Stripe; your card details go straight to Stripe and we never see them. Our legal basis is that we need this information to perform our contract with you, and to meet our legal obligations such as keeping tax records. We keep order records for as long as the law requires, usually up to 7 years.

This website is built on 1st Contact, a service of Lagrange Foundry LLC (USA), which keeps its own record of every payment made through it (what you paid for, when, how much, and your name, email address and billing address) for 7 years, so that any dispute can be dealt with. Where we are not Lagrange Foundry ourselves, Lagrange Foundry is responsible for that record and its own privacy notice applies to it.
