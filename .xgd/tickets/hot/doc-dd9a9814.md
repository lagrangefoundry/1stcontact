## When you buy from us

When you place an order, we collect your name, email address, delivery address and the details of your order, so we can take payment, deliver your order and deal with returns. Payments are taken by Stripe; your card details go straight to Stripe and we never see them. Our legal basis is that we need this information to perform our contract with you, and to meet our legal obligations such as keeping tax records. We keep order records for as long as the law requires, usually up to 7 years.
