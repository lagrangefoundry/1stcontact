## Finding 1: which of the two routes was taken, and why

The report named two honest resolutions and said the call belongs to the
operator. **The ac-edit route was taken**, after confirming the uat-edit route is
not reachable without a production change:

- `cmdDeploy` → `cmdRender` → `renderSite`, which calls `emptyDir(outDir)` and
  then **throws** on any page slug containing `/` or `\`
  (`tools/generate/src/render/render.ts:260-265`). Pages emit flat as
  `<slug>.html`; assets land under `out/assets/`. No site definition can put a
  top-level `out/draft/` directory in front of the gate.
- `assertNoReservedSegment` only inspects `out/`-prefixed entries
  (`tools/generate/src/deploy/content.ts:88-91`), so the `source/` half offers no
  route in either.
- `collectSnapshotFiles(outDir, sourceDir)` has no injection seam. Pinning the
  wiring would require adding a test-only file-list override to `DeployOptions`
  — a backdoor in production code, which the project's own "no legacy/parallel
  paths" stance and this prompt's "default to non-code resolution" both argue
  against. An instrumented R2 client cannot help either: with no colliding input,
  the gate never fires, so there is no ordering to observe.

**Operator, note this consequence explicitly:** the production wiring at
`tools/generate/src/deploy/deploy.ts:136` remains unpinned by mutation —
commenting it out will still leave the suite green. That is now *stated* in
AC-914 rather than falsely implied by an assertion that could not fail. In
exchange, the test gained a canary: the day rendered output gains nesting, the
new nested-slug assertion fails, which is the signal to promote AC-914 back to a
deploy-level proof. If you would rather have the seam now, that is a deliberate
production change and yours to authorise.
