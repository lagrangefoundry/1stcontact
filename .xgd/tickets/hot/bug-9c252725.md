## Symptom

Dina's Diner (`site_b314db907afa1d53d93f3c6af2b3b902`), 2026-10-24 UTC:

- 01:11:57: the consultant offered three headlines (`offer_headlines`) and linked
  the client to the story board: "Which of them sounds most like you? [Pick a
  headline on the story board]".
- 01:12:49: the worker started the first-draft build (`Delegate`, about 15 min).
- **01:12:58: the client wrote their own headline on the board**
  (`board.headline`: `client_did: write`, "The perfect stop between the Bay and
  Tahoe."). **Nothing replied.** The client was left hanging, the second time
  this has happened (the operator: "the same impasse just occurred again").
- 01:14:42: the client typed "Done", and the talker answered at once, confirming
  the headline and moving on (catering).

## Cause

A client's action on the Input board reaches the consultant in only two ways: a
chat message, or the end of a REQ-424 focus round (`followFocus` sends the
finished cue). This headline was offered with a plain `show_client` link, not
`start_focus`, so no round was open and choosing it cued nothing. REQ-431 removed
the banners' Done button, so there is now even less to prompt a reply. The
answer to "pick one" sits silently in the plan until the client speaks.

## Relationship to BUG-262

**Builds on BUG-262** ("Builder chat: client finishes a section and hears nothing
back", draft), which supplies the mechanism:

- the finished cue goes to the talker when the worker is busy, instead of
  waiting (`queueTalkerCue`);
- a cue turn must speak before slow work;
- no empty bubbles;
- `finishedUntold`'s at-most-once guard.

BUG-262 counts only three things as a finish: a REQ-424 focus round, the
comparison board (BUG-245), and the last open question. This ticket adds the
settling actions that happen **outside** any round, such as Dina's headline,
offered by link. It reuses BUG-262's routing and guard, and adds no second
mechanism. Implement after BUG-262.

## Fix

**When the client does something the consultant asked for and is waiting on, the
consultant answers promptly, even when no focus round is open.**

- **Settling actions count as a finish** under BUG-262's routing, whether or not a
  focus round is open:
  - choosing or writing the headline;
  - hearting or editing the position card (REQ-425);
  - hearting or editing the visual direction card (REQ-432);
  - reacting to the last unreacted note;
  - choosing or skipping a look.

  The ticket session lists which of these BUG-262 already covers, and closes the
  rest. A single reaction in the middle of a pile does not cue anything; it is the
  action that settles what was asked that does.
- **The reply** follows BUG-262: the talker acknowledges in a line or two when the
  worker is busy ("Got it, 'The perfect stop between the Bay and Tahoe' is your
  headline. It goes in the draft that's building now."). Otherwise a cued turn
  acknowledges it and moves on. The finish notice names what was settled.
- **No chatter:** settling actions within a few seconds of each other (hearting
  the card, then choosing the headline) get one reply. An action the consultant
  was not waiting on cues nothing. A settling action is told at most once, under
  BUG-262's guard.
- The cue is host guidance and never appears in the conversation the client
  reopens.

## As built

**What BUG-262 already covered, and what this adds.** BUG-262 cues on a focus round finished (`focus_finished_at`), the last open question answered (`asks_finished_at`) and, through BUG-245, the comparison board finished (the last site sent or skipped: "the last site", and "choosing or skipping a look" on the comparison board). Not covered, and closed here: the headline, the positioning card, the visual direction card and the last unreacted note when no focus round is open.

**The stamp: what the consultant was waiting on.** `waitingOn(fields)` (`plan-core.ts`) lists the items the consultant is waiting on the client for: `headline` (options on offer, none chosen), `positioning` (card written, not hearted or edited), `direction` (proposed, not agreed), `notes` (a consultant note with no reaction, until the story is settled). `clientBoardAction` and `clientDirectionAction` take the list before and after the client's action; whatever was waited on before and is not now is stamped in the new plan field `settled` (`{item: at}`, added to `PLAN_KEYS`). A reaction that leaves other notes waiting, or an action on something nobody was waiting on (re-choosing a chosen headline, editing a confirmed card), stamps nothing. The panel view carries `settled_at`, the newest stamp.

**One mechanism, BUG-262's.** `finishedSince` (`session-delta.ts`) counts `settled` as a finish, so `finishedUntold` / `tellFinished` route and guard it exactly as BUG-262 does: an idle session takes a `finished` cue turn (speak-first gate applies); a busy one gets the cue interposed to the talker; told at most once by either route. The plan notice gains `settledLine`, naming each item as it now stands ("Your client has settled what you asked them for: the positioning card; the headline, now "…". Without waiting to be asked: acknowledge it in a line or two, naming what they chose…, then carry the work on from it."). When a focus task finished on the same action, only the task's line is said. The cue is host guidance and never appears in the reopened conversation (BUG-262).

**No chatter.** The builder's `followFocus` (`app.js`) now treats `settled_at` as a finish alongside `focus_finished_at` and `asks_finished_at`, and sends the cue only once the client has paused for `FINISH_SETTLE_MS` (1.5 s, `config.js`): finishes a moment apart (card hearted, then headline chosen) are one cue and one notice. A pending cue is dropped on a site switch.
