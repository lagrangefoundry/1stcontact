### Hypothesis — where it is

- `tools/generate/src/l1/inline-runs.ts:91` `signature()` — four axes, and link
  decoration is not one of them. This is the decision that lets the rest happen.
- `tools/generate/src/l1/fold.ts:2429`
  `const box = (useFlowBox ? c.element!.inlineBox : undefined) ?? c.element!.box!`
  — with `useFlowBox` false, `box` is the glyph rect for a bare inline run, and
  nothing on the path converts it to a line box or reconciles its `height` with
  the `lineHeightPx` the renderer will use.

### Proposed change

1. **Widen `signature()`** to the axes that make a run visibly different from its
   neighbours — at minimum `textDecoration` and link-ness (`href` /
   `a11yRole === 'link'`), and consider `letterSpacingPx`, `textTransform` and
   `fontFamily`. On this bundle that alone closes the whole issue: the flow
   becomes one `text` node laid out inside its `inlineBox` (`y: 168, h: 36`), the
   spaces come back through `flowText()`, and `image-0`'s lead recomputes as
   `59 − 204 = −145` against a rendered node bottom of `168 + 36 = 204` — which
   lands the image at 59, exactly the reference.
2. **Independently, make the un-rejoined path safe**, because a single-run inline
   flow has no rejoin to fall back on (that is REQ-265's case, appended to this
   round — see below). Where a run carries an `inlineBox`, the fold should use it
   for both the run's own lead **and** the bottom the next sibling's lead is
   measured from; a run's occupied height in the fold's own model must be the
   height the renderer will give it (`lineHeightPx`), not `box.height`.

## Issue 4 — the fold drops a shadow colour's alpha, so a 60%-black drop shadow reproduces as solid black
