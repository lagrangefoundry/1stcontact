## Symptom

On the plan panel the consultant posted:

> **Logo — do you have one? Upload it, or say "none" and I'll set a wordmark**
> Decides whether the name is drawn from scratch or from your existing mark.
> [Upload files] [Uploaded] [Skip]

The client cannot say "none". An `input: upload` card has no text box and no
choices. It has only Upload files, Uploaded (BUG-216) and Skip, and Skip means
"I don't know, or it doesn't apply". "I don't have a logo" is a real answer that
decides the work (draw a wordmark). Skip loses that answer, and in the
conversation it reads to the consultant as "no answer".

## Root cause

Two halves:

1. **The card.** `plan-panel.js` `editor()` gives a pure upload ask no answer
   except a file or "Uploaded to the Library". An upload question has no way to
   say "there is no such file".
2. **The prompt.** `set_ask` in `tools/generate/src/cli/ai/plan-surface.json`
   says only that "`upload` asks for a document only". Nothing tells the
   consultant which buttons the card shows, so it wrote an instruction ("say
   none") that the card cannot accept.

## Fix

The card works for every upload question. Logo is only the case that showed the
problem.

- **Every upload card (`input: upload`, or `accepts_upload`) has a third answer
  button for "there is no such file"** next to Uploaded and before Skip.
  Suggested label "Don't have one", with the tooltip "There isn't one to send".
  The button sends an ordinary answer (suggested wording "None — I don't have
  one"), so the item goes to *What you've told us* as answered, not as skipped.
  The consultant reads it the way it reads any answer. The words live in
  `apps/control-app/src/builder/config.js` next to `PLAN_UPLOADED`.
- **`set_ask`'s description says which answers an upload card offers:** pick
  files, "already uploaded", "don't have one", Skip. It also says the prompt must
  never ask the client to type or say anything, because the card has no text box.
  When a typed alternative matters, use `accepts_upload` on a text ask instead.
  Keep it to one or two sentences, because the priming budget is close to full.
