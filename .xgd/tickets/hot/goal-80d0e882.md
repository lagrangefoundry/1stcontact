The customer-facing portal for a 1st Contact subscriber. MVP item 5 in DOC-4: the account surface where an owner signs in, sees their site, and manages their relationship with the service.

Auth is magic-link per DOC-5 -- no passwords. The portal is part of the control app, not the framework: DOC-5 is explicit that the control app is a separate application and does not pass through the site renderer.

Scope boundary: the portal is the shell and the account/session surface. Subscription billing state renders here but is owned by Payments -- see the subscriptions subgoal.

State rationale: aspiration. Named in the DOC-4 MVP scope, not yet planned, no code.
