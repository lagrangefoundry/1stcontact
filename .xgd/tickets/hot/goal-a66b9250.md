The intelligent half of asset upload: uploaded assets become knowledge the AI can consult and reason about, not just bytes in a bucket.

Without this, an upload is inert -- the operator drops in a photograph and the AI has no idea it exists, what it depicts, or when it would be the right choice. With it, the asset carries meaning: what it is, what it suits, how it has been used.

This is where asset upload meets the knowledge-management work. It cannot function before the KM core system exists, so it is a genuine ordering dependency rather than mere composition.

Open: whether asset descriptions live in the project KB (per-site) or the system KB (shared), and whether descriptions are AI-generated at upload time or supplied by the operator.
