## Symptom (operator, 2026-11-05)

On a site's checkout page, the consultant placed the action buttons on the left and in the wrong order: Continue to payment before Cancel. BUG-368 item 1 leaves this open, because "a template that already places `cancel` keeps its own placement". This ticket supersedes that sentence.

## The standard (operator: "super important")

- A **primary** action sits at the **right** of its action row. A primary action moves the visitor forward or commits: Buy, Continue to payment, Send, Save, OK.
- **Secondary** actions (Cancel, Back, Not now, Quit) sit to its **left**. The row itself is right-aligned.
- On phone widths, where the actions stack full width, the primary is on **top**.
- Right-to-left languages mirror the rule: primary on the left.

## Fix

1. **Framework defaults follow it.** Every module's default template that has a primary and a secondary action draws them secondary-then-primary in a right-aligned row, with the primary on top when stacked. Today that means checkout (`cancel`, `continue`) and any form that has a cancel or back control.
2. **The renderer enforces it for module action rows.** When a template places a module's primary and secondary controls in the same row, the row is drawn in the standard order and alignment, whatever order or alignment the template writes. This replaces BUG-368's "keeps its own placement". A site can't get it backwards.
3. **The layout check reports it on authored pages.** On a page the consultant authors, two problems are reported to the consultant as layout problems to fix, naming the rule: a row where a `button.primary` sits to the left of a `button.secondary`, and an action row in a form or checkout that is aligned left.
4. **The consultant knows the rule.** Its design guidance states it in one line: primary on the right, secondary to its left, primary on top when stacked.

## Test plan

- UAT (Chromium): a checkout template that writes `continue` before `cancel` and left-aligns their row still renders Cancel left of Continue, with the row's right edge on the column's at 1024px, and Continue above Cancel at 375px. A form with submit and cancel renders the same way. Under `dir="rtl"`, the order mirrors.
- UAT: the layout check reports an authored row with `button.primary` left of `button.secondary`, naming the rule. A correctly ordered row reports nothing.
- UAT: the consultant's design guidance contains the rule.
- Regression scope: BUG-367, BUG-368, REQ-643 checkout suites; contact-form suites.

## Implementation (free-coded)

- **Item 1, defaults.** Only checkout has a primary and a secondary action today. Its default already draws Cancel then Continue, right-aligned, with Continue on top when stacked (BUG-367). The contact form and the account forms have a submit and no cancel or back control, so there is no form default to change and no "form with submit and cancel" to render. The Test plan's form-render case doesn't apply. A form's left-aligned action row is caught by the layout check (item 3) instead.
- **Item 2, renderer** (`packages/framework/src/l2/checkout.ts`, `checkoutActions`). A template that places its own `cancel` no longer keeps its placement. Every Cancel it places, along with any box that holds only that Cancel, is lifted out. The row is then drawn the standard way: right-aligned, Cancel on Continue's left, both stacked on phones with Continue on top. The template's first Cancel keeps its own look: axes, padding and role. Only its place changes. The second, phone-only copy carries no ids, so DOM ids stay unique. A row that is already standard (the default page's) is left alone, so drawing it twice changes nothing. Under `dir="rtl"` the flex row mirrors: Continue on the left, Cancel on its right.
- **Item 3, layout check** (`tools/generate/src/cli/ai/layout-check.ts`, new check `action_order`, kinds `action_order` and `action_row_left`). The renderer now marks a node whose `component` is `button.primary` or `button.secondary` with `data-l1-component="…"` on its element. The value comes from a closed enum. Two faults are reported:
  - `action_order`: a primary button whose centre is left of a secondary button's in the same row (right of it on an RTL page).
  - `action_row_left`: two or more buttons side by side in a `form` or checkout root, against its start edge and short of its end edge.
  Both findings name the rule: "the primary action sits on the right of its row, secondary actions (Cancel, Back) to its left, and on a phone, stacked, the primary on top". Neither kind can be kept (they aren't taste).
- **Item 4, guidance.** The builder's design guidance (`builder_priming` and `builder_priming_with_corpus` in priming.json) gains one line next to its button rules: "Action buttons: primary on the right, secondary to its left, primary on top when stacked; a right-to-left page mirrors it." Pages are authored by the builder, so that is where the line goes. The consultant's own prompt is already over its REQ-466/473 budget on clean xgd-working, so it is unchanged.
- BUG-368's UAT `…keeps_its_placement` is replaced by `…a_template_placing_its_own_cancel_gets_the_standard_row`, per this ticket's supersession.
