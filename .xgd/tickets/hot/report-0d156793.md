One thing worth flagging rather than burying: **the impossible-instant refusal is narrower than AC-1443's wording suggests.** I probed the boundary, and `2026-02-30T12:00:00Z` is *not* refused — V8's ISO parser rolls it over and it formats as `2 March 2026 at 12:00`, which is literally "formatted into whatever the runtime makes of it". Out-of-range month/day/hour/minute values (`2026-13-01T…`, `2026-01-32T…`, `2026-10-28T25:00:00Z`, `…T12:61:00Z`) *are* refused, and those are what the test asserts. I did not assert the Feb-30 case, because pinning the current rollover would cement it; the AC's own Verification clause asks only for "a shaped-but-impossible date-time", which the month-13 and hour-25 cases satisfy. Closing the gap would need a calendar round-trip check in `formatDateTime` — a runtime change, out of scope here.
