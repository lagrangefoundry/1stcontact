## Symptom (operator, 2026-11-07, Settings → Payments)

With the business's Stripe account connected (it was taking test payments at the time), Settings → Payments shows:

> Stripe is waiting for:
> - person 1UXnqE4GZw3Z6WOcfLgJJuXi verification document live capture

It isn't clear what is needed, who has to do it, where to do it, by when, or whether payments are affected.

## Cause

- `classifyAccount` (`apps/control-app/src/stripe-connect.ts:415`) shows Stripe's raw requirement keys (`requirements.past_due` + `currently_due`). The only processing is `requirementWords`, which turns `.` and `_` into spaces. So a key such as `person_<id>.verification.document…` reaches the owner with a Stripe object id inside it.
- Every due item turns the whole connection into "needs attention", even while `charges_enabled` is true and the payments still work. Stripe's `current_deadline`, and the difference between `currently_due` (deadline ahead) and `past_due` (overdue), are ignored.
- The section says "Open your Stripe dashboard" without saying what to look for there (`builder/payments.js:146-157`).

## Fix

1. **Each requirement is described in plain words. Stripe's keys and object ids are never shown.**
   - A small table maps Stripe's requirement families to a sentence about what's needed and who gives it. It's kept as data so it can grow:
     - `person_*.verification.document` / `…additional_document` → "Photo ID for one of the people on the account (the owner or representative). Stripe asks for it to be taken live with a phone camera."
     - `individual.verification.*` → the same, for the account holder
     - `external_account` → a bank account for payouts
     - `business_profile.url` / `.mcc` / `.product_description` → website and business details
     - `tos_acceptance.*` → accepting Stripe's terms
     - `company.*` / `representative.*` → company details or the representative's details
   - `person_<id>` is shown as "one of the people on the account", or by the person's name if Stripe returns it for the connected account. The id is never shown.
   - An unknown key falls back to a generic "Stripe needs more information about the account", logged with the key for the mapping table. Several keys for the same person or family become one line.
2. **The owner is told whether payments are affected.** Three cases replace the single "needs attention":
   - **Payments work, but Stripe needs something by a date.** `charges_enabled` is true, the items are `currently_due`, and `current_deadline` is shown as a date. The shop keeps selling.
   - **Overdue.** `past_due` items: "Stripe may pause payments until this is done."
   - **Paused.** `disabled_reason` set, or `charges_enabled` false, said in plain words: "Payments are paused until …". `canTakePayments` follows `charges_enabled` as today.
3. **It says where to go.** "Stripe will show what's needed when you sign in to your Stripe dashboard. Look for the banner at the top." The link goes to that mode's dashboard. Where Stripe offers a direct link to finish this for the account type, use it.
4. **The consultant and the Settings assistant read the same plain sentences** (where they report payment readiness), never the raw keys.

## Test plan

UATs named `test_UAT_FC_BUG-379_*`, with the Stripe account response stubbed at the HTTP boundary.
- An account with `currently_due: ["person_X.verification.document"]`, a deadline and `charges_enabled: true` shows the photo-ID sentence and the deadline date, and still says payments work. Neither "person_" nor the id appears anywhere in the section.
- `past_due` shows the overdue wording. `disabled_reason` / `charges_enabled: false` shows "paused", and Buy is off.
- An unknown key shows the generic sentence, and its raw key is logged, not shown.
- Two keys for the same person collapse to one line.


## What landed

- `apps/control-app/src/stripe-connect.ts`: `REQUIREMENT_WORDS` (data table, first match wins) + `requirementSentences()` turn Stripe's `past_due`/`currently_due` keys into plain sentences, deduplicated, so several keys for one person or family become one line. An unmatched key gets `REQUIREMENT_FALLBACK` ("Stripe needs more information about the account.") and is logged as `{"event":"stripe_requirement_unmapped","key":…}`. No key or object id is present anywhere in the `/api/settings/payments` response.
- **Supersedes REQ-641's single `needs_attention` state.** `PaymentsState` now has `action_due` / `overdue` / `paused` in its place:
  - `action_due` — `charges_enabled` true, only `currently_due`: "Payments work. Stripe needs a few things from you by 21 November 2026." (`current_deadline`, en-GB date, UTC), or "… soon." if Stripe gives no deadline. `deadline` (ISO) is also returned.
  - `overdue` — `past_due` items, charges still enabled: "Payments still work, but Stripe's deadline has passed. Stripe may pause payments until this is done."
  - `paused` — `disabled_reason` set or `charges_enabled` not true: "Payments are paused …", with the reason in words (until Stripe has what it needs from you / while Stripe checks the information you sent / while Stripe reviews the account / because Stripe has closed the account).
- **`canTakePayments` follows `charges_enabled`**: true in `ready`, `action_due` and `overdue` (the shop keeps selling), false in `paused` and every unlinked/unreachable state. Previously it was true only in `ready`. Buy, checkout and invoices all read this one answer.
- Each attention state carries `due`, `where` ("Stripe will show what's needed when you sign in to your Stripe dashboard. Look for the banner at the top.") and the mode's `dashboardUrl`. `builder/payments.js` shows the message, "What Stripe needs:" and the list, the `where` note, the "Open your Stripe dashboard" link, and Disconnect.

## Design decisions

- **The person's name isn't shown.** It would take a second Stripe call (the Persons API) on every Settings load. "One of the people on the account (the owner or representative)" is used, and the id is never shown.
- **No direct account link.** These are Standard accounts connected by OAuth. For them the owner's own Stripe dashboard is where Stripe collects requirements, so the link goes there.
- **The consultant and the Settings assistant** see payment readiness only as `canTakePayments` / `missing: ['payments']` (describe_site). They never received the requirement keys, and still don't. Nothing changed there.
