## Symptom

Production, 2026-11-02, a test site build. REQ-620's light comparison capture is much faster, but three of four comparison tiles failed. The four `reference` capture records show three different failures:

1. **A bot-check page captured instead of the site.** `www.3dstudiosinc.com` was captured with the title "One moment, please..." — a bot-protection holding page served to automated visitors. The screenshot and extract are of that page. `description_status: ok`, so nothing flagged it.
2. **A capture stuck at "loading".** `jdscustomironworks.com`: `capture_status: loading`, `capture_started_at` 21:44:45Z, never finished or failed. Background captures are held open by `ctx.waitUntil` (REQ-620), which Cloudflare ends about 30 s after the response; two Browser Rendering page loads plus the extract can take longer, so the capture is cut off and the record never leaves `loading`.
3. **A fold failure on a video poster.** `www.thecrucible.org`: `fold_warning: "foldToL1: produced an invalid L1 document — …/poster: a URL must be http/https or site-relative: video poster '' is not an allowed URL"`.

The capture pipeline itself waits for `load`, network idle and a settle scroll before screenshots (`cf-driver.ts`), so "screenshot before the page loaded" is not the confirmed cause; if a tile shows a blank or partial screenshot on a site with no recorded error, add that case here after reproducing.

## Fix (as implemented)

1. **A bot-check page is waited out, or the capture says the site blocks us.** After a page loads, both capture drivers (Browser Rendering `cf-driver.ts` and the laptop Playwright driver) ask an in-page probe (`CHALLENGE_PROBE`, `page-scripts.ts`) whether the page is a bot check: a holding-page title ("One moment, please", "Just a moment", "Checking your browser", "Attention required", "Verifying you are human", …); known challenge markup (Cloudflare's challenge form / `cf_chl_opt`, SiteGround `sgcaptcha`, Sucuri cloud proxy, Wordfence) on a page with little text, since some of those scripts also ride on real pages; or a near-empty body with a meta refresh. A challenge is looked at again every 2 s **in the same page** (a passing check sets a cookie and moves the page on by itself) for up to 20 s; once it clears, the real page's network is waited for and the capture carries on as the real page. One still there after 20 s throws `SiteBlocksAutomationError` ("This site blocks automated visits: <url> answered with a bot-check page ("<title>")…"). The pipeline does not retry it and nothing of the holding page is written or adopted. A probe that cannot run is not treated as a challenge.
   - The comp's `reference` ticket gets a new `capture_status: blocked` (beside `loading` / `failed` / `ready`), with the reason in `capture_error`. The tile says **"This site blocks automated visits"** (reason on hover), offers **Remove** and no Retry, since a retry would hit the same check. Adding the same address again re-captures a blocked or failed one.
2. **A capture never stays "loading".**
   - **Durable runner.** Deployed, each comparison capture is now its own Cloudflare Workflows job (`CompCaptureJob` in `worker.ts`, binding `COMP_CAPTURE`, declared in every wrangler environment). It is started by the client's add/retry route and by the consultant's `add_comp`. The REQ-624 durable runner exists, but it runs consultant rounds, and a comp capture is not a round, so it gets a job of its own. The job is one step (`runCompCaptureJob`, `comps.ts`), retried once if its isolate dies. Past that, the ticket is marked failed so the tile offers Retry. Without the binding (suites, `1c` CLI) the capture runs in the isolate as before, held open by `waitUntil`.
   - **Timeout, both ways.** The job holds the capture to the existing 3-minute `COMP_CAPTURE_STALE_MS` and marks the ticket failed ("the capture took too long and was abandoned") itself. Independently, the board already reads a `loading` record older than that as failed with Retry. That covers a job that never ran at all.
   - A runner that cannot even start the capture marks the ticket failed at once.
3. **An empty video poster doesn't break the fold.** The fold drops an empty or unsafe `poster` instead of carrying `''`. The L1 `video` node's `poster` is now optional (schema, validator, renderer, asset resolver). A clip with no poster is valid and renders `poster=""`. An empty or unsafe poster that *is* present is still refused, so the URL allowlist is unchanged.
   - **Explicit supersession:** this replaces REQ-461's "a missing poster is rejected" UAT (`test_UAT_FC_REQ-461_envelope_security_a_missing_poster_is_rejected`). It is renamed `…_an_empty_or_unsafe_poster_is_rejected` and now asserts that a missing poster is accepted while empty or unsafe ones are refused.
4. **The consultant is told the truth about each tile.** `CompRecord` carries `capture: 'blocked'` and `capture_error`. `read_plan` returns both for every comp that is not ready, plus a `capture_note`: for a blocked site, "This site blocks automated visits, so nothing of it was captured … Do not describe how it looks"; for a failed one, "could not be captured … its card offers Retry". A blocked or failed capture never carries an `about`, so a holding page is never described to the consultant as the site.

## Design decisions

- The challenge wait lives in the drivers, not the pipeline. Re-navigating would open a fresh browser context, which loses the cookie a passed check sets.
- `blocked` is its own state rather than a `failed` with a special message. The tile and the consultant need to tell "retry may help" apart from "this site refuses automated visits".
- Durable capture uses its own Workflows job, not a step of the consultant job. The client's add route also starts captures, outside any consultant job.
- Known gap: a laptop `1c capture` of a live site can't be exercised in the sandbox (Chromium bypasses the proxy). Live bot-check behaviour on the three production sites still needs to be confirmed after deploy.
