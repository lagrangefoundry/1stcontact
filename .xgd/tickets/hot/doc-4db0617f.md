## If you are in the United States

If you are in the United States, depending on the state you live in, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. To make a request, contact us at {{contact_email}}. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.
