## Notes for the Editor

**Sequence.** Findings 1, 2 and 3 are the only ones that block this level. Fix 1
independently — it touches AC-1117 alone. Fix 2 and 3 together, and take
warning 8 in the same pass: the "both channels" claim is being *moved* from the
origin (where it is false) to the command line (where it is true and unowned),
and doing half of that leaves the surface's most load-bearing visibility claim
asserted nowhere. Warning 4 also lands on AC-1026, so fix 3 and warning 4
together rather than editing the same paragraph twice.

**Nothing here is a code issue.** In all three violations the implementation and
the shipped UATs agree with the story body; the AC prose is what lagged. AC-1117
is the sharper of the two lags — it is not merely stale, it directly contradicts
a sibling AC of the same story (AC-1269), so exactly one of them describes the
shipped surface and the tree currently claims both.

**The exclusivity backlog is the real pattern.** Warnings 4, 5 and 6 are on their
fourth unrepaired cycle, and warning 4 has grown from two ACs to six across
those cycles because every new AC batch copies the origin-parity clause from its
neighbours — the colour batch added AC-1273 on 2026-08-20 exactly as the image
batch added its predecessors. Warning 9 is the same shape one phase earlier: two
ACs written in the same batch, both asserting the every-painted-panel rule with
the same seeded panel. None of them blocks this level, but the cost of repair is
rising, not falling. If one warning is taken this cycle, take warning 4.

**Where the freshest risk is.** Twenty-seven of the 43 ACs have not been edited
since 2026-08-16 — before the colour phase and before today's story-body repair.
Findings 1 and 7 are both instances of that lag, found by reading each
pre-colour AC against the post-colour body rather than by any signal in the
ticket graph. A future cycle should re-run that specific comparison whenever the
story body is rewritten, since `updated_at` on the ACs is the only marker that
the two layers were last reconciled at different times.
