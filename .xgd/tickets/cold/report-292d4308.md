## Notes for the Editor

**Do not "fix" this by moving STORY-76 back.** The absorption is correct and intentional:
CAP-63's Scope section already names gradients as an owned axis, and STORY-76 plus its five
ACs are intact, `active`, and `uat_coverage: pass` under CAP-63. Every finding above is
about finishing the retirement of the *husk*, not about restoring coverage.

**Cross-cutting pattern — systemic, not local to CAP-64.** Seven capabilities carry
`merged_into` while remaining `status: active`: CAP-64 (`capability-36dd68c5`),
CAP-65 (`capability-18a822ac`), CAP-66 (`capability-ac7ca849`), CAP-67 (`capability-6e088083`),
CAP-69 (`capability-938f26ec`), CAP-72 (`capability-ce902be4`), CAP-73 (`capability-8108afab`).
**Only CAP-68 (`capability-bd0b722e`) was actually retired** — `status: superseded` +
`superseded_by_uid: capability-ae9d65d6`, on 2026-07-23. The rebalance sets `merged_into` but
never completes the lifecycle transition. Fixing CAP-64 alone leaves six identical husks that
will each burn a `capability_validation` cycle. Recommend a single sweep across all seven.

**Sequencing.** Resolve finding 4 (rebuild the index) **first**. Findings 1–3 are ticket edits
whose verification depends on index queries returning true state; repairing the body while the
index still reports a phantom story invites a second round of confusion.

**On `merged_into` vs `superseded_by_uid`.** `merged_into` appears to be an ad-hoc field used
by the rebalance; `superseded_by_uid` (`FIELD_SUPERSEDED_BY_UID`,
`xgd_source/core/ticketing/constants.py:114`) is the one the supersession-chain walker in
`xgd_source/core/ticketing/supersession.py` actually traverses. Capabilities carrying only
`merged_into` are invisible to that walker — worth confirming whether the rebalance should be
writing `superseded_by_uid` instead of, or in addition to, `merged_into`.
