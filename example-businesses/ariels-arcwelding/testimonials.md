# Ariel's Arc Welding: mock customer reviews

These are made up. They're written to read like real Google and Yelp
reviews: uneven, specific, a couple not glowing. They're consistent with
`README.md`, `services.md`, `ariel-story.md` and `classes.md`.

---

**★★★★★ Pete V., sailboat owner, Alameda**
Cracked aluminum stern pulpit on a Saturday. Ariel came to the dock Monday
morning and did it right there, timing her welds between ferry wakes. It
looks better than factory and was half what the yard quoted.

---

**★★★★★ Rosa M., Temescal**
My grandmother's cast-iron garden bench was in three pieces. Two shops said
cast iron can't be fixed. Ariel fixed it. You can barely see the joins. I
might have cried in her shop. She didn't make it weird.

---

**★★★★★ Dev K., hot rod builder, San Leandro**
Gus made me a bushing for my '62 Ford from a sketch on a paper towel. It
fit perfectly. That man is a national treasure.

---

**★★★★☆ "Kitchen guy" (Yelp)**
Our stainless prep table fits around a column like it grew there, and they
built it in a day. Four stars only because the shop is a bit hard to find
in Jingletown. Look for the giant devil, honestly.

---

**★★★★★ Laura and Jamal B., parents, Fruitvale**
Our 11-year-old did the Spark session and now wants to be a welder. She
came home with a robot made of bolts and a wind chime from forks. Ariel
gave her the confidence of a 40-year-old tradesperson. We've signed up for
Arc.

---

**★★★★★ Hangar manager, Oakland Airport (North Field)**
Ariel's shop built us engine stands and a custom cowling fixture, on time
and to drawing. She was very clear about what she would and wouldn't weld
for aircraft, which is exactly what you want to hear.

---

**★★★★★ Sam T., general contractor, West Oakland**
Seismic retrofit with moment frames and beam plates. Fabricated to the
engineer's drawings, passed special inspection first time. She'll also tell
you when your engineer's detail is going to be a nightmare to build, and
she's usually right.

---

**★★★☆☆ Google review**
Great work on our gate, but it took three weeks longer than I hoped. She
was honest about it: a boat emergency jumped the queue. I understand,
but I was annoyed at the time.

---

**★★★★★ Fiona L., artist, Berkeley**
I had a sketch and no idea how to make it 12 feet tall. Ariel did the
engineering and the build and let me be in the shop for the whole thing.
She's a real collaborator, not just a fabricator.

---

**★★★★★ Neighbour on Kennedy St**
Ten years next door. Loud? Sometimes. But the yard full of sculptures is
the best thing on the street, the kids love her, and when Old Scratch
lights up on Halloween the whole neighbourhood comes out.
