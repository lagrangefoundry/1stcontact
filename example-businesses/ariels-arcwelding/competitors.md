no single direct rival, because nobody else combines repair, fabrication,
machining and art. Each of these covers one side of what she does.

## What it means for Ariel

- **Nobody shows their work as art.** HRT and Bayfab sell certifications
  and capacity, with no person behind the welds. Ariel's yard, Old Scratch
  and the spark-plug bees are a visual identity no competitor has.
- **Marine is under-served.** HRT does "maritime" at the port scale. Nobody
  in these results pitches dock-side repair for private boats, which is
  Ariel's bread and butter.
- **Publish the rates.** HRT shows only a $600 minimum and Bayfab shows
  nothing. Ariel's $125 shop rate, $75 walk-in minimum and same-day photo
  ballpark are disarming, the same lesson as Charlie's callout fee.
- **Photo-to-quote is the expected flow.** HRT asks for a photo link with
  every quote. Ariel says the same ("90% of a quote"). The site needs an
  easy phone photo upload.
- **Kids' classes vs The Crucible:** The Crucible is the big name for
  classes, and its youth welding is for teenagers. Ariel's small groups,
  younger "Spark" track (10–12) and free places for Oakland kids are a
  different, neighbourhood-scale offer.

Searches also turned up Mobile Welding Solutions (Oakland, Yelp-listed, does
boat exhaust work) and A&S Structural Force (Hayward, mobile structural).
Neither site was read.
