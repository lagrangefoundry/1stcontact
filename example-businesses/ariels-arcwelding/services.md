# Ariel's Arc Welding: what we do, in Ariel's words

Paste-ready fragments for each line of work. Rates are 2026 Bay Area
rates.

---

## How we charge

> Shop rate is $125 an hour for welding and fabrication, and $115 for Gus on
> the machines. Materials at cost plus 15%. Mobile work, where I come to you,
> is $165 an hour with a two-hour minimum, and the drive counts from the
> shop. Small jobs that come in the door are a $75 minimum, and I'll usually
> do them while you wait.

> Send me a photo and the size, and I'll give you a ballpark the same day.
> A firm quote takes a visit or a drawing. Quotes are free.

> Weekend and after-hours mobile calls for boats are $225 an hour. I don't
> love doing them, but a boat taking on water doesn't wait until Monday.

## Marine

**Aluminum hull and deck repair**
> Cracks in aluminum hulls, transoms, pulpits, T-tops. In the yard or at
> the dock. A typical crack repair is $400 to $1,200 at the dock.

**Stainless rails, davits, ladders, swim platforms**
> Repair a bent rail and it's usually $250 to $600. New custom rails are
> priced by the foot, about $180 to $260 a foot installed for 1-inch 316
> stainless.

**Fuel and water tanks**
> Aluminum tanks built to fit your boat, not to fit a catalogue. A small
> tank is $1,500 to $3,500. I'll repair a leaking one if it's worth it,
> but it has to be cleaned and certified gas-free first. No exceptions.

**Boat trailers**
> Rusted crossmembers, broken tongues, bunk brackets. $200 to $900 mostly.

## Auto

**Exhaust and custom headers**
> Old trucks, hot rods, things the muffler shop won't touch. A custom
> exhaust is $600 to $1,800. Headers are more.

**Frame and bracket repair**
> Cracked frames, broken mounts, bumpers. A bumper or rock slider build is
> $900 to $2,500.

**Roll cages**
> For race cars, built to the rulebook of whatever you're racing. $2,800 to
> $5,500. I won't build a cage for a street car "for looks". It'll hurt you
> in a crash.

**Hard-to-find parts**
> If it's discontinued, Gus can usually make it. Bring the old one, or what's
> left of it.

## Buildings and contractors

**Gates, railings, stairs**
> Driveway and garden gates from $1,800. Stair railings $120 to $250 a
> foot depending on style. Ornamental work costs more and takes longer,
> and it's the stuff I enjoy.

**Structural steel**
> Beam plates, brackets, moment frames for seismic retrofits, posts.
> Built to the engineer's drawings and installed with the permit and the
> special inspector. I'm C-60 licensed and AWS D1.1 certified for structural
> steel.

**Security**
> Window bars that don't look like a jail, roll-up door repair and gate
> operators. $350 to $2,000.

## Aircraft and aviation (the stuff around the plane)

> Engine stands, tow bars, jigs, assembly fixtures, ground support
> equipment and hangar racks. Priced per job, usually $800 to $6,000. I
> don't weld airworthy parts. That needs an FAA repair station. Gus will
> machine non-structural parts to print for an A&P to sign off.

## Machining (Gus)

> Bushings, shafts, flanges, pulleys, adapters, threads nobody uses anymore.
> Manual lathe and mill, tolerances to a ten-thousandth. A simple bushing is
> $85 to $150. Short production runs are fine. Gus doesn't do CNC and he's
> proud of it.

## Commercial and restaurant

> Stainless prep tables, shelving, hood brackets, equipment repair, and
> cracked mixer and slicer parts. Prep tables run $900 to $2,500 built to
> fit.

## Forge work

> Hand-forged hooks, hardware, fire tools, hinges and latches for old
> buildings. The forge is the oldest tool in the shop and the most fun.
> Custom hardware is from $45 a piece.

## Art fabrication and commissions

> I build for other artists who have the idea but not the shop. Priced
> per project, with a drawing and a budget agreed before I strike an arc.

> My own sculpture commissions start around $3,500 for a garden piece and go
> way up for big ones. Fire art I'll only build with the right permits and
> a fire-safety plan, and I'll tell you what those cost before you fall in
> love with the idea.

## What we don't do

> Airworthy aircraft repairs, pressure vessels and boilers, gas line work in
> buildings (that's a plumber), and anything where the customer wants me to
> skip a permit. I'll tell you who to call.
