## The two tracks

**Spark (ages 10–12): cold metalwork**
> No welding yet. They cut, bend, drill, rivet and bolt. They make robots out
> of nuts and bolts, wind chimes from old cutlery and a bent-wire animal. By
> the end they can use a hacksaw, a drill press and a vice safely, and they're
> desperate to weld.

**Arc (ages 12–16): welding and sculpture**
> MIG welding with me standing right beside them, plasma cutting and a bit of
> forge work. Every kid builds one scrap sculpture over the four weeks. It
> gets shown in the yard at the end, and their family can come.

## Safety (Ariel)

> Leather, long sleeves, closed shoes, a proper helmet. We provide it all,
> sized for kids. Nobody touches a machine until they've passed the safety
> talk. My dad's rule is the first thing they hear: if you wouldn't stand
> under it, don't weld it. Nobody's been hurt worse than a small burn in
> nine years, and I intend to keep it that way.
