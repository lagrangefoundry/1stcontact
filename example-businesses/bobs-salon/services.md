# Bob's Salon: the menu, in Bob's words

Paste-ready fragments for each service. Prices are Bob's 2026 South Boston
prices. **Hair is priced by length and time, never by gender.**

Lengths Bob uses: **short** (above the chin), **medium** (chin to
shoulders), **long** (shoulders to mid-back) and **extra long** (past
mid-back).

---

## Haircuts

**Cuts by length**
> Short $55, medium $70, long $85, extra long $105. Every cut includes a
> wash, a consultation before the scissors come out, and a blow-dry or
> air-dry styling, whichever you'll actually do at home.

**Long-hair dusting / trim**
> If you're growing it out, come in every ten or twelve weeks for a dusting,
> which takes off the split ends and nothing else. $45 for any length, half
> an hour. That's how you get long hair. People think not cutting it is how
> you grow it, and it isn't.

**Big chop**
> If you're taking off more than eight inches, the cut is the same price but
> we book you an hour. It's a big moment. You'll want time.

**Wigs for Kids / donation cuts**
> Free for kids, every time. Adults pay half. We braid it, measure it and mail
> it for you.

**Kids under 12**
> $35, any length.

**Fringe / bang trim between cuts**
> Free for clients. Just pop in.

## Treatments for long hair

> Bond-repair treatment $45, deep conditioning $30, scalp treatment $40.
> If your ends feel like straw, do one before you cut anything off.

## Styling

> Blow-dry $45, or $60 if it's long and thick. Braids from $40. Updos for a
> wedding or prom $95, and a trial run is $60. We'll come to you for
> weddings, for a travel fee.

## Nails (Twee)

> Manicure $30. Gel manicure $45. Pedicure $50. Gel pedicure $65.
> Gel removal $15, or free if Twee is redoing them.

> Men's manicure and pedicure, same price. The guys love the pedicure, and
> they just don't tell their friends.

> Nail art from $5 a nail. Twee will do whatever you bring her a picture of.

> Walk-ins welcome for nails Tuesday to Friday. Saturdays, book.

## Facials (Aoife)

> Signature facial, 60 minutes, $110. Express facial, 30 minutes, $65,
> which you can do while your colour's processing. Men's facial $95, aimed
> at beard and shaving skin. Acne-calming facial $120. Add a brow tidy
> for $20.

> Aoife will ask you about your skin before she touches it. If you need a
> dermatologist and not a facial, she'll tell you.

## Packages

> **The Grow-Out Plan**: four dustings a year plus a treatment each time,
> $200 paid up front. You save $60 and you never lose length you didn't
> want to lose.

> **The Whole Thing**: cut, colour, manicure and express facial in one
> visit. About four hours. We take 10% off and bring you lunch from the
> bakery downstairs.

> **Bridal party**: priced together. Call and talk to me.

## Policies (Bob)

> Running late, text us. More than 15 minutes and we might need to shorten
> your appointment. Cancel 24 hours ahead, please, or it's half the
> service. If you hate your cut, come back within a week and we'll fix it,
> free.
