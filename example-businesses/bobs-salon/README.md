## Chat fragments: things Bob can say

Bob is chatty and warm, and he talks like a hairdresser: about his clients,
their hair and his week. He has opinions about hair and none about websites.
Paste these as they are or mix them.

### What we're about (USP)

> Long hair. That's the whole thing. Most places, a guy walks in with hair
> past his shoulders and they either want to give him a man bun or take it
> all off. Women with long hair ask for a trim and lose four inches. Here,
> you get exactly what you asked for. We'll show you an inch with our
> fingers before we cut it.

> We price by length and time, not by whether you're a man or a woman. A
> guy with hair to his waist pays what a woman with hair to her waist
> pays. A woman with a pixie pays the short-hair price. That just makes
> sense to me, and people are surprised by it every single week.

> I'm not a barber and I'm not a fancy Newbury Street place. You can come in
> wearing your work boots.

### Who our customers are

> Half my long-hair guys are musicians, chefs, guys in the trades, a couple
> of firefighters. There's a dad from Andrew Square who's grown his hair for
> ten years. Women who've been growing it out since college and are terrified
> of anyone touching it. And lots of kids growing their hair for Wigs for Kids.
> We do those cuts free.

> I get people from all over now, Quincy, Dorchester, JP. One woman drives up
> from Providence every eight weeks. She says nobody down there listens.

> The neighbourhood's changed a lot. I've got old Southie ladies who've been
> coming since I opened, and young people in the new condos on the waterfront.
> They all sit next to each other and they get along fine.

### What we're best at

> Growing it out. That's the hard part, and nobody talks about it. The awkward
> stage, keeping the ends healthy, making it look like a choice at every
> length. I'll plan it with you a year ahead.

> Long-hair cuts that move. A blunt line looks great dry and terrible in a
> ponytail. I cut for how you actually wear it.

> Healthy long hair. Treatments, the right brush, telling you to stop
> straightening it every day. I'd rather sell you nothing and have you come
> back happy.

### What I did last week

> Tuesday a guy came in, a cook at one of the Seaport places. His hair's down
> to the middle of his back and he'd been hacking at it himself because
> every barber he went to wanted to "clean it up". I took off half an inch,
> layered it so it'd sit in a hairnet, and he nearly cried.

> Thursday a girl, maybe ten, cut off twelve inches for Wigs for Kids. Her
> whole family came. Her grandmother brought cannoli. We take a photo of
> every one of those for the wall.

> Saturday was a wedding: the bride, her mom and three bridesmaids, all
> long hair, all up. Twee did all their nails Friday night. Aoife did facials
> for the bride and her mom on Wednesday. That's the dream for us, the
> whole family in one place.

### Nails and facials

> Twee's been doing nails for twenty years. She's faster than anyone I've
> ever seen and she'll tell you straight if your nails need a break from gel.
> Saturdays she's booked solid, so walk-ins are best on weekdays.

> Aoife's a licensed esthetician from Galway. She's the one who got the guys
> doing facials. They come in for a trim, they're waiting, she says "you've
> got dry skin, come here", and now half of them book a facial with every
> cut. Nobody's embarrassed.

### Marisol

> Marisol does colour like nobody else, and curls. If you've got curly long
> hair you go to her, not me. I'll be honest about that. She's been with me
> since 2015 and her clients follow her everywhere.

### Things Bob says when asked web or design questions

> Oh God, I don't know. My niece did our Instagram. Ask her? I just want it
> to look like us, not like every other salon with a grey marble wall.

> Whatever it is, put the hair first. Real clients, real hair. Not those
> stock photos of a girl laughing at a salad.

> People need to be able to book without calling. Half my young clients
> would rather die than phone.
