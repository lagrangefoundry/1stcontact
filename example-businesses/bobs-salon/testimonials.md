# Bob's Salon: mock client reviews

These are made up. They're written to read like real Google, Yelp and
Instagram reviews: uneven, specific, a couple not glowing. They're
consistent with `README.md`, `services.md` and `salon-story.md`.

---

**★★★★★ Danny O., Dorchester**
I've been growing my hair for six years and every barber wanted to "tidy it
up," which means taking it all off. Bob took off half an inch, showed me
the half inch first, and it's never looked better. Same price my wife pays
for her long hair. Finally.

---

**★★★★★ Kelly M., South Boston**
Asked for "just a trim" and actually got just a trim, for the first time in
my life. Bob explained the grow-out plan and I'm on it now. My hair is
longer than it's been since high school.

---

**★★★★★ Marcus T., Seaport**
Came in for a cut, Aoife told me my skin was "crying for help," and I booked
a men's facial on the spot. Now I do both every time. Zero judgement in that
place.

---

**★★★★★ Jen and Lily R., Andrew Square**
My daughter donated 12 inches for Wigs for Kids. They made it such a big
deal for her. Photo on the wall, the whole team clapping. They wouldn't
take a cent. We've all been going there ever since.

---

**★★★★☆ "Prov-bound" (Yelp)**
Worth the drive from Providence for Marisol's curly cuts. Only four stars
because parking on E Broadway on a Saturday is a nightmare, and the stairs
are steep. Not their fault, but plan for it.

---

**★★★★★ Ana P., Quincy**
Twee is a genius. My gel manicure lasts three weeks, and she talked me into
a break when my nails were wrecked. She didn't just sell me another set.
Fastest pedicure in Boston and it's still perfect.

---

**★★★★★ Mike S., firefighter, Southie**
My hair's down my back. Bob figured out a cut that works under my helmet
and still looks good off duty. Wouldn't go anywhere else.
