## What it means for Bob

- **Everyone prices by gender** wherever they list prices: a "men's" and a
  "women's" cut. Bob's length-based pricing is a real difference, and the
  site should say it loudly.
- **Nobody near him does nails, facials and hair under one roof.** House of
  Ivy has facials but no nails. Ochoa has neither in Southie. "The Whole
  Thing" package has no local equivalent.
- **Long hair is nobody's headline.** Where it appears at all it's
  extensions, which means selling long hair rather than looking after the
  hair you've grown. Bob's grow-out plan is the opposite pitch.
- **None of them show social proof on the site.** No ratings, no review
  counts. Bob's Wigs for Kids wall (300+ kids) and real client photos would
  stand out.
- All three let you book online. Bob says his young clients "would rather
  die than phone", so online booking is the minimum, not an extra.
