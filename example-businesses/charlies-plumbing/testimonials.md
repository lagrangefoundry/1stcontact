# Charlie's Plumbing: mock customer testimonials

These are made up. They're written to read like real reviews of the kind a
small independent plumber gets: uneven length, some specific, a couple
lukewarm. Charlie might paste them in or forward a screenshot. They're
consistent with the chat fragments in `README.md` (the $125 callout,
1-year comeback guarantee, Charlie and Dave on the vans, Emma on the phone,
kitchen and bathroom installs as Charlie's favourite work).

---

**★★★★★ Margaret H., Seabright**
I've used Charlie for over twenty years. When my tub backed up last week he
came the same afternoon, cleared the roots out and showed me a video of my
pipe on his phone. He didn't try to sell me anything. He told me what was
coming and how long I had. You don't find that anymore.
