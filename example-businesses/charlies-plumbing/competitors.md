## What it means for Charlie

- None of the three publishes a callout fee. Charlie's $125 is a real
  difference, which is exactly what Alice argued.
- They all lead with **numbers**: star ratings, review counts, years in
  business, awards. Our draft has none of these. Charlie should be asked
  what his reviews look like.
- Rosenthal tells the same family-succession story Charlie does, but
  in one line. Charlie's version (Walt's shoebox, Dave choosing to come back)
  is far more specific, and that's an advantage if the site uses it.
- Duncan is the competitor for the kitchen and bathroom work Charlie loves.
  Their site is a generic list of services with no person behind it.
